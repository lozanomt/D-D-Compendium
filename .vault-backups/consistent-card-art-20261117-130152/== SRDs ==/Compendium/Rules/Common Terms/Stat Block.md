# Stat Block

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Stat Block
>
> A stat block contains the game statistics of a monster. Each stat block includes the following information presented after the monster's name.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Size|Size]]. A monster is Tiny, Small, Medium, Large,
>
> *Huge, or Gargantuan. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Size|Size]]."*
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Creature Type|Creature Type]]. This entry notes the family of beings a monster belongs to, along with any descriptive tags. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Creature Type|Creature Type]]."
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Alignment|Alignment]]. An alignment is suggested for the monster, with the GM determining its actual alignment. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Alignment|Alignment]]."
>
> AC, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], and HP. These entries give the monster's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], which are detailed in "Playing the Game." In parentheses after the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], the monster's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Dice]] are provided, along with the contribution of its
>
> Constitution, if any, to its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. Following the
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] modifier is an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] score. Some creatures that are created by magic lack Hit Dice and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] information.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]].  Here the monster's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is provided, along with any special speeds. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Burrow Speed|Burrow Speed]]," "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Climb Speed|Climb Speed]]," "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]]," and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Swim Speed|Swim Speed]]."
>
> Ability Scores. A table provides the monster's ability scores, modifiers, and saving throw modifiers, all of which are detailed in "Playing the Game."
>
> Skills. This entry lists the monster's skill proficiencies, if any. See also  "Playing the Game" ("[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]]").
>
> **Resistances:** and Vulnerabilities. These entries list the monster's  
> **Resistances:** and Vulnerabilities, if any. See also "Resistance" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Vulnerability|Vulnerability]]."  
>
> Immunities. This section lists the monster's damage and condition Immunities, if any. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]]."
>
> Gear.  If the monster has any equipment that can be given away or retrieved, it's listed in this entry.
>
> Senses. This entry lists the monster's special senses, such as Darkvision, and its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]]. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]]."
>
> Languages.  This entry lists any languages the monster knows.
>
> CR.  [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Challenge Rating|Challenge Rating]] summarizes the threat a monster poses and is detailed in "Monsters." The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Experience Points|Experience Points]] characters receive for defeating a monster and its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus follow. Some creatures that are created by magic have no CR. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Challenge Rating|Challenge Rating]]" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Experience Points|Experience Points]]."
>
> Traits. The monster's traits, if any, are features that are active at all times or in certain situations.
>
> Actions.  The monster can take these actions in addition to those detailed in this glossary. See also "Playing the Game" ("Actions").
>
> Bonus Actions. If the monster has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] options, they are listed in this section.
>
> Reactions. If the monster can take special Reactions, those are listed in this section.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] Notation. The entry for a monster's attack starts by identifying whether the attack is a melee or a ranged attack and then provides the attack roll's bonus, its reach or range, and what happens on a hit. An attack is against one target unless its entry says otherwise.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Saving Throw|Saving Throw]] Effect Notation. If an effect forces a saving throw, the effect's entry starts by identifying the kind of saving throw required and then provides the save's DC, a description of which creatures must make the save, and what happens on a failed or a successful save.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Damage|Damage]] Notation. A stat block usually provides both a static number and a die expression for each instance of damage. For example, an attack might deal 4 (1d4 + 2) damage on a hit. The GM determines whether you use the static number or the die expression in parentheses; you don't use both.
>
