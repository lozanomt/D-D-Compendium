# Gloom Stalker

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Gloom Stalker
>

>
> **Class:** [[Ranger|Ranger]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Dread Ambusher
>
> Add Wisdom modifier to Initiative. Gain 10 feet of Speed during your first turn of combat. Dreadful Strike adds 2d6 Psychic damage to a weapon hit, at most once per turn, with Wisdom-modifier uses (minimum one) per Long Rest.
>
> #### Level 3: Gloom Stalker Spells
>
> Always prepare the source’s level-appropriate spells: Disguise Self, Rope Trick, Fear, Greater Invisibility, and Seeming.
>
> #### Level 3: Umbral Sight
>
> Gain 60-foot Darkvision or extend existing Darkvision by 60 feet. While entirely in Darkness, you are Invisible to creatures relying on Darkvision to see you there.
>
> #### Level 7: Iron Mind
>
> Gain Wisdom saving throw proficiency; if already proficient, choose Intelligence or Charisma instead.
>
> #### Level 11: Stalker’s Flurry
>
> Dreadful Strike becomes 2d8. Using it also permits either another same-weapon attack against a different nearby target or a burst of fear around the target.
>
> #### Level 15: Shadowy Dodge
>
> React to impose Disadvantage on an attack against you, then teleport up to 30 feet to a visible unoccupied space whether it hits or misses.
>
