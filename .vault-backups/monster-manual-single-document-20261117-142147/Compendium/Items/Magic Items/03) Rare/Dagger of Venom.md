## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Dagger of Venom
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p251-dagger-of-venom.png|320]]
>
> [[Weapon|Weapon]] (Dagger), Rare You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
>
> You can take a [[Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to magically coat the blade with poison. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature.
>
> That creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 2d10 Poison damage and have the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The weapon can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=216|SRD 5.2.1, p. 216]]
