## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Quarterstaff of the Acrobat
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] (Quarterstaff), Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) You have a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
>
> While holding this weapon, you can cause it to emit green [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] out to 10 feet, either as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] or after you roll [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], or you can extinguish the light as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]].
>
> While holding this weapon, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to alter its form, turning it into a 6-inch rod (for ease of storage) or a 10-foot pole, or reverting it a Quarterstaff; the weapon will elongate only as far as the surrounding space allows.
>
> In certain forms, the weapon has the following additional properties.
>
> Acrobatic Assist (Quarterstaff and 10-Foot Pole
>
> Forms Only). While holding this weapon, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] Deflection (Quarterstaff Form Only).
>
> When you are hit by an attack while holding the weapon, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] to twirl the weapon around you, gaining a +5 bonus to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]] against the triggering attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you. You can't use this property again until you finish a Short or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> Ranged [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] (Quarterstaff Form Only). This weapon has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Thrown|Thrown]] property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 120 feet. Immediately after you make a ranged attack with the weapon, it flies back to your hand.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=237|SRD 5.2.1, p. 237]]
