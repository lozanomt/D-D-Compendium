# Tinderbox

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Tinderbox [[Tinderbox|(5 SP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Tinderbox is a small container holding flint, fire steel, and tinder (usually dry cloth soaked in light oil) used to kindle a fire. Using it to light a [[Candle|Candle]], [[Lamp|Lamp]], Lantern, or [[Torch|Torch]] - or anything else with exposed fuel - takes a Bonus Action. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.
>
> **Weight:** 1 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
