# Staff of Withering

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Staff of Withering
> *Staff, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p315-stone-of-controlling-earth-elementals.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The [[Staff|staff]] has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. Wield it as a [[Magic|magic]] [[Quarterstaff|Quarterstaff]], dealing normal Quarterstaff damage on a hit. When you hit, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage and force the target to make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Strength and Constitution ability checks and saving throws for 1 hour.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Staff of Withering
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p315-stone-of-controlling-earth-elementals.png|320]]
>
> Staff, Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) This staff has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
>
> The staff can be wielded as a magic Quarterstaff.
>
> On a hit, it deals damage as a normal Quarterstaff, and you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d10 Necrotic damage to the target and force it to make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] for 1 hour on any ability check or saving throw that uses Strength or Constitution.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=248|SRD 5.2.1, p. 248]]
