# Two-Weapon Fighting

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Two-Weapon Fighting
> *Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you make an extra attack as a result of using a weapon that has the [[Compendium/Rules/Properties/Light|Light]] property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren’t already adding it to the damage.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
