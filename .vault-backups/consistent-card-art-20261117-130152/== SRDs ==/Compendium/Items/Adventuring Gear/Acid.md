# Acid

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Acid [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Acid|(25 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a vial of Acid. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d6 Acid damage.
>
> **Weight:** 1 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
