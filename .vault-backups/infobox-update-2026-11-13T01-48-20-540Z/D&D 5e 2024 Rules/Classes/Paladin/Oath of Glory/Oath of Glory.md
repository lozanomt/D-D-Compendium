# Oath of Glory

**Class:** [[Classes/Paladin/Paladin|Paladin]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Inspiring Smite

After casting Divine Smite, spend Channel Divinity to distribute 2d8 + Paladin level Temporary Hit Points among chosen creatures within 30 feet, including yourself.

### Level 3: Oath of Glory Spells

Always prepare the oath’s level-appropriate spells, including Guiding Bolt, Heroism, Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon, Haste, Protection from Energy, Compulsion, Freedom of Movement, Legend Lore, and Yolande’s Regal Presence.

### Level 3: Peerless Athlete

Use a Bonus Action and Channel Divinity for one hour of Advantage on Athletics and Acrobatics, and 10 extra feet of Long and High Jump distance, still costing movement.

### Level 7: Aura of Alacrity

Your Speed increases by 10 feet. Allies entering or starting a turn in Aura of Protection gain 10 feet of Speed until their next turn ends.

### Level 15: Glorious Defense

React when you or a visible creature within 10 feet is hit: add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to its AC against that attack. If it misses, make a weapon attack against the attacker if in range. Charisma-modifier uses, minimum one, per Long Rest.

### Level 20: Living Legend

A Bonus Action grants ten minutes of Advantage on Charisma checks, Reaction rerolls of failed saves, and once-per-turn conversion of a missed weapon attack into a hit. Refresh on a Long Rest; a level-5 slot restores the use.
