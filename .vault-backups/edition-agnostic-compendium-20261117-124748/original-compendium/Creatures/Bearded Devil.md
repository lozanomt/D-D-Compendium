# Bearded Devil

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Bearded Devil
>
> *Medium Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p035-bearded-devil.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 13  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 58 (9d8 + 18)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 16 | +3 | +5 | **Dex** | 15 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 15 | +2 | +4 |
> | **Int** | 9 | -1 | -1 | **Wis** | 11 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 14 | +2 | +4 |
>
> **Resistances:** Cold  
> **Immunities:** Fire, Poison; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness); [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** Infernal; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Telepathy|Telepathy]] 120 ft.  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The devil has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The devil makes one Beard attack and one Infernal Glaive attack.
>
> ***Beard.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage, and the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the start of the devil's next turn. Until this poison ends, the target can't regain Hit Points.
>
> ***Infernal Glaive.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature and doesn't already have an infernal wound, it is subjected to the following effect. *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12. *Failure:* The target receives an infernal wound. While wounded, the target loses 5 (1d10) Hit Points at the start of each of its turns. The wound closes after 1 minute, after a spell restores Hit Points to the target, or after the target or a creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to stanch the wound, doing so by succeeding on a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check.
>
