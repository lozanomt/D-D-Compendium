> #### Level 1: Spellcasting
> You have learned to channel the magical essence of nature to cast spells. See [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Spells|Spells]] for the rules on spellcasting.. The information below details how you use those rules with Ramger spells, which appear in the Ranger spell list later in the class’s description.
>
> ***Spell Slots.*** The Ranger Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Prepared Spells of Level 1+.*** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Ranger spells.
>
> The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Ranger levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Ranger Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Ranger spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Ranger Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you’re a level 5 Ranger, your list of prepared spells can include six Ranger spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.
>
> If another Ranger feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don’t count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Ranger spells for you.
>
> ***Changing Your Prepared Spells.*** Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one spell on your list with another Ranger spell for which you have spell slots.
>
> ***Spellcasting Ability.*** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Ranger spells.
>
> ***Spellcasting Focus.*** You can use a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Druidic Focus/Druidic Focus|Druidic Focus]] as a Spellcasting Focus for your Ranger spells.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Favored Enemy|Favored Enemy]]
> You always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]* spell prepared. You can cast it twice without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> The number of times you can cast the spell without a spell slot increases when you reach certain Ranger levels.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Weapon Mastery|Weapon Mastery]]
> Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Ranged/Longbow|Longbows]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Shortsword|Shortswords]]
>
> Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Scimitar|Scimitars]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Longsword|Longswords]].
> #### Level 2: Deft Explorer
> Thanks to your travels, you gain the following benefits.
>
> ***Expertise.*** Choose one of your skill proficiencies with which you lack Expertise. You gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Expertise|Expertise]] in that skill.
>
> ***Languages.*** You know two languages of your choice.
> #### Level 2: Fighting Style
> You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice.
>
> Alternatively, choose **Druidic Warrior**: learn two Druid cantrips of your choice. They count as Ranger spells for you and use Wisdom. Whenever you gain a Ranger level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Druid cantrip.
> #### Level 3: Ranger Subclass
> You gain a Ranger subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Ranger levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Ranger level or lower. The following subclasses are available for you to choose from:
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Beast Master/Beast Master|Beast Master]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Fey Wanderer/Fey Wanderer|Fey Wanderer]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Gloom Stalker/Gloom Stalker|Gloom Stalker]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Hunter/Hunter|Hunter]]
> #### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
> You gain the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Feats/02) General Feats/Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Ranger levels 8, 12, and 16.
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Extra Attack|Extra Attack]]
> You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
> #### Level 6: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Roving|Roving]]
> Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] increases by 10 feet while you aren’t wearing Heavy armor. You also have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Climbing|Climb]] Speed and a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed equal to your Speed.
> #### Level 9: Expertise
> Choose two of your skill proficiencies with which you lack Expertise. You gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Expertise|Expertise]] in those skills.
> #### Level 10: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Tireless|Tireless]]
> Primal forces now help fuel you on your journeys, granting you the following benefits.
>
> ***Temporary Hit Points.*** As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can give yourself a number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 1d8 plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). You can use this action a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Decrease Exhaustion.*** Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level, if any, decreases by 1.
> #### Level 13: Relentless Hunter
> Taking damage can’t break your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] on *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]*.
> #### Level 14: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Nature's Veil|Nature's Veil]]
> You invoke spirits of nature to magically hide yourself. As a Bonus Action, you can give yourself the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition until the end of your next turn.
>
> You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
> #### Level 17: Precise Hunter
> You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls against the creature currently marked by your *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]*.
> #### Level 18: Feral Senses
> Your connection to the forces of nature grants you [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] with a range of 30 feet.
> #### Level 19: Epic Boon
> You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.
> #### Level 20: Foe Slayer
> The damage die of your *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]* is a d10 rather than a d6.

> You have learned to channel the magical essence of nature to cast spells. See "Spells" for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Ranger spells, which appear in the Ranger spell list later in the class's description.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Slots. The Ranger Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> Prepared Spells of Level 1+. You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1
>
> Ranger spells. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Cure Wounds|Cure Wounds]]  and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Ensnaring Strike|Ensnaring Strike]] are recommended.
>
> The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Ranger levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Ranger Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Ranger spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Ranger Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 5 Ranger, your list of prepared spells can include six Ranger spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.
>
> If another Ranger feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Ranger spells for you.
>
> Changing Your Prepared Spells. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can replace one spell on your list with another Ranger spell for which you have spell slots.
>
> Spellcasting Ability. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Ranger spells.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]]. You can use a Druidic Focus as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]] for your Ranger spells.
>

> #### Level 2: Fighting Style
>
> You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice (see "Feats"). Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.
>
> Druidic Warrior. You learn two Druid cantrips of your choice (see the Druid class's section for a list of
>
> Druid spells). [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Cantrip/Guidance|Guidance]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Cantrip/Starry Wisp|Starry Wisp]] are recommended. The chosen cantrips count as Ranger spells for you, and Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for them. Whenever you gain a Ranger level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Druid cantrip.

> #### Level 3: Ranger Subclass
>
> You gain a Ranger subclass of your choice. The Hunter subclass is detailed after this class's description. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Ranger levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Ranger level or lower.
