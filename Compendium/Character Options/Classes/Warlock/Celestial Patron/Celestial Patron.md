## Player's Handbook

> [!info]

>

>
> **Class:** [[Warlock|Warlock]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Celestial Spells
>
> Always prepare the source table’s spells as you reach their levels, including healing spells and Light and Sacred Flame. Use the table for exact unlock levels.
>
> #### Level 3: Healing Light
>
> Gain a healing pool of d6s equal to Warlock level + 1. As a Bonus Action, heal yourself or a visible creature within 60 feet, spending up to Charisma modifier dice (minimum one). Recover all dice on a Long Rest.
>
> #### Level 6: Radiant Soul
>
> Resist Radiant damage. Once per turn when your spell deals Fire or Radiant damage, add Charisma modifier to its damage against one target.
>
> #### Level 10: Celestial Resilience
>
> After Magical Cunning or a Short/Long Rest, gain Warlock level + Charisma modifier Temporary Hit Points. Up to five visible creatures each gain half your Warlock level + Charisma modifier.
>
> #### Level 14: Searing Vengeance
>
> When you or an ally within 60 feet is about to make a Death Saving Throw, restore half its Hit Point maximum and allow it to end Prone. Nearby chosen creatures take Radiant damage and are briefly Blinded. Once per Long Rest; see the source for area and duration.
