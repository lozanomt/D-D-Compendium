# Defensive Tactics

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Defensive Tactics
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can replace the chosen option with the other one.
>
> ***Escape the Horde.*** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] against you.
>
> ***Multiattack Defense.*** When a creature hits you with an attack roll, that creature has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all other attack rolls against you this turn.
>

>

>
