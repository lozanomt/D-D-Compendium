# Knocking Out a Creature

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Knocking Out a Creature
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you would reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, you can instead reduce the creature to 1 Hit Point. The creature then has the [[Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition and starts a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]].
>
> The creature remains Unconscious until it regains any [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] or until someone uses an action to administer first aid to it, which requires a successful DC 10 Wisdom ([[Medicine|Medicine]]) check.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
