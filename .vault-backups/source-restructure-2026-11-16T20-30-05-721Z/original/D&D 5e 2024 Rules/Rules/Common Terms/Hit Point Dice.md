# Hit Point Dice

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Placeholder.png|320]]


Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a player character’s [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum. Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Short Rest]] to regain Hit Points.
