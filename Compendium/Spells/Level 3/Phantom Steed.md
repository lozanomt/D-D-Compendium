# Phantom Steed

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Phantom Steed
> *Level 3 Illusion (Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> A Large, quasi-real, horselike creature appears on the ground in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. You decide the creature's appearance, and it is equipped with a saddle, bit, and bridle. Any of the equipment created by the spell vanishes in a puff of smoke if it is carried more than 10 feet away from the steed.
>
> For the duration, you or a creature you choose can ride the steed. The steed uses the **Riding Horse** stat block, except it has a [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] of 100 feet and can travel 13 miles in an hour. When the spell ends, the steed gradually fades, giving the rider 1 minute to dismount. The spell ends early if the steed takes any damage.
>
> [[Compendium/Creatures/Riding Horse#Monster Manual|no-title clean]]
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
