# Fan Token

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Fan Token [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/00) Varies/Feather Token/Fan Token|(200 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This object looks like a feather. If you are on a boat or ship, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to toss the token up to 10 feet in the air. The token disappears, and a giant flapping fan takes its place. The fan floats and creates a strong wind. This wind can fill the sails of one ship, increasing its speed by 5 miles per hour for 8 hours. You can dismiss the fan as a Magic action.
>
