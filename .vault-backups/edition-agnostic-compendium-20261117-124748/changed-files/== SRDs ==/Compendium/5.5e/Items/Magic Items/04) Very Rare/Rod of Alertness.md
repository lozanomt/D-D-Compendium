## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Rod of Alertness
>
> Rod, Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This rod has the following properties.
>
> Alertness. While holding the rod, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] rolls.
>
> Spells. While holding the rod, you can cast the following spells from it:
>
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Detect Evil and Good|Detect Evil and Good]]
>
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Detect Magic|Detect Magic]]
>
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Detect Poison and Disease|Detect Poison and Disease]]
>
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 2/See Invisibility|See Invisibility]]
>
> Protective Aura.  As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod's head sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 60-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet. While in that [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]], you and your allies gain a +1 bonus to [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]] and saving throws and can sense the location of any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] creature that is also in the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]].
>
> The rod's head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes or when a creature takes a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to pull the rod from the ground. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=241|SRD 5.2.1, p. 241]]
