## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Cloak of Displacement
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While you wear this cloak, it magically projects an illusion that makes you appear to be standing in a place near your actual location, causing any creature to have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you. If you take damage, the property ceases to function until the start of your next turn. This property is suppressed while your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is 0.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=215|SRD 5.2.1, p. 215]]
