# Shining Smite

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Shining Smite
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Paladin)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/Tables/srd-p162-table-3.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]]  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> The target hit by the strike takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]], and it can't benefit from the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
