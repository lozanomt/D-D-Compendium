# Winter Wolf

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Winter Wolf
>
> *Large Monstrosity, Neutral Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p336-winter-wolf.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 13  
> **Initiative:** +1 (11)  
> **HP:** 75 (10d10 + 20)  
> **Speed:** 50 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 18 | +4 | +4 | **Dex** | 13 | +1 | +1 | **Con** | 14 | +2 | +2 |
> | **Int** | 7 | -2 | -2 | **Wis** | 12 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** | 8 | -1 | -1 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Perception|Perception]] +5, [[Stealth|Stealth]] +5  
> **Immunities:** Cold  
> **Senses:** [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 15  
> **Languages:** Common, Giant  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> ***Cold Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot Cone. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Cold damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>
