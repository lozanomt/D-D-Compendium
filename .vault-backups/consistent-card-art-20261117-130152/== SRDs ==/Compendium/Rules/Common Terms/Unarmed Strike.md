# Unarmed Strike

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Unarmed Strike
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Instead of using a weapon to make a melee attack, you can use a punch, kick, headbutt, or similar forceful blow. In game terms, this is an Unarmed Strike - a melee attack that involves you using your body to damage, grapple, or shove a target within 5 feet of you.
>
> Whenever you use your Unarmed Strike, choose one of the following options for its effect.
>
> ***Damage.*** You make an attack roll against the target. Your bonus to the roll equals your Strength modifier plus your Proficiency Bonus. On a hit, the target takes Bludgeoning damage equal to 1 plus your Strength modifier.
>
> ***Grapple.*** The target must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity saving throw (it chooses which), or it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition. The DC for the saving throw and any escape attempts equals 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus. This grapple is possible only if the target is no more than one size larger than you and if you have a hand free to grab it.
>
> ***Shove.*** The target must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity saving throw (it chooses which), or you either push it 5 feet away or cause it to have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition. The DC for the saving throw equals 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus. This shove is possible only if the target is no more than one size larger than you.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
