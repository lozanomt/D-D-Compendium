# Lay on Hands

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Lay on Hands
>
> #### Lay On Hands
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p111-paladin.png|320]]
>
> Your blessed touch can heal wounds. You have a pool of healing power that replenishes when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. With that pool, you can restore a total number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to five times your Paladin level. As a Bonus Action, you can touch a creature (which could be yourself) and draw power from the pool of healing to restore a number of Hit Points to that creature, up to the maximum amount remaining in the pool. You can also expend 5 Hit Points from the pool of healing power to remove the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions from the creature; those points don’t also restore Hit Points to the creature.
>
> ***Restoring Touch.*** At Paladin level 14, when you use Lay on Hands on a creature, you can also remove one or more of the following conditions from the creature: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]]. You must expend 5 Hit Points from the healing pool of Lay On Hands for each of these conditions you remove; those points don’t also restore Hit Points to the creature.
>
