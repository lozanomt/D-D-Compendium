# Disciple of Life

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Disciple of Life
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p076-life-domain.png|320]]
>
> When a spell you cast with a spell slot restores [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature, that creature regains additional Hit Points on the turn you cast the spell. The additional Hit Points equal 2 plus the spell slot’s level.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Disciple of Life
>
> When a spell you cast with a spell slot restores [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature, that creature regains additional [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] on the turn you cast the spell. The additional [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal 2 plus the spell slot's level.
