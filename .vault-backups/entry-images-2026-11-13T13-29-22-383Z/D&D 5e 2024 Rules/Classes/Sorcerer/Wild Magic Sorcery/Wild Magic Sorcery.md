# Wild Magic Sorcery

**Class:** [[Classes/Sorcerer/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Wild Magic Surge

Once per turn after casting a Sorcerer spell with a slot, you may roll a d20; on 20, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table. Spell effects produced by a surge cannot be modified by your Metamagic. Use the linked handbook table for all results.

### Level 3: Tides of Chaos

Gain Advantage on one D20 Test before rolling. Recover after casting a Sorcerer spell with a slot or finishing a Long Rest; casting that spell before resting automatically triggers a surge-table roll.

### Level 6: Bend Luck

Immediately after another visible creature rolls a D20 Test, use a Reaction and 1 Sorcery Point to add or subtract 1d4.

### Level 14: Controlled Chaos

When rolling on the surge table, roll twice and choose either result.

### Level 18: Tamed Surge

After casting a Sorcerer spell with a slot, choose a surge-table effect instead of rolling, except the final row. Still make rolls required by that effect. Once per Long Rest.
