# Manacles

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Manacles [[Manacles|(2 GP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can use Manacles to bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Grappled|Grappled]], [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity ([[Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]) check. While bound, a creature has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls, and the creature is Restrained if the Manacles are attached to a chain or hook that is fixed in place. Escaping the Manacles requires a successful DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check as an action. Bursting them requires a successful DC 25 Strength ([[Athletics|Athletics]]) check as an action.
>
> Each set of Manacles comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use [[Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] to pick the Manacles' lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check.
>
> **Weight:** 6 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
