# Burrow Speed

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Burrow Speed
>
> A creature that has a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Burrow Speed|Burrow Speed]] can use that speed to move through sand, earth, mud, or ice. The creature can't burrow through solid rock unless the creature has a trait that allows it to do so. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]]."
>
