# Superior Hunter's Prey

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Superior Hunter's Prey
>
> ![[phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> Once per turn when you deal damage to a creature marked by your *[[Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]*, you can also deal that spell’s extra damage to a different creature that you can see within 30 feet of the first creature.
>
