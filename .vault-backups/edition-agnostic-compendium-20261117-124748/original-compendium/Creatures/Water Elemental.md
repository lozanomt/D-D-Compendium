# Water Elemental

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Water Elemental
>
> *Large Elemental, Neutral*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p324-water-elemental.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 14  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 114 (12d10 + 48)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] 90 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 18 | +4 | +4 | **Dex** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 18 | +4 | +4 |
> | **Int** | 5 | -3 | -3 | **Wis** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 8 | -1 | -1 |
>
> **Resistances:** Acid, Fire  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** Primordial (Aquan)  
> **CR:** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Freeze.*** If the elemental takes Cold damage, its Speed decreases by 20 feet until the end of its next turn.
>
> ***Water Form.*** The elemental can enter an enemy's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Slam attacks.
>
> ***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> ***Whelm (Recharge 4-6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 15, each creature in the elemental's space. *Failure:* 22 (4d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 14). Until the grapple ends, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition, is suffocating unless it can breathe water, and takes 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of the elemental's turns. The elemental can grapple one Large creature or up to two Medium or smaller creatures at a time with Whelm. As an action, a creature within 5 feet of the elemental can pull a creature out of it by succeeding on a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
