# Circle of Death

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Circle of Death
> *Level 6 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (the powder of a crushed black pearl worth 500+ GP)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Negative energy ripples out in a 60-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] from a point you choose within range. Each creature in that area makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d8 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 6.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
