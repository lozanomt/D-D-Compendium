# Smite of Protection

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Smite of Protection
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p116-oath-of-devotion.png|320]]
>
> Your magical smite now radiates protective energy. Whenever you cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Divine Smite|Divine Smite]]*, you and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Half Cover|Half Cover]] while in your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection|Aura of Protection]]. The aura has this benefit until the start of your next turn.
