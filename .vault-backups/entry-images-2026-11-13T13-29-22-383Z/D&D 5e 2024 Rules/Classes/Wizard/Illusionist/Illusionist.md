# Illusionist

**Class:** [[Classes/Wizard/Wizard|Wizard]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Illusion Savant

Add two level-2-or-lower Illusion Wizard spells to your spellbook for free. Each new spell-slot level in this class grants another free eligible Illusion spell.

### Level 3: Improved Illusions

Illusion spells need no Verbal components and ranges of at least 10 feet increase by 60 feet. Gain Minor Illusion without using a known-cantrip choice; it can create sound and image together and be cast as a Bonus Action. If already known, gain another Wizard cantrip.

### Level 6: Phantasmal Creatures

Always prepare Summon Beast and Summon Fey. They may be cast as Illusions; a slot-free Illusion casting of each is available per Long Rest, but halves the summoned creature’s Hit Points.

### Level 10: Illusory Self

React when an attack hits you to make it miss instead. Refresh on a Short or Long Rest, or restore the use with a level-2+ slot.

### Level 14: Illusory Reality

For an Illusion cast with a spell slot, a Bonus Action can make one inanimate, nonmagical object in it real for one minute. That object cannot deal damage or impose conditions.
