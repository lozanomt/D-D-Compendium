# Darkvision

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Darkvision
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a dried carrot)  
> **Duration:** 8 hours  
>
> For the duration, a willing creature you touch has [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] with a range of 150 feet.
>
