# Weapon of Warning

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Weapon of Warning [[Weapon of Warning|(Varies)]]
> *Weapon (Any Simple or Martial), Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p328-weapon-of-warning.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you and allies within 30 feet of you gain the following benefits.
>
> ***Alarm.*** The weapon magically awakens each subject who is sleeping naturally when combat begins. This benefit doesn’t wake a subject from magically induced sleep.
>
> ***Supernatural Readiness.*** Each subject has [[Advantage|Advantage]] on its [[Initiative|Initiative]] rolls.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
