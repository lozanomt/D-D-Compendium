# Wall of Stone

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wall of Stone
> *Level 5 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a cube of granite)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> A nonmagical wall of solid stone springs into existence at a point you choose within range. The wall is 6 inches thick and is composed of ten 10-foot-by-10-foot panels. Each panel must be contiguous with another panel. Alternatively, you can create 10-footby-20-foot panels that are only 3 inches thick.
>
> If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (you choose which side). If a creature would be surrounded on all sides by the wall (or the wall and another solid surface), that creature can make a Dexterity saving throw. On a success, it can use its Reaction to move up to its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] so that it is no longer enclosed by the wall.
>
> The wall can have any shape you desire, though it can't occupy the same space as a creature or object. The wall doesn't need to be vertical or rest on a firm foundation. It must, however, merge with and be solidly supported by existing stone. Thus, you can use this spell to bridge a chasm or create a ramp.
>
> If you create a span greater than 20 feet in length, you must halve the size of each panel to create supports. You can crudely shape the wall to create battlements and the like.
>
> The wall is an object made of stone that can be damaged and thus breached. Each panel has AC 15 and 30 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] per inch of thickness, and it has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to Poison and Psychic damage. Reducing a panel to 0 Hit Points destroys it and might cause connected panels to collapse at the GM's discretion.
>
> If you maintain your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] on this spell for its full duration, the wall becomes permanent and can't be dispelled. Otherwise, the wall disappears when the spell ends.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
