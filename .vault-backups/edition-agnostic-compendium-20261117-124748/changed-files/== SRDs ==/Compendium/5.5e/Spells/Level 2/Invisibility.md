# Invisibility

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Invisibility
> *Level 2 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (an eyelash in gum arabic)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> A creature you touch has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition until the spell ends. The spell ends early immediately after the target makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
