## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Rod of Alertness
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Rod, Very Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) This rod has the following properties.
>
> Alertness. While holding the rod, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on [[Initiative|Initiative]] rolls.
>
> Spells. While holding the rod, you can cast the following spells from it:
>
> - [[Detect Evil and Good|Detect Evil and Good]]
>
> - [[Detect Magic|Detect Magic]]
>
> - [[Detect Poison and Disease|Detect Poison and Disease]]
>
> - [[See Invisibility|See Invisibility]]
>
> Protective Aura.  As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod's head sheds [[Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 60-foot radius and [[Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet. While in that [[Bright Light|Bright Light]], you and your allies gain a +1 bonus to [[Armor Class|Armor Class]] and saving throws and can sense the location of any [[Invisible|Invisible]] creature that is also in the [[Bright Light|Bright Light]].
>
> The rod's head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes or when a creature takes a [[Magic|Magic]] action to pull the rod from the ground. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=241|SRD 5.2.1, p. 241]]
