# Eyes of the Eagle

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Eyes of the Eagle [[Eyes of the Eagle|(400 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[dmg-p264-eyes-of-charming.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([[Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
