# Gloves of Thievery [[Gloves of Thievery|(400 GP)]]
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Dungeon Masters Guide/dmg-p268-goggles-of-night.png|320]]


**Attunement:** No

These gloves are imperceptible while worn. While wearing them, you gain a +5 bonus to Dexterity ([[Sleight of Hand]]) checks.
