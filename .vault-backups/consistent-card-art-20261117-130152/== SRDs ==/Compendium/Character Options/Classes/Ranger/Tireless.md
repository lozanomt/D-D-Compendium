# Tireless

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Tireless
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p121-ranger.png|320]]
>
> Primal forces now help fuel you on your journeys, granting you the following benefits.
>
> ***Temporary Hit Points.*** As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can give yourself a number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 1d8 plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). You can use this action a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Decrease Exhaustion.*** Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level, if any, decreases by 1.
>
> #### Handbook source
>

>
