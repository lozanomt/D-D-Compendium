# Eyes of Minute Seeing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Eyes of Minute Seeing
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[dmg-p264-eyes-of-charming.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> While wearing these [[Crystal|crystal]] lenses, you have [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] to a [[Range|range]] of 1 foot and [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Intelligence ([[Investigation|Investigation]]) checks to examine something within that range.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Eyes of Minute Seeing
>
> ![[dmg-p264-eyes-of-charming.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon
>
> These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, your vision improves significantly out to a range of 1 foot, granting you Darkvision within that range and [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to examine something within that range.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=221|SRD 5.2.1, p. 221]]
