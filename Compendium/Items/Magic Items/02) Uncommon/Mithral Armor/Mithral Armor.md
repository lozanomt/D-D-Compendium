# Mithral Armor

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Mithral Armor [[Mithral Armor|(Varies)]]
> *Armor (any Medium or Heavy, except [[Hide Armor|Hide Armor]]), Uncommon*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This armor weighs half as much as it normally would and can be worn under regular clothes. In addition, if the armor normally imposes [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Dexterity ([[Stealth|Stealth]]) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor does not.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Mithral Armor
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Armor (Any Medium or [[Heavy|Heavy]], Except [[Hide|Hide]] Armor), Uncommon
>
> Mithral is a light, flexible metal. Armor made of this substance can be worn under normal clothes. If the armor normally imposes [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.
>
