# Dragon Companion

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dragon Companion
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p151-draconic-sorcery.png|320]]
>
> You can cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 5/Summon Dragon|Summon Dragon]]* without a Material component. You can also cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in this way when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. Whenever you start casting the spell, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]]. If you do so, the spell’s duration becomes 1 minute for that casting.
>
