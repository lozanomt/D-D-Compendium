# Psi Warrior

**Class:** [[Classes/Fighter/Fighter|Fighter]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Psionic Power

Gain Psionic Energy Dice using the source table. Recover one die on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest. Protective Field reacts to reduce damage; Psionic Strike adds Force damage to a qualifying weapon hit once on your turn; Telekinetic Movement transports a willing creature or eligible loose object. Intelligence modifies the powers.

### Level 7: Telekinetic Adept

Psi-Powered Leap briefly grants flight at twice your Speed; free use recovers on a Short or Long Rest and a die can restore it. Psionic Strike can also force a Strength save to knock the target Prone or move it horizontally.

### Level 10: Guarded Mind

Gain Psychic Resistance. At your turn’s start, spend a Psionic Energy Die to end effects making you Charmed or Frightened.

### Level 15: Bulwark of Force

A Bonus Action grants Half Cover for one minute to up to your Intelligence modifier in creatures within 30 feet, minimum one. Refresh on a Long Rest; a Psionic Energy Die can restore the use.

### Level 18: Telekinetic Master

Prepare Telekinesis and cast it using Intelligence without a slot or components once per Long Rest; a die can restore the use. While concentrating, you can make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action each turn.
