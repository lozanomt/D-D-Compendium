# Rope

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Rope [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Rope|(1 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can tie a knot with Rope if you succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]) check. The Rope can be burst with a successful DC 20 Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check.
>
> You can bind an unwilling creature with the Rope only if the creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition. If the creature’s legs are bound, the creature has the Restrained condition until it escapes. Escaping the Rope requires the creature to make a successful DC 15 Dexterity ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check as an action.
>
> **Weight:** 5 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
