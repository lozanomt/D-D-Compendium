# Net

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Net [[Net|(1 GP)]]
>
> ![[phb-p230-net.png|320]]
>
> When you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a Net. Target a creature you can see within 15 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or have the [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition until it escapes. The target succeeds automatically if it is Huge or larger.
>
> To escape, the target or a creature within 5 feet of it must take an action to make a DC 10 Strength ([[Athletics|Athletics]]) check, freeing the Restrained creature on a success. Destroying the Net (AC 10; 5 HP; [[Immunity|Immunity]] to Bludgeoning, Poison, and Psychic damage) also frees the target, ending the effect.
>
> **Weight:** 3 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
