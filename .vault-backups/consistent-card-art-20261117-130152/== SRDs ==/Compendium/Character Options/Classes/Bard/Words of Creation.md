# Words of Creation

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Words of Creation
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p061-bard.png|320]]
>
> You have mastered two of the Words of Creation: the words of life and death. You therefore always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Power Word Heal|Power Word Heal]]* and *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Power Word Kill|Power Word Kill]]* spells prepared. When you cast either spell, you can target a second creature with it if that creature is within 10 feet of the first target.
>
