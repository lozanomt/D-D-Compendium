# Abjurer

**Class:** [[Classes/Wizard/Wizard|Wizard]]

## Features

### Level 3: Abjuration Savant

Add two level-2-or-lower Abjuration Wizard spells to your spellbook for free. Each new spell-slot level in this class grants one more free eligible Abjuration Wizard spell.

### Level 3: Arcane Ward

Casting a slot-funded Abjuration spell can create a ward with maximum Hit Points equal to twice Wizard level + Intelligence modifier. It absorbs damage after applicable Resistance/Vulnerability. Later slot-funded Abjurations or Bonus Action slot expenditures restore twice the slot level in ward Hit Points. Create once per Long Rest.

### Level 6: Projected Ward

React when a visible creature within 30 feet takes damage to have your Arcane Ward absorb it, after that creature’s Resistance/Vulnerability. Any excess reaches the creature.

### Level 10: Spell Breaker

Always prepare Counterspell and Dispel Magic. Dispel Magic can be a Bonus Action and adds Proficiency Bonus to its ability check. A slot spent on either spell is not expended if it fails to stop a spell.

### Level 14: Spell Resistance

Gain Advantage on saves against spells and Resistance to spell damage.
