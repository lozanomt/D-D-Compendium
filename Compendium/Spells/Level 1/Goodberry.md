# Goodberry

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Goodberry
>
> *Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a sprig of mistletoe)  
> **Duration:** 24 hours  
>
> Ten berries appear in your hand and are infused with magic for the duration. A creature can take a Bonus Action to eat one berry. Eating a berry restores 1 [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] and the berry provides enough nourishment to sustain a creature for one day.
>
> Uneaten berries disappear when the spell ends.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
