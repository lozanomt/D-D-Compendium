# See Invisibility

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### See Invisibility
> *Level 2 Divination (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of talc)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> For the duration, you see creatures and objects that have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition as if they were visible, and you can see into [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Lore/The Ethereal Plane|The Ethereal Plane]]. Creatures and objects there appear ghostly.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
