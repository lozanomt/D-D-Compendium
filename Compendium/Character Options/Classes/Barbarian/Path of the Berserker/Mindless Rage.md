# Mindless Rage

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Mindless Rage
>
> ![[phb-p057-path-of-the-berserker.png|320]]
>
> You have Immunity to the [[Charmed|Charmed]] and [[Frightened|Frightened]] conditions while your Rage is active. If you’re Charmed or Frightened when you enter your Rage, the condition ends on you.
