# Lightning Bolt

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Lightning Bolt
> *Level 3 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of fur and a crystal rod)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A stroke of lightning forming a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Line|Line]] blasts out from you in a direction you choose. Each creature in the Line makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
