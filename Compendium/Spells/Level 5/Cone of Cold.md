# Cone of Cold

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Cone of Cold
> *Level 5 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Attachments/Artwork/phb-p240-cone-of-cold.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a small crystal or glass cone)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You unleash a blast of cold air. Each creature in a 60-foot [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cone|Cone]] originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d8 Cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature killed by this spell becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 5.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
