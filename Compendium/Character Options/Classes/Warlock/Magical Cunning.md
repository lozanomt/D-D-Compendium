# Magical Cunning

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Magical Cunning
>
> ![[phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You can perform an esoteric rite for 1 minute. At the end of it, you regain expended [[Pact Magic|Pact Magic]] spell slots but no more than a number equal to half your maximum (round up). Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Eldritch Master.*** At Warlock level 20, when you use your Magical Cunning feature, you regain all your expended Pact Magic spell slots.
>
