# Stable

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Stable
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature is Stable if it has 0 Hit Points but isn’t required to make [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]], but it still has the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition. If the creature takes damage, it stops being Stable and starts making Death Saving Throws again. A Stable creature that isn't healed regains 1 [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] after 1d4 hours.
>
> You can take the [[Compendium/Rules/Actions/Help|Help]] action to try to stabilize a creature with 0 Hit Points, which requires a successful DC 10 Wisdom ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Medicine|Medicine]]) check.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
