# Cloak of Invisibility

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Cloak of Invisibility
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The cloak has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to pull its hood over your head and expend 1 charge to give yourself the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you pull the hood down (no action required) or cease wearing the cloak.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Cloak of Invisibility
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This cloak has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the cloak, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to pull its hood over your head and expend 1 charge to give yourself the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you pull the hood down (no action required) or cease wearing the cloak.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=215|SRD 5.2.1, p. 215–216]]
