## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Holy Symbol
>
> A Holy [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Symbol|Symbol]] takes one of the forms in the Holy [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Symbol|Symbol]] table and is bejeweled or painted to channel divine magic. A Cleric or Paladin can use a Holy [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Symbol|Symbol]] as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]].
>
> The table indicates whether a Holy [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Symbol|Symbol]] needs to be held, worn, or borne on fabric (such as a tabard or banner) or a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]].
