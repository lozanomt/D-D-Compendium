>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Guided Strike
>
> When you or a creature within 30 feet misses an attack, use Channel Divinity to add +10 to the roll. Helping another creature also requires your Reaction.
>
> #### Level 3: War Domain Spells
>
> Always prepare the level-appropriate spells from the source table, including Guiding Bolt, Magic Weapon, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon, Crusader’s Mantle, Spirit Guardians, Fire Shield, Freedom of Movement, Hold Monster, and Steel Wind Strike.
>
> #### Level 3: War Priest
>
> Make a weapon attack or Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action. Uses equal Wisdom modifier, minimum one; recover on a Short or Long Rest.
>
> #### Level 6: War God’s Blessing
>
> Use Channel Divinity instead of a spell slot to cast Shield of Faith or Spiritual Weapon. These castings do not require Concentration and last one minute, subject to the source’s early-ending conditions.
>
> #### Level 17: Avatar of Battle
>
> Gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
