# Arcane Trickster

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Arcane Trickster
>
> ![[phb-p135-arcane-trickster.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Rogue|Rogue]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Spellcasting
>
> Cast Wizard spells using Intelligence and an Arcane Focus. Know Mage Hand plus two other cantrips; add another at Rogue level 10. Start with three prepared level-1 spells. On gaining a Rogue level, replace one prepared spell and one cantrip other than Mage Hand. Slots recover on Long Rests; use the source table for progression.
>
> #### Level 3: Mage Hand Legerdemain
>
> Cast and control Mage Hand as a Bonus Action, make the hand Invisible, and perform Sleight of Hand checks through it.
>
> #### Level 9: Magical Ambush
>
> While Invisible when casting a spell on a creature, impose Disadvantage on its saves against that spell on that turn.
>
> #### Level 13: Versatile Trickster
>
> When using Cunning Strike’s Trip option, also apply it to another creature within 5 feet of your Mage Hand.
>
> #### Level 17: Spell Thief
>
> React to a spell targeting or including you and force the caster to make an Intelligence save. Failure negates its effect on you and can give you that spell prepared for eight hours while denying it to the caster, subject to the spell-level requirements. Once you steal a spell, refresh on a Long Rest.
>
