# Draconic Spirit

## Player's Handbook

> [!statblock]
> ### Draconic Spirit
> *Large Dragon, Neutral*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p328-summon-dragon.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 14 + the spell's level  
> **HP:** 50 + 10 for each spell level above 5  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Fly]] 60 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] 30 ft.  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> | **Int** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Wis** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Cha** | 14 | +2 | +2 |
>
> **Resistances:** Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison  
> **Immunities:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] 30 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 12  
> **Languages:** Draconic, understands the languages you know  
> **CR:** None (XP 0; PB equals your Proficiency Bonus)  
>
> #### Traits
> ***Shared Resistances.*** When you summon the spirit, choose one of its Resistances. You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to the chosen damage type until the spell ends.
> #### Actions
> ***Multiattack.*** The spirit makes a number of Rend attacks equal to half this spell's level (round down), and it uses Breath Weapon.
>
> ***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 1d6 + 4 + the spell’s level Piercing damage.
>
> ***Breath Weapon.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC equals your spell save DC, each creature in a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cone|Cone]]. *Failure:* 2d6 damage of a type this spirit has Resistance to (your choice when you cast the spell). *Success:* Half damage.
>
