# Dwarven Thrower

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Dwarven Thrower [[Dwarven Thrower|(40,000 GP)]]
> *Weapon ([[Warhammer|Warhammer]]), Very Rare*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes (by a Dwarf or a Creature Attuned to a [[Belt of Dwarvenkind|Belt of Dwarvenkind]])  
>
> You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. It has the [[Thrown|Thrown]] property with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. When you hit with a ranged attack using this weapon, it deals an extra 1d8 Force damage, or an extra 2d8 Force damage if the target is a Giant. Immediately after hitting or missing, the weapon flies back to your hand
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
