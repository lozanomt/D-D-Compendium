## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Cubic Gate
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p250-instant-fortress.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary This cube is 3 inches across and radiates palpable magical energy. The six sides of the cube are each keyed to a different plane of existence, one of which is the Material Plane. The other sides are linked to planes determined by the GM.
>
> The cube has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend 1 of the cube's charges to cast one of the following spells using the cube.
>
> [[Gate|Gate]]. Pressing one side of the cube, you cast [[Gate|Gate]], opening a portal to the plane of existence keyed to that side.
>
> [[Plane Shift|Plane Shift]]. Pressing one side of the cube twice, you cast [[Plane Shift|Plane Shift]], transporting the targets to the plane of existence keyed to that side.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=216|SRD 5.2.1, p. 216]]
