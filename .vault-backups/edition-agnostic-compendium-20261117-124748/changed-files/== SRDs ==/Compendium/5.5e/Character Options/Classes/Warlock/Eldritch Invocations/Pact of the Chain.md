# Pact of the Chain

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Pact of the Chain
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You learn the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]* spell and can cast it as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action without expending a spell slot.
>
> When you cast the spell, you choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Imp#Monster Manual|Imp]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Pseudodragon#Monster Manual|Pseudodragon]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Quasit#Monster Manual|Quasit]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Skeleton#Monster Manual|Skeleton]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Slaad Tadpole|Slaad Tadpole]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Sphinx of Wonder#Monster Manual|Sphinx of Wonder]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Sprite#Monster Manual|Sprite]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Venomous Snake#Monster Manual|Venomous Snake]].
>
> Additionally, when you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own with its Reaction.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Pact of the Chain
>
> You learn the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Find Familiar|Find Familiar]] spell and can cast it as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action without expending a spell slot.
>
> When you cast the spell, you choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: Imp, Pseudodragon, Quasit, Skeleton, Sphinx of Wonder, Sprite, or Venomous Snake (see "Monsters" for the familiar's stat block).
>
> Additionally, when you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own with its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=74|SRD 5.2.1, p. 74]]
