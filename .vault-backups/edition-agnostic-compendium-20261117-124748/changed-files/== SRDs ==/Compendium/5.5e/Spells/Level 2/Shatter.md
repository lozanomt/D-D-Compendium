# Shatter

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Shatter
> *Level 2 Evocation (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a chip of mica)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A loud noise erupts from a point of your choice within range. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered there makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 3d8 Thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A Construct has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the save.
>
> A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
