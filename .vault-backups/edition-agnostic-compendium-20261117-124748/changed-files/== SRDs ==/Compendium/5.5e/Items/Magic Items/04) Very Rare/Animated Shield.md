# Animated Shield

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Animated Shield
> *Armor (Shield), Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While holding the Shield, take a Bonus Action to animate it. It hovers in your space and protects you as if you were wielding it, leaving your hands free. It remains animated for 1 minute, until you end the effect with a Bonus Action, or until you die or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. It then falls to the ground or into a free hand.
>

>
