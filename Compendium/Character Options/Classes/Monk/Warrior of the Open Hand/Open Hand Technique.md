# Open Hand Technique

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Open Hand Technique
>
> ![[phb-p110-warrior-of-the-open-hand.png|320]]
>
> Whenever you hit a creature with an attack granted by your Flurry of Blows, you can impose one of the following effects on that target.
>
> ***Addle.*** The target can’t make [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] until the start of its next turn.
>
> ***Push.*** The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 15 feet away from you.
>
> ***Topple.*** The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the [[Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
