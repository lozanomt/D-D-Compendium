# Stroke of Luck

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]

You have a marvelous knack for succeeding when you need to. If you fail a [[D20 Test]], you can turn the roll into a 20.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or[[Long Rest]].
