# Uncanny Metabolism

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Uncanny Metabolism
>
> ![[phb-p103-monk.png|320]]
>
> When you roll [[Initiative|Initiative]], you can regain all expended Focus Points. When you do so, roll your Martial Arts die, and regain a number of [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to your Monk level plus the number rolled. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Perfect Focus.*** At Monk level 15, when you roll Initiative and don’t use Uncanny Metabolism, you regain expended Focus Points until you have 4 if you have 3 or fewer.
>

>
