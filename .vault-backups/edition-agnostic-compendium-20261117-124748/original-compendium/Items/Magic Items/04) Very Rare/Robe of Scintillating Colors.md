## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Robe of Scintillating Colors
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This robe has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While you wear it, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and expend 1 charge to cause the garment to display a shifting pattern of dazzling hues until the end of your next turn.
>
> During this time, the robe sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet, and creatures that can see you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you. Any creature in the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] that can see you when the robe's power is activated must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]] condition until the effect ends.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=240|SRD 5.2.1, p. 240]]
