# Knocking Out a Creature

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Knocking Out a Creature
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you would reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack, you can instead reduce the creature to 1 Hit Point. The creature then has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition and starts a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]].
>
> The creature remains Unconscious until it regains any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or until someone uses an action to administer first aid to it, which requires a successful DC 10 Wisdom ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Medicine|Medicine]]) check.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
