# Frigid Water

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Frigid Water
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature can be immersed in frigid water for a number of minutes equal to its Constitution score before suffering any ill effects. Each additional minute spent in frigid water requires the creature to succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level. Creatures with [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] or [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to Cold damage automatically succeed on the save, as do creatures that are naturally adapted to living in ice-cold water.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
