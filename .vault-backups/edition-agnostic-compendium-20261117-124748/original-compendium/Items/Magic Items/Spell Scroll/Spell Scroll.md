# Spell Scroll

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Spell Scroll
> *Scroll, Rarity Varies*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p308-spell-scroll.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> A Spell Scroll holds one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list, you can read and cast it without Material [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Components|components]]; otherwise the scroll is unintelligible. Use the spell's normal casting time. The scroll crumbles to dust when the spell is cast; interrupting the casting does not destroy it.
>
> If the spell is on your list but is a higher level than you can normally cast, make an ability check using your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Spellcasting|spellcasting]] ability, with DC 10 + the spell's level. On a failure, the spell disappears from the scroll without effect.

The copier makes an Intelligence ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Arcana|Arcana]]) check with DC 10 + the spell's level. A success copies the spell. The scroll is destroyed whether the check succeeds or fails.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Spell Scroll
>
> #### [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Cantrip|Cantrip]], 30 GP; Level 1, 50 GP) — p. 99
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Cantrip|Cantrip]]) or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll (Level 1) is a magic item that bears the words of a cantrip or level 1 spell, respectively, determined by the scroll's creator. If the spell is on your class's spell list, you can read the scroll and cast the spell using its normal casting time and without providing any Material components.
>
> If the spell requires a saving throw or an attack roll, the spell save DC is 13, and the attack bonus is +5. The scroll disintegrates when the casting is completed.
>
> #### [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll — p. 244
>
> Scroll, Rarity Varies A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll bears the words of a single spell, written in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list, you can read the scroll and cast its spell without Material components. Otherwise, the scroll is unintelligible. Casting the spell by reading the scroll requires the spell's normal casting time. Once the spell is cast, the scroll crumbles to dust. If the casting is interrupted, the scroll isn't lost.
>
> If the spell is on your spell list but of a higher level than you can normally cast, you make an ability check using your spellcasting ability to determine whether you cast the spell. The DC equals 10 plus the spell's level. On a failed check, the spell disappears from the scroll with no other effect.
>
> The level of the spell on the scroll determines the spell's saving throw DC and attack bonus, as well as the scroll's rarity, as shown in the following table.
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/Tables/srd-p244-table-2.png]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/Tables/srd-p244-table-1.png]]
>
> Copying a Scroll into a Spellbook. A Wizard spell on a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll can be copied into a spellbook.
>
> When a spell is copied in this way, the copier must succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) check with a
>
> DC equal to 10 plus the spell's level. On a successful check, the spell is copied. Whether the check succeeds or fails, the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Scroll  is destroyed.
