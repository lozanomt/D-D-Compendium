# Potion of Climbing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Climbing [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/01) Common/Potion of Climbing|(50 GP)]]
> *Potion, Common*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p289-potion-of-climbing.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> When you drink this potion, you gain a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Climbing|Climb]] Speed equal to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) checks to climb. This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
