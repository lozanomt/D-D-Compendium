# Longstrider

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Longstrider
> *Level 1 Transmutation (Bard, Druid, Ranger, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of dirt)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> You touch a creature. The target's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] increases by 10 feet until the spell ends.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
