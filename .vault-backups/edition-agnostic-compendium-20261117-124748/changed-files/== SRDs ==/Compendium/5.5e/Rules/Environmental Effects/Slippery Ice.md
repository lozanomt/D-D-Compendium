# Slippery Ice

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Slippery Ice
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Slippery ice is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]. A creature that moves onto slippery ice for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
