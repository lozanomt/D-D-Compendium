# Wand of Magic Detection [[Wand of Magic Detection|(400 GP)]]
*Wand, Uncommon*

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Dungeon Masters Guide/dmg-p323-wand-of-lightning-bolts.png|320]]


**Attunement:** No

This wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge to cast *[[Detect Magic]]* from it. The wand regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
