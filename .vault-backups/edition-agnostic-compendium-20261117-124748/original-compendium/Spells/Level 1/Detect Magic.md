# Detect Magic

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Detect Magic
> *Level 1 Divination (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> For the duration, you sense the presence of magical effects within 30 feet of yourself. If you sense such effects, you can take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears the magic, and if an effect was created by a spell, you learn the spell's school of magic.
>
> The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
