# Armor Class

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Armor Class
>
> An [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]] (AC) is the target number for an attack roll. AC represents how difficult it is to hit a target.
>
> Your base AC calculation is 10 plus your Dexterity modifier. If a rule gives you another base AC calculation, you choose which calculation to use; you can't use more than one. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attack Roll|Attack Roll]]."
>
