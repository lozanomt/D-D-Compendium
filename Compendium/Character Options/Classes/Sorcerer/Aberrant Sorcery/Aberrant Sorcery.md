# Aberrant Sorcery

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Aberrant Sorcery
>
> ![[phb-p148-aberrant-sorcery.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Sorcerer|Sorcerer]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Psionic Spells
>
> Always prepare the source’s Psionic Spells as you reach their levels, including Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver, Hunger of Hadar, Sending, Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration, Telepathic Bond, and Telekinesis.
>
> #### Level 3: Telepathic Speech
>
> A Bonus Action links you to a visible creature within 30 feet for Sorcerer-level minutes. Communicate over Charisma-modifier miles, minimum one mile, while using a language the other knows. A new connection ends the old one.
>
> #### Level 6: Psionic Sorcery
>
> Cast level-1+ Psionic Spells using Sorcery Points equal to spell level instead of a slot. Doing so removes Verbal, Somatic, and noncostly, unconsumed Material components.
>
> #### Level 6: Psychic Defenses
>
> Resist Psychic damage and gain Advantage on saves to avoid or end Charmed or Frightened.
>
> #### Level 14: Revelation in Flesh
>
> A Bonus Action spends Sorcery Points for ten-minute mutations: aquatic movement and breathing, hovering flight, seeing Invisible creatures, or squeezing through tiny spaces and escaping restraints. Each chosen benefit costs one point.
>
> #### Level 18: Warping Implosion
>
> A Magic action teleports you up to 120 feet. Creatures within 30 feet of your departure make Strength saves against 5d10 Force damage and a pull toward that space, taking half damage on success. Refresh on a Long Rest; 5 Sorcery Points restore the use.
>
