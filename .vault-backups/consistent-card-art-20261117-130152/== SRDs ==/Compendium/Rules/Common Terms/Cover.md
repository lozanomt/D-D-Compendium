# Cover

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Cover
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] provides a degree of protection to a target behind it. There are three degrees of cover, each of which provides a different benefit to a target: Half [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws), Three-Quarters [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] (+5 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws), and Total [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] (can't be targeted directly). If behind more than one degree of cover, a target benefits only from the most protective degree. See also  "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
>
