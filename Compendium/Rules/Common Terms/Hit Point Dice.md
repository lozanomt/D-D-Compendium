# Hit Point Dice

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hit Point Dice
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Hit Point Dice, or Hit Dice for short, help determine a player character’s [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum. Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]] to regain Hit Points.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Hit Point Dice
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> [[Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Dice]], or Hit Dice for short, help determine a player character's Hit Point maximum, as explained in "Character [[Creation|Creation]]." Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]] to regain [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. See also  "[[Short Rest|Short Rest]]."
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=183|SRD 5.2.1, p. 183]]
