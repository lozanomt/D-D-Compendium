# Hover

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hover
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Some creatures can hover, as noted in their stat blocks, and some spells and other effects grant the ability to hover. Hovering while [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Flying]] prevents you from falling in certain circumstances.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Hover
>
> Some creatures can hover, as noted in their stat blocks, and some spells and other effects grant the ability to hover. Hovering while flying prevents you from falling in certain circumstances. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Flying]]."
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=183|SRD 5.2.1, p. 183]]
