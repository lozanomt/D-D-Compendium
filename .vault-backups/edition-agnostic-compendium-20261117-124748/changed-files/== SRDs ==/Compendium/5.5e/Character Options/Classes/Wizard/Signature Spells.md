# Signature Spells

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Signature Spells
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p167-wizard.png|320]]
>
> Choose two level 3 spells in your spellbook as your signature spells. You always have these spells prepared, and you can cast each of them once at level 3 without expending a spell slot. When you do so, you can’t cast them in this way again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. To cast either spell at a higher level, you must expend a spell slot.
>
