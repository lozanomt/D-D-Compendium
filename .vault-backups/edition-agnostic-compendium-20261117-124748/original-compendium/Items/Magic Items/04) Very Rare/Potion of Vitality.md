## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Vitality
>
> Potion, Very Rare When you drink this potion, it removes any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] levels you have and ends the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition on you. For the next 24 hours, you regain the maximum number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] for any Hit Point Die you spend.
>
> This potion's crimson liquid regularly pulses with dull light, calling to mind a heartbeat.
>
