#### Weapon Mastery
Weapon Mastery depends on the class that grants it. Use the class’s own progression table for the number of mastered weapon kinds.

| Class | Initial choices | Eligible weapons | Replacement after a Long Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Barbarian]] | 2 | Simple or Martial Melee weapons | Change one choice |
| [[Fighter]] | 3 | Simple or Martial weapons | Change one choice |
| [[Paladin]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
| [[Ranger]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
| [[Rogue]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |

Barbarian and Fighter gain more choices at the levels shown in their class tables. Ranged weapons are eligible where the class’s restrictions allow them.
