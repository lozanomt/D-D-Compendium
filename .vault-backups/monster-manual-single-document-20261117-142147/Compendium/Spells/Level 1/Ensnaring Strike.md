# Ensnaring Strike

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Ensnaring Strike
> *Level 1 Conjuration (Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a weapon  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> As you hit the target, grasping vines appear on it, and it makes a Strength saving throw. A Large or larger creature has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on this save. On a failed save, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. On a successful save, the vines shrivel away, and the spell ends.
>
> While Restrained, the target takes 1d6 Piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The target or a creature within reach of it can take an action to make a Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check against your spell save DC. On a success, the spell ends.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
