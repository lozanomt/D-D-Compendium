## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Talisman of the Sphere
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While holding or wearing this talisman, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on any Intelligence (Arcana) check you make to control a [[Sphere|Sphere]] of Annihilation. In addition, when you start your turn in control of a [[Sphere|Sphere]] of Annihilation, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to move it 10 feet plus a number of additional feet equal to 10 times your Intelligence modifier. This movement doesn't have to be in a straight line.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=249|SRD 5.2.1, p. 249]]
