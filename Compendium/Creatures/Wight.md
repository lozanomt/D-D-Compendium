# Wight

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Wight
> *Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p334-wight.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 14  
> **Initiative:** +4 (14)  
> **HP:** 82 (11d8 + 33)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 15 | +2 | +2 | **Dex** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 16 | +3 | +3 |
> | **Int** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Wis** | 13 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** | 15 | +2 | +2 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Perception|Perception]] +3, [[Stealth|Stealth]] +4  
> **Resistances:** Necrotic  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Gear:** [[Studded Leather Armor|Studded Leather Armor]]  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 13  
> **Languages:** Common plus one other language  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> **Habitat** Desert, Planar (Shadowfell), Swamp, Underdark, Urban
> **Treasure** Armaments
>
> #### Traits
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the wight has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on ability checks and attack rolls.
> #### Actions
> ***Multiattack.*** The wight makes two attacks, using Necrotic Sword or Necrotic Bow in any combination. It can replace one attack with a use of Life Drain.
>
> ***Necrotic Sword.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Necrotic Bow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Life Drain.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 13, one creature within 5 feet. *Failure:* 6 (1d8 + 2) Necrotic damage, and the target's [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum decreases by an amount equal to the damage taken. A Humanoid slain by this attack rises 24 hours later as a [[Zombie#Monster Manual|Zombie]] under the wight's control, unless the Humanoid is restored to life or its body is destroyed. The wight can have no more than twelve zombies under its control at a time.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
