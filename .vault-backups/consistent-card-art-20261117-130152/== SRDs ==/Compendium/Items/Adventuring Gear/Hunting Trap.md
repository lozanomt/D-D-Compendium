# Hunting Trap

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hunting Trap [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Hunting Trap|(5 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can set a Hunting Trap, which is a sawtooth steel ring that snaps shut when a creature steps on a pressure plate in the center. The trap is affixed by a heavy chain to an immobile object, such as a tree or a spike driven into the ground. A creature that steps on the plate must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d4 Piercing damage and have its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. Thereafter, until the creature breaks free of the trap, its movement is limited by the length of the chain (typically 3 feet). A creature can use its action to make a DC 13 Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success. Each failed check deals 1 Piercing damage to the trapped creature.
>
> **Weight:** 25 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
