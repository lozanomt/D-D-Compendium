# Chain

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Chain [[Chain|(5 GP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[Grappled|Grappled]], [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Strength ([[Athletics|Athletics]]) check. If the creature’s legs are bound, the creature has the Restrained condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 Dexterity ([[Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check as an action.
>
> **Weight:** 10 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
