# Stoneskin

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Stoneskin
> *Level 4 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
