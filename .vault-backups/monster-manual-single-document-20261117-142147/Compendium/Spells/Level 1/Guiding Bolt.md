# Guiding Bolt

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Guiding Bolt
> *Level 1 Evocation (Cleric)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 1 round  
>
> You hurl a bolt of light toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 4d6 Radiant damage, and the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]].
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
