# Nature's Veil

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Nature's Veil
>
> ![[phb-p121-ranger.png|320]]
>
> You invoke spirits of nature to magically hide yourself. As a Bonus Action, you can give yourself the [[Invisible|Invisible]] condition until the end of your next turn.
>
> You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>

>
