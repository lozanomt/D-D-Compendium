# Favored Enemy

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Favored Enemy
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p121-ranger.png|320]]
>
> You always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]* spell prepared. You can cast it twice without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> The number of times you can cast the spell without a spell slot increases when you reach certain Ranger levels.
>
> ***Relentless Hunter.*** At Ranger level 13, taking damage can't break your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] on *Hunter's Mark*.
>
> ***Precise Hunter.*** At Ranger level 17, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls against the creature currently marked by your *Hunter's Mark*.
>
> ***Foe Slayer.*** At Ranger level 20, the damage die of your *Hunter's Mark* is a d10 rather than a d6
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
