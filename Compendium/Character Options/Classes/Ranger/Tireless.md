# Tireless

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Tireless
>
> ![[phb-p121-ranger.png|320]]
>
> Primal forces now help fuel you on your journeys, granting you the following benefits.
>
> ***Temporary Hit Points.*** As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can give yourself a number of [[Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to 1d8 plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). You can use this action a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Decrease Exhaustion.*** Whenever you finish a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]], your [[Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level, if any, decreases by 1.
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=122|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 122]]
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
