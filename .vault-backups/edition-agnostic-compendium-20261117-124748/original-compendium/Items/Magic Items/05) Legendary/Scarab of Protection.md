## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Scarab of Protection
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This beetle-shaped medallion provides three benefits while it is on your person.
>
> Defense. You gain a +1 bonus to [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]].
>
> Preservation.  The scarab has 12 charges. If you fail a saving throw against a Necromancy spell or a harmful effect originating from an Undead, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] to expend 1 charge and turn the failed save into a successful one. The scarab crumbles into powder and is destroyed when its last charge is expended.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Resistance.  You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=243|SRD 5.2.1, p. 243]]
