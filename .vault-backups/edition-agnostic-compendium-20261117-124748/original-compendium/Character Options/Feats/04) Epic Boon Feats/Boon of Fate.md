# Boon of Fate

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Boon of Fate
> *Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You gain the following benefits.
>
> ***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.
>
> ***Improve Fate.*** When you or another creature within 60 feet of you succeeds on or fails a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], you can roll 2d4 and apply the total rolled as a bonus or penalty to the d20 roll. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you roll Initiative or finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
