# Eldritch Smite

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Eldritch Smite
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock, [[Pact of the Blade|Pact of the Blade]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can expend a [[Pact Magic|Pact Magic]] spell slot to deal an extra 1d8 Force damage to the target, plus another 1d8 per level of the spell slot, and you can give the target the [[Prone|Prone]] condition if it is Huge or smaller.
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=156|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 156]]
>
