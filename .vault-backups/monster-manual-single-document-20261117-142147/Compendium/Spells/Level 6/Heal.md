# Heal

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Heal
> *Level 6 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Choose a creature that you can see within range. Positive energy washes through the target, restoring 70 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. This spell also ends the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]], and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions on the target.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The healing increases by 10 for each spell slot level above 6.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
