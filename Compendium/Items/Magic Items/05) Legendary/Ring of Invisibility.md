# Ring of Invisibility

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Invisibility
> *Ring, Legendary*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p296-ring-of-invisibility.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the ring, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to give yourself the [[Invisible|Invisible]] condition. It lasts until the ring is removed or you take a Bonus Action to become visible again.
>
