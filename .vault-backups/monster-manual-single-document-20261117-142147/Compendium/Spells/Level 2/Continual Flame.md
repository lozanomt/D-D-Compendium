# Continual Flame

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Continual Flame
> *Level 2 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (ruby dust worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** Until dispelled  
>
> A flame springs from an object that you touch. The effect casts [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. It looks like a regular flame, but it creates no heat and consumes no fuel. The flame can be covered or hidden but not smothered or quenched.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
