#### Protection from Poison
*Level 2 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch a creature and end the [[Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition, and it has [[Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]]]] to Poison damage.
