> #### As a Level 1 Character
> - Gain all the traits Core Rogue Traits table
> - Gain the Rogue's level 1 features, which are listed in the Rogue Features table.
> #### As a Multiclass Character
> - Gain the following traits from the Core Rogue Traits table: Hit Point Die, proficiency in one skill of your choice from the Rogue's skill list, proficiency with Thieves' Tools, and training with Light armor.
> - Gain the Rogue's level 1 features, which are listed in the Rogue Features table.

> #### Level 1: Expertise
> You gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Expertise|Expertise]] in two of your skill proficiencies of your choice.
>
> At Rogue level 6, you gain Expertise in two more of your skill proficiencies of your choice.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]]
> You know how to strike subtly and exploit a foe’s distraction. Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on the roll and the attack uses a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Finesse|Finesse]] or a Ranged weapon. The extra damage’s type is the same as the weapon’s type.
>
> You don’t need Advantage on the attack roll if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the ally doesn’t have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, and you don’t have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll.
>
> The extra damage increases as you gain Rogue levels, as shown in the Sneak Attack column of the Rogue Features table.
> #### Level 1: Thieves’ Cant
> You picked up various languages in the communities where you plied your roguish talents. You know Thieves’ Cant and one other language of your choice.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Weapon Mastery|Weapon Mastery]]
> Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Simple Melee/Dagger|Daggers]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Simple Ranged/Shortbow|Shortbows]].
>
> Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Scimitar|Scimitar]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Shortsword|Shortsword]].
> #### Level 2: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Cunning Action|Cunning Action]]
> Your quick thinking and agility allow you to move and act quickly. On your turn, you can take one of the following actions as a Bonus Action: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Dash|Dash]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Disengage|Disengage]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]].
> #### Level 3: Rogue Subclass
> You gain a Rogue subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Rogue levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Rogue level or lower. The following subclasses are available for you to choose from:
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Arcane Trickster/Arcane Trickster|Arcane Trickster]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Assassin/Assassin|Assassin]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Soulknife/Soulknife|Soulknife]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Thief|Thief]]
> #### Level 3: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Steady Aim|Steady Aim]]
> As a Bonus Action, you give yourself [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this feature only if you haven’t moved during this turn, and after you use it, your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is 0 until the end of the current turn.
> #### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
> You gain the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Feats/02) General Feats/Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Rogue levels 8, 12, and 16.
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Cunning Strike|Cunning Strike]]
> You’ve developed cunning ways to use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]]. When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can add one of the following Cunning Strike effects. Each effect has a die cost, which is the number of Sneak Attack damage dice you must forgo to add the effect. You remove the die before rolling, and the effect occurs immediately after the attack’s damage is dealt. For example, if you add the Poison effect, remove 1d6 from the Sneak Attack’s damage before rolling.
>
> If a Cunning Strike effect requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus.
>
> ***Poison (Cost: 1d6).*** You add a toxin to your strike, forcing the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the Poisoned target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. To use this effect, you must have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Tools/Poisoner's Kit|Poisoner's Kit]] on your person.
>
> ***Trip (Cost: 1d6).*** If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> ***Withdraw (Cost: 1d6).*** Immediately after the attack, you move up to half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] without provoking [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Uncanny Dodge|Uncanny Dodge]]
> When an attacker that you can see hits you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to halve the attack’s damage against you (round down).
> #### Level 7: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Evasion|Evasion]]
> When you’re subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw and only half damage if you fail. You don’t benefit from this feature if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
> #### Level 7: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Reliable Talent|Reliable Talent]]
> Whenever you make an ability check that uses one of your skill or tool proficiencies, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.
> #### Level 11: Improved Cunning Strike
> You can use up to two [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Cunning Strike|Cunning Strike]] effects when you deal [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]] damage, paying the die cost for each effect.
> #### Level 14: Devious Strikes
> You’ve practiced new ways to use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]] deviously. The following effects are now among your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Cunning Strike|Cunning Strike]] options.
>
> ***Daze (Cost: 2d6).*** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or on its next turn, it can do only one of the following: move or take an action or a Bonus Action.
>
> ***Knock Out (Cost: 6d6).*** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The Unconscious target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
> ***Obscure (Cost: 3d6).*** The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
> #### Level 15: Slippery Mind
> Your cunning mind is exceptionally difficult to control. You gain proficiency in Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.
> #### Level 18: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Elusive|Elusive]]
> You’re so evasive that attackers rarely gain the upper hand against you. No attack roll can have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] against you unless you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
> #### Level 19: Epic Boon
> You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.
> #### Level 20: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Stroke of Luck|Stroke of Luck]]
> You have a marvelous knack for succeeding when you need to. If you fail a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], you can turn the roll into a 20.
>
> Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
