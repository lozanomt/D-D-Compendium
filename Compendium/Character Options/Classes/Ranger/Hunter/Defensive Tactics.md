# Defensive Tactics

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Defensive Tactics
>
> ![[phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can replace the chosen option with the other one.
>
> ***Escape the Horde.*** [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] have [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] against you.
>
> ***Multiattack Defense.*** When a creature hits you with an attack roll, that creature has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all other attack rolls against you this turn.
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=130|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 130]]
>
