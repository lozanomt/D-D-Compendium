# Healing Word

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Healing Word
> *Level 1 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A creature of your choice that you can see within range regains [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The healing increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
