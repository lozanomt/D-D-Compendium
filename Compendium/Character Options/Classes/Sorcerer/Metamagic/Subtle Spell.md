# Subtle Spell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Subtle Spell
>
> ![[phb-p141-sorcerer.png|320]]
>
> **Cost:** 1 Sorcery Points  
>
> When you cast a spell, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to cast it without any Verbal, Somatic, or Material [[Components|components]], except Material components that are consumed by the spell or that have a cost specified in the spell.
>
