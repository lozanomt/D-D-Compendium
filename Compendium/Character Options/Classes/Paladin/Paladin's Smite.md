# Paladin's Smite

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Paladin's Smite
>
> ![[phb-p111-paladin.png|320]]
>
> #### Paladin’s Smite
> You always have the *[[Divine Smite|Divine Smite]]* spell prepared. In addition, you can cast it without expending a spell slot, but you must finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]] before you can cast it in this way again.
