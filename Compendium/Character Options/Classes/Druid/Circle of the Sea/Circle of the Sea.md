# Circle of the Sea

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Circle of the Sea
>
> ![[phb-p090-circle-of-the-sea.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Druid|Druid]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Circle of the Sea Spells
>
> Always prepare the level-appropriate circle spells: Fog Cloud, Gust of Wind, Ray of Frost, Shatter, Thunderwave, Lightning Bolt, Water Breathing, Control Water, Ice Storm, Conjure Elemental, and Hold Monster.
>
> #### Level 3: Wrath of the Sea
>
> Use a Bonus Action and a Wild Shape use to manifest a 5-foot Emanation for ten minutes. On activation and later Bonus Actions, another visible creature in it makes a Constitution save or takes Cold damage equal to Wisdom modifier d6s (minimum one die), and if Large or smaller can be pushed up to 15 feet.
>
> #### Level 6: Aquatic Affinity
>
> Wrath of the Sea expands to 10 feet. Gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
>
> #### Level 10: Stormborn
>
> While Wrath of the Sea is active, gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed and Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Thunder damage.
>
> #### Level 14: Oceanic Gift
>
> Manifest Wrath of the Sea around a willing creature within 60 feet instead of yourself. It uses your Wisdom and spell DC. Spending two Wild Shape uses manifests it around both of you.
>
