# Study

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Study
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you take the Study action, you make an Intelligence check to study your memory, a book, a clue, or another source of knowledge and call to mind an important piece of information about it.
>
> The Areas of Knowledge table suggests which skills are applicable to various areas of knowledge.
> #### Areas of Knowledge
> | Skill         | Areas                                                                                                                                                               |
> | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | [[Arcana\|Arcana]]        | Spells, magic items, eldritch symbols, magical traditions, planes of existence, and certain creatures (Aberrations, Constructs, Elementals, Fey, and Monstrosities) |
> | [[History\|History]]      | Historic events and people, ancient civilizations, wars, and certain creatures (Giants and Humanoids)                                                               |
> | [[Investigation\|Investigation]] | Traps, ciphers, riddles, and gadgetry                                                                                                                               |
> | [[Nature\|Nature]]        | Terrain, flora, weather, and certain creatures (Beasts, Dragons, Oozes, and Plants)                                                                                 |
> | [[Religion\|Religion]]      | Deities, religious hierarchies and rites, holy symbols, cults, and certain creatures (Celestials, Fiends, and Undead)                                               |
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
