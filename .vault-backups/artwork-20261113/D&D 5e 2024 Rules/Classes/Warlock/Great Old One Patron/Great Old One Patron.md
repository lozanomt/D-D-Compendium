## Features

### Level 3: Awakened Mind

A Bonus Action creates a telepathic bond with a visible creature within 30 feet. It lasts Warlock-level minutes, works over Charisma-modifier miles (minimum one), and requires a mutually understood language. A new bond ends the old one.

### Level 3: Great Old One Spells

Always prepare the source’s patron spells as you level, from Detect Thoughts and Dissonant Whispers through Modify Memory and Telekinesis.

### Level 3: Psychic Spells

Warlock spells can deal Psychic instead of their normal damage. Your Enchantment and Illusion Warlock spells need no Verbal or Somatic components.

### Level 6: Clairvoyant Combatant

When forming an Awakened Mind bond, force a Wisdom save. Failure grants your attacks Advantage against the target and its attacks Disadvantage against you for the bond’s duration. Refresh on Short/Long Rest; a Pact Magic slot restores it.

### Level 10: Eldritch Hex

Always prepare Hex. Its chosen ability also suffers Disadvantage on saving throws for the spell’s duration.

### Level 10: Thought Shield

Your thoughts cannot be read without permission. Resist Psychic damage; a creature dealing Psychic damage to you takes the same damage you actually take.

### Level 14: Create Thrall

Summon Aberration may waive Concentration for a one-minute duration and gain Warlock level + Charisma modifier Temporary Hit Points. Its first hit per turn against your Hex target adds the spell’s bonus damage as Psychic.
