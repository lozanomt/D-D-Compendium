# Circle of the Stars

**Class:** [[Classes/Druid/Druid|Druid]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Star Map

A personal star chart serves as your Druid focus. While holding it, prepare Guidance and Guiding Bolt and cast Guiding Bolt without a slot Wisdom-modifier times per Long Rest (minimum one). A one-hour ceremony replaces a lost chart.

### Level 3: Starry Form

A Bonus Action and Wild Shape use grant a ten-minute constellation form without changing your statistics. Choose Archer (Bonus Action ranged Radiant attack), Chalice (extra healing when a spell-slot spell heals), or Dragon (minimum d20 roll of 10 for Intelligence/Wisdom checks and Concentration saves). See the source for targeting and range.

### Level 6: Cosmic Omen

After a Long Rest, a die determines Weal or Woe. Your Reaction adds or subtracts 1d6 from a nearby visible creature’s upcoming D20 Test. Uses equal Wisdom modifier, minimum one; recover on a Long Rest.

### Level 10: Twinkling Constellations

Archer and Chalice dice become 2d8; Dragon grants a hovering 20-foot Fly Speed. You can switch constellation at the start of your turns while Starry Form lasts.

### Level 14: Full of Stars

While in Starry Form, gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
