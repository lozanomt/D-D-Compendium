# Fey Wanderer

**Class:** [[Classes/Ranger/Ranger|Ranger]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Dreadful Strikes

Weapon hits can add 1d4 Psychic damage to a target, once per target per turn. The die becomes 1d6 at Ranger level 11.

### Level 3: Fey Wanderer Spells

Always prepare Charm Person at level 3, Misty Step at 5, Summon Fey at 9, Dimension Door at 13, and Mislead at 17. Choose a cosmetic Feywild gift from the source or invent an equivalent appearance.

### Level 3: Otherworldly Glamour

Add Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) to Charisma checks. Gain proficiency in Deception, Performance, or Persuasion.

### Level 7: Beguiling Twist

Gain Advantage on saves to avoid or end Charmed or Frightened. When you or a visible creature within 120 feet succeeds at such a save, a Reaction can force a different visible creature within 120 feet to save against a minute of Charmed or Frightened, with repeated saves.

### Level 11: Fey Reinforcements

Cast Summon Fey without its Material component, with one slot-free use per Long Rest. You may waive Concentration by reducing the casting’s duration to one minute.

### Level 15: Misty Wanderer

Cast Misty Step without slots Wisdom-modifier times per Long Rest, minimum one. Whenever casting it, bring a willing creature within 5 feet to a space within 5 feet of your destination.
