## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Iron Bands
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p277-iron-bands.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare This rusty iron sphere measures 3 inches in diameter and weighs 1 pound. You can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to throw the sphere at a Huge or smaller creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. As the sphere moves through the air, it opens into a tangle of metal bands.
>
> Make a ranged attack roll with an attack bonus equal to your Dexterity modifier plus your [[Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus. On a hit, the target has the [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition until you take a [[Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to issue a command that releases it. Doing so or missing with the attack causes the bands to contract and become a sphere once more.
>
> A creature that can touch the bands, including the one [[Restrained|Restrained]], can take an action to make a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check to break the iron bands.
>
> On a successful check, the item is destroyed, and the
>
> [[Restrained|Restrained]] creature is freed. On a failed check, any further attempts made by that creature automatically fail until 24 hours have elapsed.
>
> Once the bands are used, they can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=228|SRD 5.2.1, p. 228]]
