# Ritual

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Ritual
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have a spell prepared that has the Ritual tag, you can cast that spell as a Ritual. The Ritual version of a spell takes 10 minutes longer to cast than normal. It also doesn’t expend a spell slot, which means the ritual version of a spell can’t be cast at a higher level.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ritual
>
> If you have a spell prepared that has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]] tag, you can cast that spell as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]. The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]] version of a spell takes 10 minutes longer to cast than normal. It also doesn't expend a spell slot, which means the ritual version of a spell can't be cast at a higher level. See also "Spells."
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=187|SRD 5.2.1, p. 187]]
