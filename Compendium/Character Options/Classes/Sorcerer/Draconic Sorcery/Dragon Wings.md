# Dragon Wings

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dragon Wings
>
> ![[phb-p151-draconic-sorcery.png|320]]
>
> As a Bonus Action, you can cause draconic wings to appear on your back. The wings last for 1 hour or until you dismiss them (no action required). For the duration, you have a [[Flying|Fly]] Speed of 60 feet. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]] unless you spend 3 Sorcery Points (no action required) to restore your use of it.
>
