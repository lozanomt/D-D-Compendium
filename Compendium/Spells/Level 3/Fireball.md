# Fireball

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fireball
> *Level 3 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Attachments/Artwork/phb-p277-fireball.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into a fiery explosion. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> Flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried start [[Compendium/Rules/Hazards/Burning|burning]].
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 3.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=277|Player's Handbook]]
