# Wand of Fear

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Wand of Fear
> *Wand, Rare*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p322-wand-of-enemy-detection.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The [[Wand|wand]] has 7 charges. While holding it, expend 1 charge to cast [[Command|Command]] (only the flee or grovel options) or 3 charges to cast [[Fear|Fear]] with a 60-foot [[Cone|Cone]] (save DC 15). It regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend its last charge, roll 1d20; on a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
>

>
