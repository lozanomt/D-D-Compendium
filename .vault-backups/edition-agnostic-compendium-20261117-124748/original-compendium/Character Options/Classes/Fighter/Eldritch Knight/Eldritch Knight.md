> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Spellcasting
>
> Use Intelligence to cast Wizard spells, with an Arcane Focus allowed. Start with two cantrips and three prepared level-1 spells; gain another cantrip at Fighter level 10. Replace one cantrip and one prepared spell when gaining a Fighter level. Recover slots on a Long Rest. Use the source’s subclass spell-slot and preparation table.
>
> #### Level 3: War Bond
>
> An hour-long ritual bonds a weapon; up to two weapons can be bonded. You cannot be disarmed of a bonded weapon unless Incapacitated and can summon one from the same plane as a Bonus Action. Existing bonds or another creature’s attunement can prevent a bond.
>
> #### Level 7: War Magic
>
> During the Attack action on your turn, replace one attack with one of your action-casting Wizard cantrips.
>
> #### Level 10: Eldritch Strike
>
> A weapon hit gives the creature Disadvantage on its next saving throw against a spell you cast before your next turn ends.
>
> #### Level 15: Arcane Charge
>
> When using Action Surge, teleport up to 30 feet to a visible unoccupied space before or after the additional action.
>
> #### Level 18: Improved War Magic
>
> During your Attack action, replace two attacks with an action-casting level-1 or level-2 Wizard spell.
