## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Mace of Terror
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] (Mace), Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This magic weapon has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the weapon, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and expend 1 charge to release a wave of terror from it.
>
> Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition for 1 minute. While [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] in this way, a creature must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can, and it can't make [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]]. For its action, it can use only the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Dash|Dash]] action or try to escape from an effect that prevents it from moving. If it has nowhere it can move, the creature can take the
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Dodge|Dodge]] action. At the end of each of its turns, a creature repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=229|SRD 5.2.1, p. 229]]
