> #### Becoming a Paladin...
> #### As a Level 1 Character
> - Gain all the traits Core Paladin Traits table
> - Gain the Paladin's level 1 features, which are listed in the Paladin Features table
> #### As a Multiclass Character
> - Gain the following traits from the Core Paladin Traits table: Hit Point Die, proficiency with Martial weapons, and training with Light and Medium armor and Shields
> - Gain the Paladin's level 1 features, which are listed in the Paladin Features table. See the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Miscellaneous/Multiclassing|Multiclassing]] rules to determine your available spell slots.

> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Lay on Hands|Lay on Hands]]
> Your blessed touch can heal wounds. You have a pool of healing power that replenishes when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. With that pool, you can restore a total number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to five times your Paladin level. As a Bonus Action, you can touch a creature (which could be yourself) and draw power from the pool of healing to restore a number of Hit Points to that creature, up to the maximum amount remaining in the pool. You can also expend 5 Hit Points from the pool of healing power to remove the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions from the creature; those points don’t also restore Hit Points to the creature.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Spellcasting|Spellcasting]]
> You have learned to cast spells through prayer and meditation.
>
> ***Spell Slots.*** The Paladin Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Prepared Spells of Level 1+.*** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Paladin spells.
>
> The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Paladin levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Paladin Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Paladin spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Paladin Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you’re a level 5 Paladin, your list of prepared spells can include six Paladin spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.
>
> If another Paladin feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don’t count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Paladin spells for you.
>
> ***Changing Your Prepared Spells.*** Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one spell on your list with another Paladin spell for which you have spell slots.
>
> ***Spellcasting Ability.*** Charisma is your spellcasting ability for your Paladin spells.
>
> ***Spellcasting Focus.*** You can use a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Holy Symbol/Holy Symbol|Holy Symbol]] as a Spellcasting Focus for your Paladin spells.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Weapon Mastery|Weapon Mastery]]
> Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Longsword|Longswords]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Simple Melee/Javelin|Javelins]]. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Halberd|Halberds]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Weapons/Martial Melee/Flail|Flails]].
> #### Level 2: Fighting Style
> You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice.
>
> Alternatively, choose **Blessed Warrior**: learn two Cleric cantrips of your choice. They count as Paladin spells for you and use Charisma. Whenever you gain a Paladin level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Cleric cantrip.
> #### Level 2: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Paladin's Smite|Paladin's Smite]]
> You always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Divine Smite|Divine Smite]]* spell prepared. In addition, you can cast it without expending a spell slot, but you must finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]] before you can cast it in this way again.
> #### Level 3: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Channel Oath|Channel Divinity]]
> You can channel divine energy directly from your oath, using it to fuel magical effects. You start with one such effect: Divine Sense, which is described below. Other Paladin features give additional Channel Divinity effect options. Each time you use this class’s Channel Divinity, you choose which effect from this class to create.
>
> You can use this class’s Channel Divinity twice. You regain one of its expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. You gain an additional use when you reach Paladin level 11. If a Channel Divinity effect requires a saving throw, the DC equals the spell save DC from this class’s Spellcasting feature.
>
> ***Divine Sense.*** As a Bonus Action, you can open your awareness to detect Celestials, Fiends, and Undead. For the next 10 minutes or until you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, you know the location of any creature of those types within 60 feet of yourself, and you know its creature type. Within the same radius, you also detect the presence of any place or object that has been consecrated or desecrated, as with the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Hallow|Hallow]]* spell.
> #### Level 3: Paladin Subclass
> You gain a Paladin subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Paladin levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Paladin level or lower. The following subclasses are available for you to choose from:
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Oath of Devotion/Oath of Devotion|Oath of Devotion]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Oath of Glory/Oath of Glory|Oath of Glory]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Oath of Vengeance/Oath of Vengeance|Oath of Vengeance]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Oath of the Ancients/Oath of the Ancients|Oath of the Ancients]]
> #### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
> You gain the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Feats/02) General Feats/Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Paladin levels 8, 12, and 16.
> #### Level 5: Extra Attack
> You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Faithful Steed|Faithful Steed]]
> You can call on the aid of an otherworldly steed. You always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 2/Find Steed|Find Steed]]* spell prepared. You can also cast the spell once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
> #### Level 6: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection|Aura of Protection]]
> You radiate a protective, unseeable aura in a 10-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] that originates from you. The aura is inactive while you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. You and your allies in the aura gain a bonus to saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum bonus of +1). If another Paladin is present, a creature can benefit from only one Aura of Protection at a time; the creature chooses which aura while in them.
> #### Level 9: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Abjure Foes|Abjure Foes]]
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend one use of this class’s [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Channel Oath|Channel Divinity]] to overwhelm foes with awe. As you present your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Holy Symbol/Holy Symbol|Holy Symbol]] or weapon, you can target a number of creatures equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one creature) that you can see within 60 feet of yourself. Each target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. While Frightened in this way, a target can do only one of the following on its turns: move, take an action, or take a Bonus Action.
> #### Level 10: Aura of Courage
> You and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition while in your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection|Aura of Protection]]. If a Frightened ally enters the aura, that condition has no effect on that ally while there.
> #### Level 11: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Radiant Strikes|Radiant Strikes]]
> Your strikes now carry supernatural power. When you hit a target with an attack roll using a Melee weapon or an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]], the target takes an extra 1d8 Radiant damage.
> #### Level 14: Restoring Touch
> When you use [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Lay on Hands|Lay on Hands]] on a creature, you can also remove one or more of the following conditions from the creature: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]]. You must expend 5 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] from the healing pool of Lay On Hands for each of these conditions you remove; those points don’t also restore Hit Points to the creature.
> #### Level 18: Aura Expansion
> Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection|Aura of Protection]] is now a 30-foot Emanation.
> #### Level 19: Epic Boon
> You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.

> ### Becoming a Paladin ...
>
> #### As a Level 1 Character
>
> - Gain all the traits in the Core Paladin Traits table.
>
> - Gain the Paladin's level 1 features, which are listed in the Paladin Features table.
>
> #### As a Multiclass Character
>
> - Gain the following traits from the Core Paladin Traits table: Hit Point Die, proficiency with Martial weapons, and training with Light and Medium armor and Shields.
>
> - Gain the Paladin's level 1 features, which are listed in the Paladin Features table. See the multiclassing rules in "Character [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Creation|Creation]]" to determine your available spell slots.
>
> #### Paladin Class Features
>
> As a Paladin, you gain the following class features when you reach the specified Paladin levels. These features are listed in the Paladin Features table.
>
> #### Level 1: Lay On Hands
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Lay on Hands#SRD 5.2.1|Lay on Hands]]
>
> #### Level 1: Spellcasting
>
> You have learned to cast spells through prayer and meditation. See "Spells" for the rules on spellcasting. The information below details how you use those rules with Paladin spells, which appear in the Paladin spell list later in the class's description.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Slots. The Paladin Features table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> Prepared Spells of Level 1+. You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose two level 1 Paladin spells. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Heroism|Heroism]]  and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Searing Smite|Searing Smite]] are recommended.
>
> The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Paladin levels, as shown in the Prepared
>
> Spells column of the Paladin Features table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional Paladin spells until the number of spells on your list matches the number in the Paladin Features table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you're a level 5 Paladin, your list of prepared spells can include six Paladin spells of level 1 or 2 in any combination.
>
> If another Paladin feature gives you spells that you always have prepared, those spells don't count against the number of spells you can prepare with this feature, but those spells otherwise count as Paladin spells for you.
>
> Changing Your Prepared Spells. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can replace one spell on your list with another Paladin spell for which you have spell slots.
>
> Spellcasting Ability. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for your Paladin spells.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]]. You can use a Holy [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Symbol|Symbol]] as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]] for your Paladin spells.
>
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] Mastery
>
> Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Longswords and Javelins.
>
> Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Halberds and Flails.
>
> #### Level 2: Fighting Style
>
> You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice (see "Feats" for feats). Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.
>
> Blessed Warrior. You learn two Cleric cantrips of your choice (see the Cleric class's section for a list of Cleric spells). [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Cantrip/Guidance|Guidance]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Cantrip/Sacred Flame|Sacred Flame]] are recommended. The chosen cantrips count as Paladin spells for you, and Charisma is your spellcasting ability for them. Whenever you gain a Paladin level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another Cleric cantrip.
>
> #### Level 2: Paladin's Smite
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Paladin's Smite#SRD 5.2.1|Paladin's Smite]]
>
> #### Level 3: Channel Divinity
>
> You can channel divine energy directly from the
>
> Outer Planes, using it to fuel magical effects. You start with one such effect: Divine Sense, which is described below. Other Paladin features give additional Channel Divinity effect options. Each time you use this class's Channel Divinity, you choose which effect from this class to create.
>
> You can use this class's Channel Divinity twice.
>
> You regain one of its expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. You gain an additional use when you reach Paladin level 11.
>
> If a Channel Divinity effect requires a saving throw, the DC equals the spell save DC from this class's Spellcasting feature.
>
> Divine Sense. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]], you can open your awareness to detect Celestials, Fiends, and
>
> Undead. For the next 10 minutes or until you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, you know the location of any creature of those types within 60 feet of yourself, and you know its creature type. Within the same radius, you also detect the presence of any place or object that has been consecrated or desecrated, as with the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Hallow|Hallow]]  spell.
>
> #### Level 3: Paladin Subclass
>
> You gain a Paladin subclass of your choice. The Oath of Devotion subclass is detailed after this class's description. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Paladin levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass's features that are of your Paladin level or lower.
>
> #### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
>
> You gain the Ability Score Improvement feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Paladin levels 8, 12, and 16.
>
> #### Level 5: Extra [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]]
>
> You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
>

>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection#SRD 5.2.1|Aura of Protection]]

>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Abjure Foes#SRD 5.2.1|Abjure Foes]]

> #### Level 10: Aura of Courage
>
> You and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition while in your Aura of Protection. If a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] ally enters the aura, that condition has no effect on that ally while there.
>
> #### Level 11: Radiant Strikes
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Radiant Strikes#SRD 5.2.1|Radiant Strikes]]
>
> #### Level 14: Restoring Touch
>
> When you use Lay On Hands on a creature, you can also remove one or more of the following conditions from the creature: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]],
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]]. You must expend 5 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] from the healing pool of Lay On Hands for each of these conditions you remove; those points don't also restore [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to the creature.
>
> #### Level 18: Aura Expansion
>
> Your Aura of Protection is now a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]].
>
> #### Level 19: Epic Boon
>
> You gain an Epic Boon feat (see "Feats") or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. Boon of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]] is recommended.
>
