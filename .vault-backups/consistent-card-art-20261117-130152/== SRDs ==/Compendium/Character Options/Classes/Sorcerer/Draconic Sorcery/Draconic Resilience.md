# Draconic Resilience

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Draconic Resilience
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p151-draconic-sorcery.png|320]]
>
> The magic in your body manifests physical traits of your draconic gift. Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum increases by 3, and it increases by 1 whenever you gain another Sorcerer level. Parts of you are also covered by dragon-like scales. While you aren’t wearing armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Charisma modifiers.
>
