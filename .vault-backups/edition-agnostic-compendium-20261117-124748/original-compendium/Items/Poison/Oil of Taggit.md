# Oil of Taggit

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Oil of Taggit [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Poison/Oil of Taggit|400 GP]]
> *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Poison/Contact|Contact]] Poison*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Oil of Taggit must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 24 hours. The creature also has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition while Poisoned in this way. It wakes up if it takes damage.
>
