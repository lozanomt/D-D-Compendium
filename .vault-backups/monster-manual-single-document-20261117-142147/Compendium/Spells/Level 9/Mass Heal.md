# Mass Heal

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Mass Heal
> *Level 9 Abjuration (Cleric)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A flood of healing energy flows from you into creatures around you. You restore up to 700 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], divided as you choose among any number of creatures that you can see within range. Creatures healed by this spell also have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]], and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] conditions removed from them.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
