## Player's Handbook

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Jack of All Trades
>
> You can add half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus (round down) to any ability check you make that uses a skill proficiency you lack and that doesn't otherwise use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus.
>
> For example, if you make a Strength (Athletics) check and lack Athletics proficiency, you can add half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus to the check.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=32|SRD 5.2.1, p. 32]]
