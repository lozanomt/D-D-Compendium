# Half Plate Armor of Etherealness

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Half Plate Armor of Etherealness
> *Armor (Half Plate Armor), Legendary*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p289-half-plate-armor-of-etherealness.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the armor, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and use a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Command|command]] word to gain the effect of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 7/Etherealness|Etherealness]]. It ends immediately if you remove the armor or take a Magic action to repeat the command word. This property recharges at the next dawn.
>
> The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Book|book]] lists this under **[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/05) Legendary/Plate Armor of Etherealness|Plate Armor of Etherealness]]** and permits either [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Armor/Medium/Half Plate Armor|Half Plate Armor]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Armor/Heavy/Plate Armor|Plate Armor]].
