# Thunderwave

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Thunderwave
> *Level 1 Evocation (Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You unleash a wave of thunderous energy. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] originating from you makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.
>
> In addition, unsecured objects that are entirely within the Cube are pushed 10 feet away from you, and a thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
