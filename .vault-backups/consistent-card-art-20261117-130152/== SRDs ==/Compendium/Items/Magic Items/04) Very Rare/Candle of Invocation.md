# Candle of Invocation

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Candle of Invocation
> *Wondrous Item, Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> Lighting the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Candle|candle]] takes a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and activates its magic. It is destroyed after a total of 4 hours of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Hazards/Burning|burning]]. You can snuff it early for later use; deduct expended time in increments of 1 minute. While lit, it sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] in a 30-foot radius. You and your allies in the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Light|light]] have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on D20 Tests. A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Cleric/Cleric|Cleric]] or Druid in the light can also cast prepared level 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Spells|spells]] without expending spell slots.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Candle of Invocation
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This candle's magic is activated when the candle is lit, which requires a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action. After burning for 4 hours, the candle is destroyed. You can snuff it out early for use at a later time. Deduct the time it burned in increments of 1 minute from its total burn time.
>
> While lit, the candle sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] in a 30-foot radius. While you are within that light, you have
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on D20 Tests. In addition, a Cleric or Druid in the light can cast level 1 spells they have prepared without expending spell slots.
>
> Alternatively, when you light the candle for the first time, you can cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Gate|Gate]] with it. Doing so destroys the candle. The portal created by the spell links to a particular Outer Plane chosen by the GM or determined by rolling on the following table.
>
