# Power Word Stun

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Power Word Stun
> *Level 8 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You overwhelm the mind of one creature you can see within range. If the target has 150 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or fewer, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]] condition. Otherwise, its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is 0 until the start of your next turn.
>
> The Stunned target makes a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on itself on a success.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
