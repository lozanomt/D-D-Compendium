# Sleep

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sleep
> *Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sand or rose petals)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Each creature of your choice in a 5-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on a point within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn, at which point it must repeat the save. If the target fails the second save, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for the duration. The spell ends on a target if it takes damage or someone within 5 feet of it takes an action to shake it out of the spell's effect.
>
> Creatures that don't sleep, such as elves, or that have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] condition automatically succeed on saves against this spell.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
