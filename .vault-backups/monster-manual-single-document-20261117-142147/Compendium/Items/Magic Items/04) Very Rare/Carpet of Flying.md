## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Carpet of Flying
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare You can make this carpet hover and fly by taking a [[Magic|Magic]] action and using the carpet's command word.
>
> It moves according to your directions if you are within 30 feet of it.
>
> Four sizes of Carpet of [[Flying|Flying]] exist. The GM chooses the size of a given carpet or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table. A carpet can carry up to twice the weight shown on the table, but its [[Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] is halved if it carries more than its normal capacity.
