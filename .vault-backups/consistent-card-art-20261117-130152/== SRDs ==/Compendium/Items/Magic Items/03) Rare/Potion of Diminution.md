## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Diminution
>
> Potion, Rare When you drink this potion, you gain the "reduce" effect of the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Enlarge + Reduce|Enlarge/Reduce]] spell for 1d4 hours (no [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] required).
>
> The red in the potion's liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.
>
