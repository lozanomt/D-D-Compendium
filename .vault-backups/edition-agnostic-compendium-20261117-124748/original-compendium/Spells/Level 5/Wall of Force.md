# Wall of Force

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wall of Force
> *Level 5 Evocation (Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a shard of glass)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> An [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] wall of force springs into existence at a point you choose within range. The wall appears in any orientation you choose, as a horizontal or vertical barrier or at an angle. It can be free floating or resting on a solid surface. You can form it into a hemispherical dome or a globe with a radius of up to 10 feet, or you can shape a flat surface made up of ten 10-foot-by-10-foot panels. Each panel must be contiguous with another panel. In any form, the wall is 1/4 inch thick and lasts for the duration. If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature is pushed to one side of the wall (you choose which side).
>
> Nothing can physically pass through the wall. It is immune to all damage and can't be dispelled by *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 3/Dispel Magic|Dispel Magic]]*. A *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 6/Disintegrate|Disintegrate]]* spell destroys the wall instantly, however. The wall also extends into [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Lore/The Ethereal Plane|The Ethereal Plane]] and blocks ethereal travel through the wall.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
