# Wild Companion

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wild Companion
>
> ![[phb-p081-druid.png|320]]
>
> You can summon a nature spirit that assumes an animal form to aid you. As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a spell slot or a use of [[Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] to cast the *[[Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]* spell without Material components. When you cast the spell in this way, the familiar is Fey and disappears when you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=82|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 82]]
>
