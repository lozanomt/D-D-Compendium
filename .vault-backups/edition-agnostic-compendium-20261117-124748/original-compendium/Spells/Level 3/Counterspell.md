# Counterspell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Counterspell
> *Level 3 Abjuration (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you see a creature within 60 feet of yourself casting a spell with Verbal, Somatic, or Material [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Components|components]]  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell. The creature makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the spell dissipates with no effect, and the action, Bonus Action, or Reaction used to cast it is wasted. If that spell was cast with a spell slot, the slot isn't expended.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
