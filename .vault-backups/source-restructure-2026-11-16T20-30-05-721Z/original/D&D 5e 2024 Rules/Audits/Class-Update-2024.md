## Audit corrections

The original Weapon Mastery audit overstated Fighter replacement and misdescribed Rogue eligibility. Rechecking the supplied handbook established:

| Class | Eligibility | Replacement after Long Rest |
|---|---|---|
| Barbarian | Simple or Martial Melee weapons | One choice |
| Fighter | Simple or Martial weapons | One choice |
| Paladin, Ranger, Rogue | Weapons with which the character has proficiency | Chosen weapon kinds |

The shared note and the audit’s SH-02 entry now use these rules.
