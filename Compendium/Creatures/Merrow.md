# Merrow

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Merrow
>
> *Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p212-merrow.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 13  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 45 (6d10 + 12)  
> **Speed:** 10 ft., [[Swimming|Swim]] 40 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 18 | +4 | +4 | **Dex** | 15 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 15 | +2 | +2 |
> | **Int** | 8 | -1 | -1 | **Wis** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 9 | -1 | -1 |
>
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** Abyssal, Primordial (Aquan)  
> **CR:** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Amphibious.*** The merrow can breathe air and water.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The merrow makes two attacks, using Bite, Claw, or Harpoon in any combination.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 4) Piercing damage, and the target has the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of the merrow's next turn.
>
> ***Claw.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) Slashing damage.
>
> ***Harpoon.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, the merrow pulls the target up to 15 feet straight toward itself.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
