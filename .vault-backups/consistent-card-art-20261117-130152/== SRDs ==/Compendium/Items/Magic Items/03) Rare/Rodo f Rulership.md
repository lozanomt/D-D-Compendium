## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Rodo f Rulership
>
> Rod, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) You can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to present the rod and command obedience from each creature of your choice that you can see within 120 feet of yourself. Each target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition for 8 hours. While [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] in this way, the creature regards you as its trusted leader. If harmed by you or your allies or commanded to do something contrary to its nature, a target ceases to be [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] in this way. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
