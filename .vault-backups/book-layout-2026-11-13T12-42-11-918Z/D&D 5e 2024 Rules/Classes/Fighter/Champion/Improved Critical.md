#### Improved Critical
Your attack rolls with weapons and [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]] can score a [[Critical Hit]] on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

***Superior Critical.*** At Fighter level 15, your attack rolls with weapons and Unarmed Strikes can now score a Critical Hit on a roll of 18-20 on the d20.
