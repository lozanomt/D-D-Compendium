# Passive Perception

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Passive Perception
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Passive Perception is a score that reflects a creature’s general awareness of its surroundings. The DM uses this score when determining whether a creature notices something without consciously making a Wisdom ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]]) check.
>
> A creature’s Passive Perception equals 10 plus the creature’s Wisdom (Perception) check bonus. If the creature has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on such checks, increase the score by 5. If the creature has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on them, decrease the score by 5. For example, a level 1 character with a Wisdom of 15 and proficiency in Perception has a Passive Perception of 14 (10 + 2 + 2). If that character has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks, the score becomes 19.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
