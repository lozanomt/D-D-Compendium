# Enhance Ability

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Enhance Ability
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (fur or a feather)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> You touch a creature and choose Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. For the duration, the target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on ability checks using the chosen ability.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2. You can choose a different ability for each target.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
