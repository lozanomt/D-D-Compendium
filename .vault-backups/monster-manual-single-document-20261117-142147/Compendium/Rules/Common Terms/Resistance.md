# Resistance

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Resistance
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have Resistance to a damage type, damage of that type is halved against you (round down). Resistance is applied only once to an instance of damage.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Resistance
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have Resistance to a damage type, damage of that type is halved against you (round down).
>
> Resistance is applied only once to an instance of damage. See also "Playing the Game" ("[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Damage|Damage]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Healing|Healing]]").
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=187|SRD 5.2.1, p. 187]]
