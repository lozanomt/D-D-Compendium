# Challenge Rating

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Challenge Rating
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Challenge Rating|Challenge Rating]] (CR) summarizes the threat a monster poses to a group of four player characters.
>
> Compare a monster's CR to the characters' level. If the CR is higher, the monster is likely a danger. If the
>
> **CR:** is lower, the monster likely poses little threat.  
>
> But circumstances and the number of player characters can significantly alter how threatening a monster is in actual play. "Gameplay Toolbox" ("Combat Encounters") provides guidance to the GM on using
>
> **CR:** while planning potential combat encounters. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Stat Block|Stat Block]]."  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=178|SRD 5.2.1, p. 178]]
