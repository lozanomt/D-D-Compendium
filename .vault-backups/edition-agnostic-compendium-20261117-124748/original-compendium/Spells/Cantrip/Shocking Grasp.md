# Shocking Grasp

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Shocking Grasp
> *Evocation Cantrip (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Lightning springs from you to a creature that you try to touch. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Lightning damage, and it can't make [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] until the start of its next turn.
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
