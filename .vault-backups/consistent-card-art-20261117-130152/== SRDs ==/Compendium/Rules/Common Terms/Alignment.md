# Alignment

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Alignment
>
> A creature's alignment broadly describes its ethical attitudes and ideals. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Alignment|Alignment]] is a combination of two factors: one identifies morality (good, evil, or neutral), and the other describes attitudes toward order (lawful, chaotic, or neutral). These factors allow for nine possible combinations, such as Lawful
>
> Good and Neutral Evil. See also  "Character [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 5/Creation|Creation]]" ("Create Your Character").
>
