# Circle of the Moon

**Class:** [[Classes/Druid/Druid|Druid]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Circle Forms

Wild Shape can use Beasts up to CR equal to one-third your Druid level, rounded down, with the remaining Beast-form restrictions. Your form’s AC can be 13 + Wisdom modifier if higher, and you gain three times your Druid level in Temporary Hit Points.

### Level 3: Circle of the Moon Spells

Always prepare the source’s circle spells and cast them while Wild Shaped: Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp, Conjure Animals, Fount of Moonlight, and Mass Cure Wounds, unlocked at the listed levels.

### Level 6: Improved Circle Forms

Wild Shape attacks can deal Radiant instead of their normal damage. While transformed, add Wisdom modifier to Constitution saving throws.

### Level 10: Moonlight Step

As a Bonus Action, teleport 30 feet to a visible unoccupied space and gain Advantage on your next attack before the turn ends. Uses equal Wisdom modifier, minimum one, per Long Rest; level 2+ slots can restore uses.

### Level 14: Lunar Form

Once per turn, a Wild Shape attack can deal 2d10 extra Radiant damage. Moonlight Step can also transport a willing creature near you to a space near your destination.
