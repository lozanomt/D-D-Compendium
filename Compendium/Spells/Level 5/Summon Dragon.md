# Summon Dragon

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Summon Dragon
> *Level 5 Conjuration (Wizard)*
>
> ![[Attachments/Artwork/phb-p328-summon-dragon.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (an object with the image of a dragon engraved on it worth 500+ GP)  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> You call forth a Dragon spirit. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range and uses the **Draconic Spirit** stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or when the spell ends.
>
> The creature is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the creature shares your [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the [[Compendium/Rules/Actions/Dodge|Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
>
> [[Compendium/Creatures/Draconic Spirit|no-title clean]]
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
