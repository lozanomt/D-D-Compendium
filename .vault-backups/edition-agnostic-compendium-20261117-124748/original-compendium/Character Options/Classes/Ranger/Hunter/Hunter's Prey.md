# Hunter's Prey

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hunter's Prey
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can replace the chosen option with the other one.
>
> ***Colossus Slayer.*** Your tenacity can wear down even the most resilient foes. When you hit a creature with a weapon, the weapon deals an extra 1d8 damage to the target if it’s missing any of its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. You can deal this extra damage only once per turn.
>
> ***Horde Breaker.*** Once on each of your turns when you make an attack with a weapon, you can make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature that is within 5 feet of the original target, that is within the weapon’s range, and that you haven’t attacked this turn.
>
