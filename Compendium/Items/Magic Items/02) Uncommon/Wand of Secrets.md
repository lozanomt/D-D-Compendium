# Wand of Secrets

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Wand of Secrets [[Wand of Secrets|(400 GP)]]
> *Wand, Uncommon*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p325-wand-of-secrets.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This wand has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to expend 1 charge, and if a secret door or trap is within 60 feet of you, the wand pulses and points at the one nearest to you.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
