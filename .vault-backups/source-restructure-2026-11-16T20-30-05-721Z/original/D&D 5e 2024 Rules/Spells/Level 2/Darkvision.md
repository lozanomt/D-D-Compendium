# Darkvision
*Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Placeholder.png|320]]


**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a dried carrot)
**Duration:** 8 hours

For the duration, a willing creature you touch has [[Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] with a range of 150 feet.
