# Dretch

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Dretch
>
> *Small Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p106-dretch.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 11  
> **Initiative:** +0 (10)  
> **HP:** 18 (4d6 + 4)  
> **Speed:** 20 ft.  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

>
> **Resistances:** Cold, Fire, Lightning  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 9  
> **Languages:** Abyssal; [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Telepathy|Telepathy]] 60 ft. (works only with creatures that understand Abyssal)  
> **CR:** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)  
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +3, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) Slashing damage.
>
> ***Fetid Cloud (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 11, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the dretch. *Failure:* The target has the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of its next turn. While Poisoned, the creature can take either an action or a Bonus Action on its turn, not both, and it can't take Reactions.
