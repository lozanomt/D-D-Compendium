# Ring of Mind Shielding

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Mind Shielding
> *Ring, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p296-ring-of-mind-shielding.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the ring, you are immune to [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|magic]] that reads your thoughts, determines whether you are lying, or reveals your alignment or creature type. Other creatures can communicate telepathically with you only if you allow it.
>
> Take a Magic action to make the ring imperceptible. It remains so until you take another Magic action to make it perceptible, remove it, or die.
>
> If you die while wearing the ring, your soul enters it unless it already contains a soul. You can stay in the ring or depart for the afterlife. While in it, you can communicate telepathically with any wearer, who cannot block this communication.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Mind Shielding
>
> Ring, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this ring, you are immune to magic that allows other creatures to read your thoughts, determine whether you are lying, know your alignment, or know your creature type. Creatures can telepathically communicate with you only if you allow it.
>
> You can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to cause the ring to become imperceptible until you take another [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to make it perceptible, until you remove the ring, or until you die.
>
> If you die while wearing the ring, your soul enters it, unless it already houses a soul. You can remain in the ring or depart for the afterlife. As long as your soul is in the ring, you can telepathically communicate with any creature wearing it. A wearer can't prevent this telepathic communication.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=238|SRD 5.2.1, p. 238]]
