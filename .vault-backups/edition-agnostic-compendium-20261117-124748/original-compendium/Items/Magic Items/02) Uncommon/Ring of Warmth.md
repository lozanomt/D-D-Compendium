# Ring of Warmth

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Warmth
> *Ring, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p299-ring-of-warmth.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Warmth
>
> Ring, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) If you take Cold damage while wearing this ring, the ring reduces the damage you take by 2d8.
>
> In addition, while wearing this ring, you and everything you wear and carry are unharmed by temperatures of 0 degrees Fahrenheit or lower.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=239|SRD 5.2.1, p. 239]]
