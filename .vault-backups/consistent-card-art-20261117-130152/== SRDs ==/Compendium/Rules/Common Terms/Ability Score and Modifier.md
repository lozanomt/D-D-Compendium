# Ability Score and Modifier

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ability Score and Modifier
>
> A creature has six ability scores—Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma—each of which has a corresponding modifier. Add the modifier when you make a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]] with the corresponding ability or when a rule asks you to do so. See also "Playing the Game" ("The Six Abilities").
>
