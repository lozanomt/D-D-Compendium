# Proficiency

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Proficiency
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have proficiency with something, you can add your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus to any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]] you make using that thing. A creature might have proficiency in a skill or saving throw or with a weapon or tool.
>
> See also "Playing the Game" ("[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]]").
