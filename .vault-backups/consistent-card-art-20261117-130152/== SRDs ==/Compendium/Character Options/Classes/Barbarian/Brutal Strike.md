# Brutal Strike

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Brutal Strike
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p053-barbarian.png|320]]
>
> If you use Reckless Attack, you can forgo any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on one Strength-based attack roll of your choice on your turn. The chosen attack roll mustn’t have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]. If the chosen attack roll hits, the target takes an extra 1d10 damage of the same type dealt by the weapon or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]], and you can cause one Brutal Strike effect of your choice. You have the following effect options.
>
> ***Forceful Blow.*** The target is pushed 15 feet straight away from you. You can then move up to half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] straight toward the target without provoking [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].
>
> ***Hamstring Blow.*** The target’s Speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of your next turn. A target can be affected by only one Hamstring Blow at a time - the most recent one.
>
> At Barbarian level 13, you have honed new ways to attack furiously. The following effects are now among your Brutal Strike options.
>
> ***Staggering Blow.*** The target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the next saving throw it makes, and it can’t make [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] until the start of your next turn.
>
> ***Sundering Blow.*** Before the start of your next turn, the next attack roll made by another creature against the target gains a +5 bonus to the roll. An attack roll can gain only one Sundering Blow bonus.
>
> At Barbarian level 17, the extra damage of your Brutal Strike increases to 2d10. In addition, you can use two different Brutal Strike effects whenever you use your Brutal Strike feature.
>
