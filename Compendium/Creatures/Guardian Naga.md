# Guardian Naga

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Guardian Naga
>
> *Large Celestial, Lawful Good*
>
> ![[mm-p163-guardian-naga.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 18  
> **Initiative:** +4 (14)  
> **HP:** 136 (16d10 + 48)  
> **Speed:** 40 ft., [[Climbing|Climb]] 40 ft., [[Swimming|Swim]] 40 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 19 | +4 | +4 | **Dex** | 18 | +4 | +8 | **Con** | 16 | +3 | +7 |
> | **Int** | 16 | +3 | +7 | **Wis** | 19 | +4 | +8 | **Cha** | 18 | +4 | +8 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Arcana|Arcana]] +11, [[History|History]] +11, [[Religion|Religion]] +11  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[Charmed|Charmed]], [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[Poisoned|Poisoned]], [[Restrained|Restrained]]  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 14  
> **Languages:** Celestial, Common  
> **CR:** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Celestial Restoration.*** If the naga dies, it returns to life in 1d6 days and regains all its Hit Points unless *Dispel Evil and Good* is cast on its remains.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The naga makes two Bite attacks. It can replace any attack with a use of Poisonous Spittle.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d12 + 4) Piercing damage plus 22 (4d10) Poison damage.
>
> ***Poisonous Spittle.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the naga can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* 31 (7d8) Poison damage, and the target has the [[Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the start of the naga's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
> ***Spellcasting.*** The naga casts one of the following spells, requiring no Somatic or Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):
>
> - **At Will:** *Thaumaturgy*
> - **1/Day Each:** *Clairvoyance*, *Cure Wounds* (level 6 version), *Flame Strike* (level 6 version), *Geas*, *True Seeing*
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
