# Broom of Flying

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Broom of Flying
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p244-bracers-of-defense.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> Stand astride this wooden broom and take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to make it [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hover|hover]] beneath you. It has a Fly [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] of 50 feet and can carry up to 400 pounds; its Fly Speed becomes 30 feet while carrying more than 200 pounds. It stops hovering when you land or are no longer riding it.
>
> As a Magic action, send the broom to a destination within 1 mile by naming a location you are familiar with. If the broom is still within 1 mile, another Magic action and a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Command|command]] word call it back to you.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Broom of Flying
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This wooden broom functions like a mundane broom until you stand astride it and take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to make it hover beneath you, at which time it can be ridden in the air. It has a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] of 50 feet. It can carry up to 400 pounds, but its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] becomes 30 feet while carrying over 200 pounds.
>
> The broom stops hovering when you land or when you're no longer riding it.
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can send the broom to travel alone to a destination within 1 mile of you if you name the location and are familiar with it. The broom comes back to you when you take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and use a command word if the broom is still within 1 mile of you.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=214|SRD 5.2.1, p. 214]]
