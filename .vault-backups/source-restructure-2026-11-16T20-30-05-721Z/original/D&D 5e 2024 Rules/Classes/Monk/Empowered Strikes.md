# Empowered Strikes

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p103-monk.png|320]]

Whenever you deal damage with your [[Unarmed Strike]], it can deal your choice of Force damage or its normal damage type.
