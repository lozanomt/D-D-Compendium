# Invisible

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Invisible
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Invisible condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Surprise.*** If you're Invisible when you roll [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], you have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on the roll.
>
> ***Concealed.*** You aren't affected by any effect that requires its target to be seen unless the effect's creator can somehow see you. Any equipment you are wearing or carrying is also concealed.
>
> ***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]], and your attack rolls have Advantage. If a creature can somehow see you, you don't gain this benefit against that creature.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
