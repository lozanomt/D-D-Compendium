## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Crystal Ball of Telepathy
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While touching this crystal orb, you can cast [[Scrying|Scrying]] (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you can communicate telepathically with creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You can also cast [[Suggestion|Suggestion]] (save DC 17) through the sensor on one of those creatures. You don't need to concentrate on this [[Suggestion|Suggestion]]  to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if [[Scrying|Scrying]] ends. You can't cast [[Suggestion|Suggestion]] in this way again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=216|SRD 5.2.1, p. 216]]
