# Flying

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Flying
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Fly Speed can be used to travel through the air. While you have a Fly Speed, you can stay aloft until you land, [[Falling|fall]], or [[Dead|die]].
>
> A variety of effects allow a creature to fly. While flying, you fall if you have the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or [[Prone|Prone]] condition or your Fly Speed is reduced to 0. You can stay aloft in those circumstances if you can [[Hover|Hover]].
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
