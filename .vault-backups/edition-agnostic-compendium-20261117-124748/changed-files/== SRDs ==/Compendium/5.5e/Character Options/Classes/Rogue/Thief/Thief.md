# Thief

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Thief
>
> *Hunt for Treasure as a Classic Adventurer*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p140-thief.png|320]]
>
> A mix of burglar, treasure hunter, and explorer, you are the epitome of an adventurer. In addition to improving your agility and stealth, you gain abilities useful for delving into ruins and getting maximum benefit from the magic items you find there.
> #### Level 3: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Fast Hands|Fast Hands]]
> As a Bonus Action, you can do one of the following.
>
> ***Sleight of Hand.*** Make a Dexterity ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Sleight of Hand|Sleight of Hand]]) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Tools/Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] or to pick a pocket.
>
> ***Use an Object.*** Take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, or take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to use a magic item that requires that action.
> #### Level 3: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Second-Story Work|Second-Story Work]]
> You’ve trained to get into especially hard-to-reach places, granting you these benefits.
>
> ***Climber.*** You gain a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Climbing|Climb]] Speed equal to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]].
>
> ***Jumper.*** You can determine your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Jumping|jump]] distance using your Dexterity rather than your Strength.
> #### Level 9: Supreme Sneak
> You gain the following Cunning Strike option.
>
> **Stealth:** Attack (Cost: 1d6).*** If you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]] action’s [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition, this attack doesn’t end that condition on you if you end the turn behind [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Three-Quarters Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Total Cover|Total Cover]].  
> #### Level 13: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Use Magic Device|Use Magic Device]]
> You’ve learned how to maximize use of magic items, granting you the following benefits.
>
> ***Attunement.*** You can attune to up to four magic items at once.
>
> ***Charges.*** Whenever you use a magic item property that expends charges, roll 1d6. On a roll of 6, you use the property without expending the charges.
>
> ***Scrolls.*** You can use any *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/Spell Scroll/Spell Scroll|Spell Scroll]]*, using Intelligence as your spellcasting ability for the spell. If the spell is a cantrip or a level 1 spell, you can cast it reliably. If the scroll contains a higher-level spell, you must first succeed on an Intelligence ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Arcana|Arcana]]) check (DC 10 plus the spell’s level). On a successful check, you cast the spell from the scroll. On a failed check, the scroll disintegrates.
> #### Level 17: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Thief's Reflexes|Thief's Reflexes]]
> You are adept at laying ambushes and quickly escaping danger. You can take two turns during the first round of any combat. You take your first turn at your normal [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] and your second turn at your Initiative minus 10.
>
