## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Robe of the Archmagi
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]] by a Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard) This elegant garment is made from exquisite cloth and adorned with runes.
>
> You gain these benefits while wearing the robe.
>
> Armor. If you aren't wearing armor, your base [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]] is 15 plus your Dexterity modifier.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] Resistance. You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> War Mage. Your spell save DC and spell attack bonus each increase by 2.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=240|SRD 5.2.1, p. 240]]
