# Armor Training

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Armor Training
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Armor training allows you to use armor of a certain category without the following drawbacks. If you wear Light, Medium, or [[Heavy|Heavy]] armor and lack training with it, you have [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on any [[D20 Test|D20 Test]] that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can't cast spells. If you use a [[Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] and lack training with it, you don't gain its AC bonus. See also  "[[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]" and "Equipment" ("Armor").
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=177|SRD 5.2.1, p. 177]]
