# Gift of the Depths

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Gift of the Depths
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock*  
>
> ![[phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You can breathe underwater, and you gain a [[Swimming|Swim]] Speed equal to your [[Speed|Speed]].
>
> You can also cast *[[Water Breathing|Water Breathing]]* once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=156|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 156]]
>
