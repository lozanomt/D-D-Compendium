# Climber's Kit

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Climber's Kit [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Climber's Kit|(25 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Climber’s Kit includes boot tips, gloves, pitons, and a harness. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can use the Climber’s Kit to anchor yourself; when you do, you can’t fall more than 25 feet from the anchor point, and you can’t move more than 25 feet from there without undoing the anchor as a Bonus Action.
>
> **Weight:** 12 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
