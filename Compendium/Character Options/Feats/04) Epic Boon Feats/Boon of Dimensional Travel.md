# Boon of Dimensional Travel

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Boon of Dimensional Travel
> *Epic Boon Feat (Prerequisite: Level 19+)*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You gain the following benefits.
>
> ***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.
>
> ***Blink Steps.*** Immediately after you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action or the [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
