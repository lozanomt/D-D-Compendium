# Long Rest

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Long Rest
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Long Rest is a period of extended downtime - at least 8 hours - available to any creature. During a Long Rest, you sleep for at least 6 hours and perform no more than 2 hours of light activity, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch. During sleep, you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition. After you finish a Long Rest, you must wait at least 16 hours before starting another one. To start a Long Rest, you must have at least 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]].
>
> ***Benefits of the Rest.*** When you finish the rest, you gain the following benefits:
>
>    **Regain All HP.** You regain all lost Hit Points and all spent [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Dice]]. If your Hit Point maximum was reduced, it returns to normal.
>
> **Ability Scores:** Restored.** If any of your ability scores were reduced, they return to normal.  
>
>    **Exhaustion Reduced.** If you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] condition, its level decreases by 1.
>
>    **Special Feature.** Some features are recharged by a Long Rest. If you have such a feature, it recharges in the way specified in its description.
>
> ***Interrupting the Rest.*** A Long Rest is stopped by the following interruptions:
> - Rolling [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]]
> - Casting a spell other than a cantrip
> - Taking any damage
> - 1 hour of walking or other physical exertion
>
> If you rested at least 1 hour before the interruption, you gain the benefits of a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]].
>
> You can resume a Long Rest immediately after an interruption. If you do so, the rest requires 1 additional hour per interruption to finish.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
