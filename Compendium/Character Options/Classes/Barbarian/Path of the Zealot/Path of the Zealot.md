# Path of the Zealot

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Path of the Zealot
>
> ![[phb-p060-path-of-the-zealot.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Barbarian|Barbarian]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Divine Fury
>
> While raging, the first creature hit on each of your turns by a weapon or Unarmed Strike takes extra Necrotic or Radiant damage: 1d6 + half your Barbarian level, rounded down.
>
> #### Level 3: Warrior of the Gods
>
> A pool of four d12s lets you heal yourself with a Bonus Action, spending and rolling chosen dice. Recover the pool on a Long Rest; its size increases at Barbarian levels 6, 12, and 17.
>
> #### Level 6: Fanatical Focus
>
> Once per Rage, reroll a failed saving throw with your Rage Damage bonus added; use the new roll.
>
> #### Level 10: Zealous Presence
>
> A Bonus Action gives up to ten other creatures within 60 feet Advantage on attacks and saving throws until your next turn starts. Refresh on a Long Rest, or expend a Rage use to restore it.
>
> #### Level 14: Rage of the Gods
>
> Once per Long Rest, entering Rage can grant a one-minute divine form, ending at 0 Hit Points. Gain hovering flight and Resistance to Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant damage. A Reaction and a Rage use can prevent a nearby creature from dropping to 0; consult the source for range and timing.
>
