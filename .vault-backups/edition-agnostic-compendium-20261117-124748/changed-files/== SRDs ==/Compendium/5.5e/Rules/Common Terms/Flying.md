# Flying

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Flying
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Fly Speed can be used to travel through the air. While you have a Fly Speed, you can stay aloft until you land, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Hazards/Falling|fall]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dead|die]].
>
> A variety of effects allow a creature to fly. While flying, you fall if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition or your Fly Speed is reduced to 0. You can stay aloft in those circumstances if you can [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hover|Hover]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Flying
>
> A variety of effects allow a creature to fly. While flying, you fall if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition or your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] is reduced to 0. You can stay aloft in those circumstances if you can hover.
>
> See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Hazards/Falling|Falling]]" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]]."
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=182|SRD 5.2.1, p. 182]]
