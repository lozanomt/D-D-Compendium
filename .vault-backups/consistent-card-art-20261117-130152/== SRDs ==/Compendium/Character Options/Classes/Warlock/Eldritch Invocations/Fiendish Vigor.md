# Fiendish Vigor

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fiendish Vigor
> **Prerequisite:** Level 2+ Warlock*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You can cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/False Life|False Life]]* on yourself without expending a spell slot. When you cast the spell with this feature, you don’t roll the die for the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]]; you automatically get the highest number on the die.
>
