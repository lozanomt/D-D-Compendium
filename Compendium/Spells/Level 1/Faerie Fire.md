# Faerie Fire

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Faerie Fire
> *Level 1 Evocation (Bard, Druid)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Objects in a 20-foot [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] within range are outlined in blue, green, or violet light (your choice). Each creature in the Cube is also outlined if it fails a Dexterity saving throw. For the duration, objects and affected creatures shed [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> Attack rolls against an affected creature or object have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] if the attacker can see it.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
