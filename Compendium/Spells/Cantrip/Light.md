# Light

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Light
> *Evocation Cantrip (Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, M (a firefly or phosphorescent moss)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> You touch one Large or smaller object that isn't being worn or carried by someone else. Until the spell ends, the object sheds [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. The light can be colored as you like.
>
> Covering the object with something opaque blocks the light. The spell ends if you cast it again.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
