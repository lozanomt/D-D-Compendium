# Scorching Ray

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Scorching Ray
> *Level 2 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You hurl three fiery rays. You can hurl them at one target within range or at several. Make a ranged spell attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 Fire damage.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You create one additional ray for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
