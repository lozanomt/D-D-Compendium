# Ring of Spell Turning

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Spell Turning
> *Ring, Legendary*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the ring, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Spells|spells]]. If you succeed on the save against a spell of level 7 or lower, that spell has no effect on you. If it targeted only you and did not create an area of effect, you can take a Reaction to deflect it back at its caster. The caster must save against the spell using their own spell save DC.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Spell Turning
>
> Ring, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this ring, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells. If you succeed on the save for a spell of level 7 or lower, the spell has no effect on you. If that spell targeted only you and didn't create an area of effect, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] to deflect the spell back at the spell's caster; the caster must make a saving throw against the spell using their own spell save DC.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=239|SRD 5.2.1, p. 239]]
