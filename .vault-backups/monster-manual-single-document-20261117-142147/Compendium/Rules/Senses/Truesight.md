# Truesight

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Truesight
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have Truesight, your vision is enhanced within a specified range. Within that range, your vision pierces through the following:
>
> **Darkness.** You can see in normal and magical [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Darkness|Darkness]].
>
> **Invisibility.** You see creatures and objects that have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> **Visual Illusions.** Visual illusions appear transparent to you, and you automatically succeed on saving throws against them.
>
> **Transformations.** You discern the true form of any creature or object you see that has been transformed by magic.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Truesight
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]], your vision is enhanced within a specified range. Within that range, your vision pierces through the following:
>
> Darkness. You can see in normal and magical Darkness.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Invisibility|Invisibility]]. You see creatures and objects that have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> Visual [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Illusions|Illusions]]. Visual illusions appear transparent to you, and you automatically succeed on saving throws against them.
>
> Transformations.  You discern the true form of any creature or object you see that has been transformed by magic.
>
> Ethereal Plane.  You see into the Ethereal Plane.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=190|SRD 5.2.1, p. 190]]
