# Cunning Strike

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Cunning Strike
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]
>
> You’ve developed cunning ways to use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]]. When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can add one of the following Cunning Strike effects. Each effect has a die cost, which is the number of Sneak Attack damage dice you must forgo to add the effect. You remove the die before rolling, and the effect occurs immediately after the attack’s damage is dealt. For example, if you add the Poison effect, remove 1d6 from the Sneak Attack’s damage before rolling.
>
> If a Cunning Strike effect requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus.
>
> - **Poison (Cost: 1d6).** You add a toxin to your strike, forcing the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the Poisoned target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success. To use this effect, you must have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Tools/Poisoner's Kit|Poisoner's Kit]] on your person.
> - **Trip (Cost: 1d6).** If the target is Large or smaller, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
> - **Withdraw (Cost: 1d6).** Immediately after the attack, you move up to half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] without provoking [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].
>
> ***Supreme Sneak.*** At Rogue level 9, if you are a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Thief/Thief|Thief]], you gain the following Cunning Strike option.
> - ***Stealth Attack (Cost: 1d6).*** If you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]] action’s [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition, this attack doesn’t end that condition on you if you end the turn behind [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Three-Quarters Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Total Cover|Total Cover]].
>
> ***Improved Cunning Strike.*** At Rogue level 11, you can use up to two Cunning Strike effects when you deal Sneak Attack damage, paying the die cost for each effect.
>
> ***Devious Strikes.*** At Rogue level 14, you’ve practiced new ways to use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Sneak Attack|Sneak Attack]] deviously. The following effects are now among your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Cunning Strike|Cunning Strike]] options.
>
> - **Daze (Cost: 2d6).** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or on its next turn, it can do only one of the following: move or take an action or a Bonus Action.
> - **Knock Out (Cost: 6d6).** The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for 1 minute or until it takes any damage. The Unconscious target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
> - **Obscure (Cost: 3d6).** The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
>
