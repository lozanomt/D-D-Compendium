# Deep Water

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Deep Water
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Swimming through deep water (more than 100 feet deep) presents additional challenges because of the water’s pressure and cold temperature. After each hour of swimming in deep water, a creature that lacks a [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
