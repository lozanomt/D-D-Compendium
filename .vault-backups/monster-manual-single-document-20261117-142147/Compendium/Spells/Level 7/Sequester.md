# Sequester

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sequester
>
> *Level 7 Transmutation (Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (gem dust worth 5,000+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** Until dispelled  
>
> With a touch, you magically sequester an object or a willing creature. For the duration, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition and can't be targeted by Divination spells, detected by magic, or viewed remotely with magic.
>
> If the target is a creature, it enters a state of suspended animation; it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition, doesn't age, and doesn't need food, water, or air.
>
> You can set a condition for the spell to end early. The condition can be anything you choose, but it must occur or be visible within 1 mile of the target. Examples include "after 1,000 years" or "when the tarrasque awakens." This spell also ends if the target takes any damage.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
