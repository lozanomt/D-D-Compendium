## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Elixir of Health
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Potion, Rare When you drink this potion, you are cured of all magical contagions. In addition, the following conditions end on you: [[Blinded|Blinded]], [[Deafened|Deafened]], [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], and [[Poisoned|Poisoned]].
>
> The clear, red liquid has tiny bubbles of light in it.
>
