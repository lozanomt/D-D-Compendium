# Heroic Inspiration

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Heroic Inspiration
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Sometimes the DM or a rule gives you Heroic Inspiration. If you have Heroic Inspiration, you can expend it to reroll any die immediately after rolling it, and you must use the new roll.
>
> ***Only One at a Time.*** You can never have more than one instance of Heroic Inspiration. If something gives you Heroic Inspiration and you already have it, you can give it to a player character in your group who lacks it.
>
> ***Gaining Heroic Inspiration.*** Your DM can give you Heroic Inspiration for a variety of reasons. Typically, DMs award it when you do something particularly heroic, in character, or entertaining. It’s a reward for making the game more fun for everyone playing.
>
> Other rules might allow your character to gain Heroic Inspiration independent of the DM’s decision. For example, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Species/Human|Human]] characters start each day with Heroic Inspiration.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Heroic Inspiration
>
> If you (a player character) have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heroic Inspiration|Heroic Inspiration]], you can expend it to reroll any die immediately after rolling it, and you must use the new roll.
>
> If you gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heroic Inspiration|Heroic Inspiration]] but already have it, it's lost unless you give it to a player character who lacks it.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=183|SRD 5.2.1, p. 183]]
