# Hurl Through Hell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hurl Through Hell
>
> ![[phb-p165-fiend-patron.png|320]]
>
> Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack roll, you can try to instantly transport the target through the Lower Planes. The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC, or the target disappears and hurtles through a nightmare landscape. The target takes 8d10 Psychic damage if it isn’t a Fiend, and it has the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition until the end of your next turn, when it returns to the space it previously occupied or the nearest unoccupied space.
>
> Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]] unless you expend a [[Pact Magic|Pact Magic]] spell slot (no action required) to restore your use of it.
>
