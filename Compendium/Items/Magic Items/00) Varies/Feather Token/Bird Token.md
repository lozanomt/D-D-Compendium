# Bird Token

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Bird Token [[Bird Token|(2,000 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This object looks like a feather. You can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to toss the token 5 feet into the air. The token disappears and an enormous, multicolored bird takes its place. The bird has the statistics of a [[Roc|Roc]], but it can’t attack. It obeys your simple commands and can carry up to 500 pounds while flying at its maximum speed (16 miles per hour for a maximum of 144 miles per day, with a 1-hour rest for every 3 hours of flying) or 1,000 pounds at half that speed. The bird disappears after flying its maximum distance for a day or if it drops to 0 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. You can dismiss the bird as a Magic action.
