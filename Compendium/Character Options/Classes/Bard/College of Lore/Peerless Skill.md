# Peerless Skill

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Peerless Skill
>
> ![[phb-p069-college-of-lore.png|320]]
>
> When you make an ability check or attack roll and fail, you can expend one use of [[Bardic Inspiration|Bardic Inspiration]]; roll the Bardic Inspiration die, and add the number rolled to the d20, potentially turning a failure into a success. On a failure, the Bardic Inspiration isn’t expended.
>
