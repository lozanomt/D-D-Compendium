# Augury

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Augury
> *Level 2 Divination (Cleric, Druid, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (specially marked sticks, bones, cards, or other divinatory tokens worth 25+ GP)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You receive an omen from an otherworldly entity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The GM chooses the omen from the table.
>
> | Omen         | For Results That Will Be …   |
> |--------------|------------------------------|
> | Weal         | Good                         |
> | Woe          | Bad                          |
> | Weal and woe | Good and bad                 |
> | Indifference | Neither good nor bad         |
>
> The spell doesn't account for circumstances, such as other spells, that might change the results.
>
> If you cast the spell more than once before finishing a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
