# Power Word Kill

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Power Word Kill
> *Level 9 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You compel one creature you can see within range to die. If the target has 100 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes 12d12 Psychic damage.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
