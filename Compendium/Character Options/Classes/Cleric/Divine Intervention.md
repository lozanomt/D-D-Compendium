# Divine Intervention

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Divine Intervention
>
> ![[phb-p071-cleric.png|320]]
>
> You can call on your deity or pantheon to intervene on your behalf. As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, choose any Cleric spell of level 5 or lower that doesn’t require a Reaction to cast. As part of the same action, you cast that spell without expending a spell slot or needing Material [[Components|components]]. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> At Cleric level 20, you can call on even more powerful divine intervention. When you use your [[Divine Intervention|Divine Intervention]] feature, you can choose *[[Wish|Wish]]* when you select a spell. If you do so, you can't use Divine Intervention again until you finish 2d4 [[Long Rest|Long Rests]].
>
