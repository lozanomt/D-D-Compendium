# Oil of Taggit

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Oil of Taggit [[Oil of Taggit|400 GP]]
> *[[Contact|Contact]] Poison*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Oil of Taggit must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 24 hours. The creature also has the [[Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition while Poisoned in this way. It wakes up if it takes damage.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
