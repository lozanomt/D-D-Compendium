# Fly Speed

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Fly Speed
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] can be used to travel through the air.
>
> While you have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]], you can stay aloft until you land, fall, or die. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Flying]]" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]]."
>
