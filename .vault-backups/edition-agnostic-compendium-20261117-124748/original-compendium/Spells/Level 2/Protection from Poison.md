# Protection from Poison

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Protection from Poison
> *Level 2 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> You touch a creature and end the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition on it. For the duration, the target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition, and it has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Poison damage.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
