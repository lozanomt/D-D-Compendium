# Strong Wind

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Strong Wind
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Strong wind imposes [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on ranged attack rolls with weapons. It also extinguishes open flames and disperses fog. A flying creature in a strong wind must land at the end of its turn or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Hazards/Falling|fall]]. A strong wind in a desert can create a sandstorm that imposes Disadvantage on Wisdom ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]]) checks.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
