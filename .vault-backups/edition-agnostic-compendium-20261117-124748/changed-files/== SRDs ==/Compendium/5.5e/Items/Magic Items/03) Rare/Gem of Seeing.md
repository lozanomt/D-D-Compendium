## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Gem of Seeing
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]])
>
> This gem has 3 charges. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend 1 charge. For the next 10 minutes, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]] out to 120 feet when you peer through the gem.
>
> The gem regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
>
