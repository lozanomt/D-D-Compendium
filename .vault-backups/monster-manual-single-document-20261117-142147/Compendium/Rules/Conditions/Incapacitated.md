# Incapacitated

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Incapacitated
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Incapacitated condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Inactive.*** You can't take any action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.
>
> ***No Concentration.*** Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] is broken.
>
> ***Speechless.*** You can't speak.
>
> ***Surprised.*** If you're Incapacitated when you roll [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the roll.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
