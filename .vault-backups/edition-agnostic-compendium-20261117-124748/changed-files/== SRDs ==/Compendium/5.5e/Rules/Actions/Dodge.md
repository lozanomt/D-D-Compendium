# Dodge

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dodge
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]].
>
> You lose these benefits if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition or if your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is 0.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
