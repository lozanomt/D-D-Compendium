# Daylight

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Daylight
> *Level 3 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer)*
>
> ![[Attachments/Artwork/phb-p263-daylight.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> For the duration, sunlight spreads from a point within range and fills a 60-foot-radius [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]]. The sunlight's area is [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] and sheds [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 60 feet.
>
> Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the sunlight to fill a 60-foot [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the sunlight.
>
> If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Darkness|Darkness]] created by a spell of level 3 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
