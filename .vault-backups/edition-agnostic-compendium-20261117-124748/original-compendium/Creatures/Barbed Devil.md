# Barbed Devil

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Barbed Devil
>
> *Medium Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p032-barbed-devil.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 15  
> **Initiative:** +3 (13)  
> **HP:** 110 (13d8 + 52)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Climbing|Climb]] 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 16 | +3 | +6 | **Dex** | 17 | +3 | +3 | **Con** | 18 | +4 | +7 |
> | **Int** | 12 | +1 | +1 | **Wis** | 14 | +2 | +5 | **Cha** | 14 | +2 | +5 |
>
> **Skills:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Deception|Deception]] +5, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Insight|Insight]] +5, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] +8  
> **Resistances:** Cold  
> **Immunities:** Fire, Poison; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 120 ft. (unimpeded by magical Darkness); [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 18  
> **Languages:** Infernal; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Telepathy|Telepathy]] 120 ft.  
> **CR:** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Barbed Hide.*** At the start of each of its turns, the devil deals 5 (1d10) Piercing damage to any creature it is grappling or any creature grappling it.
>
> ***Diabolical Restoration.*** If the devil dies outside the Nine Hells, its body disappears in sulfurous smoke, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Nine Hells.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The devil has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The devil makes one Claws attack and one Tail attack, or it makes two Hurl Flame attacks.
>
> ***Claws.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) from both claws.
>
> ***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d10 + 3) Slashing damage.
>
> ***Hurl Flame.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 150 ft. *Hit:* 17 (5d6) Fire damage. If the target is a flammable object that isn't being worn or carried, it starts burning.
>
