# Air Elemental

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Air Elemental
>
> *Large Elemental, Neutral*
>
> ![[mm-p015-air-elemental.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 15  
> **Initiative:** +5 (15)  
> **HP:** 90 (12d10 + 24)  
> **Speed:** 10 ft., [[Flying|Fly]] 90 ft. (hover)  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Dex** | 20 | +5 | +5 | **Con** | 14 | +2 | +2 |
> | **Int** | 6 | -2 | -2 | **Wis** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 6 | -2 | -2 |
>
> **Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Lightning, Piercing, Slashing  
> **Immunities:** Poison, Thunder; [[Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[Grappled|Grappled]], [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[Petrified|Petrified]], [[Poisoned|Poisoned]], [[Prone|Prone]], [[Restrained|Restrained]], [[Unconscious|Unconscious]]  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** Primordial (Auran)  
> **CR:** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Air Form.*** The elemental can enter a creature's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The elemental makes two Thunderous Slam attacks.
>
> ***Thunderous Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) Thunder damage.
>
> ***Whirlwind (Recharge 4-6).*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Medium or smaller creature in the elemental's space. *Failure:* 24 (4d10 + 2) Thunder damage, and the target is pushed up to 20 feet straight away from the elemental and has the [[Prone|Prone]] condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
