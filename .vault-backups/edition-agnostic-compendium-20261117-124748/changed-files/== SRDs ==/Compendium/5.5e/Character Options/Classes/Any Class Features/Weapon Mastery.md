# Weapon Mastery

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Weapon Mastery
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Weapon Mastery depends on the class that grants it. Use the class’s own progression table for the number of mastered weapon kinds.
>
> | Class | Initial choices | Eligible weapons | Replacement after a Long Rest |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Barbarian\|Barbarian]] | 2 | Simple or Martial Melee weapons | Change one choice |
> | [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Fighter/Fighter\|Fighter]] | 3 | Simple or Martial weapons | Change one choice |
> | [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Paladin\|Paladin]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
> | [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Ranger/Ranger\|Ranger]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
> | [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Rogue/Rogue\|Rogue]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
>
> Barbarian and Fighter gain more choices at the levels shown in their class tables. Ranged weapons are eligible where the class’s restrictions allow them.
