# Primal Knowledge

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Primal Knowledge
>
> ![[phb-p053-barbarian.png|320]]
>
> You gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the skill list available to Barbarians at level 1.
>
> In addition, while your Rage is active, you can channel primal power when you attempt certain tasks; whenever you make an ability check using one of the following skills, you can make it as a Strength check even if it normally uses a different ability: [[Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[Perception|Perception]], [[Stealth|Stealth]], or [[Survival|Survival]]. When you use this ability, your Strength represents primal power coursing through you, honing your agility, bearing, and senses.
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=54|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 54]]
>
