# Hold Monster

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hold Monster
> *Level 5 Enchantment (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 90 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Choose a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 5.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
