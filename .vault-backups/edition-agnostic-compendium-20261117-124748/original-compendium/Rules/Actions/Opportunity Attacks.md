# Opportunity Attacks

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Opportunity Attacks
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You can make an Opportunity Attack when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its action, its Bonus Action, its Reaction, or one of its speeds. To make the Opportunity Attack, take a Reaction to make one melee attack with a weapon or an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Opportunity Attacks
>
> You can make an Opportunity [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its action, its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]], its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]], or one of its speeds. To make the Opportunity [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]], take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] to make one melee attack with a weapon or an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] against the provoking creature.
>
> The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach. See also "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=185|SRD 5.2.1, p. 185]]
