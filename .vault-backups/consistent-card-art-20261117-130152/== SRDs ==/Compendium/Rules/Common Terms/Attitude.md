# Attitude

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Attitude
>
> A monster has a starting attitude toward a player character: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Friendly|Friendly]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hostile|Hostile]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Indifferent|Indifferent]]. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Friendly|Friendly]]," "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hostile|Hostile]]," "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Indifferent|Indifferent]]," and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Influence|Influence]]."
>
