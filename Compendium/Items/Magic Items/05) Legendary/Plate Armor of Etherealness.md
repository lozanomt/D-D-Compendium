# Plate Armor of Etherealness

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Plate Armor of Etherealness
> *Armor (Plate Armor), Legendary*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p289-half-plate-armor-of-etherealness.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the armor, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action and use a [[Command|command]] word to gain the effect of [[Etherealness|Etherealness]]. It ends immediately if you remove the armor or take a Magic action to repeat the command word. This property recharges at the next dawn.
>
> The [[Book|book]] lists this under **Plate Armor of Etherealness** and permits either [[Half Plate Armor|Half Plate Armor]] or [[Plate Armor|Plate Armor]].
>
> *Rules summary checked against the supplied book page image; see the source for the original wording and illustration.*
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=289|Dungeon Master's Guide]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Plate Armor of Etherealness
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p289-half-plate-armor-of-etherealness.png|320]]
>
> Armor (Half Plate Armor or Plate Armor), Legendary (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While you're wearing this armor, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action and use a command word to gain the effect of the [[Etherealness|Etherealness]]  spell. The spell ends immediately if you remove the armor or take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to repeat the command word. This property of the armor can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=234|SRD 5.2.1, p. 234]]
