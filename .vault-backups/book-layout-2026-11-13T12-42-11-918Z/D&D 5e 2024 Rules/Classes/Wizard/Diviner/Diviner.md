# Diviner

**Class:** [[Classes/Wizard/Wizard|Wizard]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Divination Savant

Add two level-2-or-lower Divination Wizard spells to your spellbook for free. Each new spell-slot level in this class grants another free eligible Divination spell.

### Level 3: Portent

After each Long Rest, record two d20 results. Before a visible creature’s or your own D20 Test, replace its roll with a recorded result, at most once per turn. Each result is used once; unused results expire on your next Long Rest.

### Level 6: Expert Divination

Casting a Divination spell with a level-2+ slot restores a lower-level expended slot, no higher than level 5.

### Level 10: The Third Eye

A Bonus Action chooses enhanced sight until you start a Short/Long Rest: Darkvision, reading any language, or slot-free See Invisibility. Refresh on a Short or Long Rest; the source specifies the options’ exact ranges.

### Level 14: Greater Portent

Roll and record three Portent dice instead of two.
