# Meteor Swarm

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Meteor Swarm
> *Level 9 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 1 mile  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Blazing orbs of fire plummet to the ground at four different points you can see within range. Each creature in a 40-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on each of those points makes a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 20d6 Fire damage and 20d6 Bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature in the area of more than one fiery Sphere is affected only once.
>
> A nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried also takes the damage if it's in the spell's area, and the object starts [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Hazards/Burning|burning]] if it's flammable.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
