# Boots of Striding and Springing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Boots of Striding and Springing
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the boots, your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] becomes 30 feet unless it is already higher. Carrying more than your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Carrying Capacity|carrying capacity]] or wearing [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Heavy|Heavy]] Armor doesn't reduce your Speed. Once on each of your turns, you can [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Jump|jump]] up to 30 feet by spending only 10 feet of movement.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Boots of Striding and Springing
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While you wear these boots, your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] becomes 30 feet unless your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is higher, and your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] isn't reduced by you carrying weight in excess of your carrying capacity or wearing [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Heavy|Heavy]] Armor.
>

>
