# Private Sanctum

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Private Sanctum
> *Level 4 Abjuration (Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 10 minutes  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a thin sheet of lead)  
> **Duration:** 24 hours  
>
> You make an area within range magically secure. The area is a [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] that can be as small as 5 feet to as large as 100 feet on each side. The spell lasts for the duration.
>
> When you cast the spell, you decide what sort of security the spell provides, choosing any of the following properties:
>
> - Sound can't pass through the barrier at the edge of the warded area.
> - The barrier of the warded area appears dark and foggy, preventing vision (including [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]]) through it.
> - Sensors created by Divination spells can't appear inside the protected area or pass through the barrier at its perimeter.
> - Creatures in the area can't be targeted by Divination spells.
> - Nothing can teleport into or out of the warded area.
> - Planar travel is blocked within the warded area.
>
> Casting this spell on the same spot every day for 420 days makes the spell last until dispelled.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can increase the size of the Cube by 100 feet for each spell slot level above 4.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
