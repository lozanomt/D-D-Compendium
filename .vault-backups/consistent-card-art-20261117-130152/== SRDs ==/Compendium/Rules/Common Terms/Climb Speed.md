# Climb Speed

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Climb Speed
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Climb Speed|Climb Speed]] can be used in place of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] to traverse a vertical surface without expending the extra movement normally associated with climbing. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Climbing|Climbing]]" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]]."
>
