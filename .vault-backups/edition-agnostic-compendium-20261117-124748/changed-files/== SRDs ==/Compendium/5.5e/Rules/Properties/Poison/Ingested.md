# Ingested

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ingested
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature must swallow an entire dose of ingested poison to suffer its effects. The dose can be delivered in food or a liquid. You may decide that a partial dose has a reduced effect, such as allowing [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on the saving throw or dealing only half as much damage on a failed save.
>
