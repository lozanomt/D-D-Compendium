# Reaction

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Reaction
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] is a special action taken in response to a trigger defined in the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]]'s description. You can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] on another creature's turn, and if you take it on your turn, you can do so even if you also take an action, a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]], or both. Once you take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]], you can't take another one until the start of your next turn. The Opportunity
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] is a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] available to all creatures. See also "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]]" and "Playing the Game" ("Actions").
