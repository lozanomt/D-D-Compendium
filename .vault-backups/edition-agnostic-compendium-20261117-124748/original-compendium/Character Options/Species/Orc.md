# Orc

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Orc
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p198-orc.png|320]]
>
> **Creature Type:** Humanoid  
> **Size:** Medium (about 6 - 7 feet tall)  
> **Speed:** 30 feet  
>
> As an Orc, you have these special traits.
>
> ***Adrenaline Rush.*** You can take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Dash|Dash]] action as a Bonus Action. When you do so, you gain a number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> ***Darkvision.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet.
>
> ***Relentless Endurance.*** When you are reduced to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once you use this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
