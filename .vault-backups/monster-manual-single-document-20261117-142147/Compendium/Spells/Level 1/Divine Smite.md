# Divine Smite

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Divine Smite
> *Level 1 Evocation (Paladin)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p267-divine-smite.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]]  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> The target takes an extra 2d8 Radiant damage from the attack. The damage increases by 1d8 if the target is a Fiend or an Undead.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
