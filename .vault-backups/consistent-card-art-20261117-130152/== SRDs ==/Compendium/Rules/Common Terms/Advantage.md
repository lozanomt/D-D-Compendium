# Advantage

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Advantage
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have Advantage on a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], roll two d20s and use the lower roll. If multiple situations affect a roll and they all grant Advantage on it, you still roll only two d20s.
>
> If circumstances cause a roll to have both Advantage and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]], the roll has neither of them, and you roll one d20. This is true even if multiple circumstances impose Disadvantage and only one grants Advantage or vice versa. In such a situation, you have neither Advantage nor Disadvantage.
>
> When you have Advantage or Disadvantage and something in the game lets you reroll or replace the d20, you can reroll or replace only one die, not both. You choose which one.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Advantage
>
> If you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], roll two d20s, and use the higher roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]], and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the same roll cancel each other. See also "Playing the Game" ("D20 Tests").
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=176|SRD 5.2.1, p. 176]]
