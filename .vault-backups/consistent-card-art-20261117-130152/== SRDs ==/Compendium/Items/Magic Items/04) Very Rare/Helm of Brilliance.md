## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Helm of Brilliance
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This helm is set with 1d10 diamonds, 2d10 rubies, 3d10 fire opals, and 4d10 opals. Any gem pried from the helm crumbles to dust. When all the gems are removed or destroyed, the helm loses its magic.
>
> You gain the following benefits while wearing the helm.
>
> Diamond Light. As long as it has at least one diamond, the helm emits a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]].
>
> When at least one Undead is within that area, the
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] is filled with [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]]. Any Undead that starts its turn in that area takes 1d6 Radiant damage.
>
> Fire Opal Flames. As long as the helm has at least one fire opal, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to cause one weapon you are holding to burst into flames.
>
> The flames emit [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot radius and
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet. The flames are harmless to you and the weapon. When you hit with an attack using the blazing weapon, the target takes an extra 1d6 Fire damage. The flames last until you take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to extinguish them or until you drop or stow the weapon.
>
> Ruby Resistance. As long as the helm has at least one ruby, you have Resistance to Fire damage.
>
> Spells. You can cast one of the following spells (save DC 18), using one of the helm's gems of the specified type as a component: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 3/Daylight|Daylight]]  (opal), [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 3/Fireball|Fireball]] (fire opal), [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 7/Prismatic Spray|Prismatic Spray]]  (diamond), or Wall of
>
> Fire (ruby). The gem is destroyed when the spell is cast and disappears from the helm.
>
> Taking Fire [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Damage|Damage]]. Roll 1d20 if you are wearing the helm and take Fire damage as a result of failing a saving throw against a spell. On a roll of 1, the helm emits beams of light from its remaining gems and is then destroyed. Each creature within a 60- foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from you must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or be struck by a beam, taking Radiant damage equal to the number of gems in the helm.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=225|SRD 5.2.1, p. 225–226]]
