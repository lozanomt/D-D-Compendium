# Entangle

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Entangle
> *Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p272-entangle.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 90 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Grasping plants sprout from the ground in a 20-foot square within range. For the duration, these plants turn the ground in the area into [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]. They disappear when the spell ends.
>
> Each creature (other than you) in the area when you cast the spell must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition until the spell ends. A Restrained creature can take an action to make a Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check against your spell save DC. On a success, it frees itself from the grasping plants and is no longer Restrained by them.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
