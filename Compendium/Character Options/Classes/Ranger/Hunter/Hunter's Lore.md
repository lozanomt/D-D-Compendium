# Hunter's Lore

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hunter's Lore
>
> ![[phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> You can call on the forces of nature to reveal certain strengths and weaknesses of your prey. While a creature is marked by your *[[Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]*, you know whether that creature has any [[Immunity|Immunities]], [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistances]], or [[Vulnerability|Vulnerabilities]], and if the creature has any, you know what they are.
>
> #### Handbook source
>

>
