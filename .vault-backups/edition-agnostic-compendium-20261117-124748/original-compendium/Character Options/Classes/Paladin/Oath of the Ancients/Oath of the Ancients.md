>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Nature’s Wrath
>
> A Magic action and Channel Divinity force chosen visible creatures within 15 feet to make Strength saves or become Restrained for one minute, repeating the save each turn.
>
> #### Level 3: Oath of the Ancients Spells
>
> Always prepare the source’s oath spells as you level, from Ensnaring Strike and Speak with Animals through Commune with Nature and Tree Stride.
>
> #### Level 7: Aura of Warding
>
> You and allies in Aura of Protection resist Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant damage.
>
> #### Level 15: Undying Sentinel
>
> Once per Long Rest, when reduced to 0 without being killed outright, drop to 1 instead and then regain three times Paladin level Hit Points. You cannot be aged magically and stop visibly aging.
>
> #### Level 20: Elder Champion
>
> A Bonus Action empowers your aura for one minute: enemies there have Disadvantage against your spells and Channel Divinity, you heal 10 at each turn’s start, and action-casting spells can be cast as Bonus Actions. Refresh on a Long Rest or with a level-5 slot.
