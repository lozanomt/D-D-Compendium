# Fiendish Resilience

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fiendish Resilience
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p165-fiend-patron.png|320]]
>
> Choose one damage type, other than Force, whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to that damage type until you choose a different one with this feature.
