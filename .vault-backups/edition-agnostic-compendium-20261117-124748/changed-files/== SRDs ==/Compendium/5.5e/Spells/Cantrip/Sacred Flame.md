# Sacred Flame

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sacred Flame
> *Evocation Cantrip (Cleric)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 Radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Half Cover|Half Cover]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Three-Quarters Cover|Three-Quarters Cover]] for this save.
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
