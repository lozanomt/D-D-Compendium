# Level 6 Spell Scroll

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Level 6 Spell Scroll
> *Scroll, Very Rare*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> **Spell level:** 6
> **Spell save DC:** 17
> **Spell [[Attack|attack]] bonus:** +9
>
> A [[Spell Scroll|Spell Scroll]] holds one spell in a mystical cipher. If the spell is on your spell list, you can read and cast it without Material [[Components|components]]; otherwise the scroll is unintelligible. Use the spell's normal casting time. The scroll crumbles to dust when the spell is cast; interrupting the casting does not destroy it.
>
> If the spell is on your list but is a higher level than you can normally cast, make an ability check using your [[Spellcasting|spellcasting]] ability, with DC 10 + the spell's level. On a failure, the spell disappears from the scroll without effect.
>
> See Spell Scroll for the full rarity and level table.
>
> **Copying into a [[Spellbook|Spellbook]].** A [[Wizard|Wizard]] spell on a scroll can be copied into a spellbook. The copier makes an Intelligence ([[Arcana|Arcana]]) check with DC 10 + the spell's level. A success copies the spell. The scroll is destroyed whether the check succeeds or fails.
