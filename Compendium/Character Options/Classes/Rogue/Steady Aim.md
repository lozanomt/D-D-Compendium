# Steady Aim

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Steady Aim
>
> ![[phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]
>
> As a Bonus Action, you give yourself [[Advantage|Advantage]] on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this feature only if you haven’t moved during this turn, and after you use it, your [[Speed|Speed]] is 0 until the end of the current turn.
>
