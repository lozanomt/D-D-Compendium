# Revivify

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Revivify
> *Level 3 Necromancy (Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a diamond worth 300+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You touch a creature that has died within the last minute. That creature revives with 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]]. This spell can't revive a creature that has died of old age, nor does it restore any missing body parts.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
