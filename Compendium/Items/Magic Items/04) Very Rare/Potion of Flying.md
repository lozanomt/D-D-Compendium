> [!info]
> ### Potion of Flying
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Potion, Very Rare When you drink this potion, you gain a [[Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] equal to your [[Speed|Speed]] for 1 hour and can hover. If you're in the air when the potion wears off, you fall unless you have some other means of staying aloft.
>
> This potion's clear liquid floats at the top of its container and has cloudy white impurities drifting in it.
>
