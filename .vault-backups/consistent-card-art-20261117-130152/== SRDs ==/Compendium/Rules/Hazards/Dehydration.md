# Dehydration

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dehydration
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature requires an amount of water per day based on its size, as shown in the Water Needs per Day table. A creature that drinks less than half the required water for a day gains 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level at the day’s end. Exhaustion caused by dehydration can’t be removed until the creature drinks the full amount of water required for a day.
> #### Water Needs per Day
> | Size       | Food      |
> | ---------- | --------- |
> | Tiny       | 1/4 gallon |
> | Small      | 1 gallon   |
> | Medium     | 1 gallon   |
> | Large      | 4 gallons  |
> | Huge       | 16 gallons |
> | Gargantuan | 64 gallons |
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
