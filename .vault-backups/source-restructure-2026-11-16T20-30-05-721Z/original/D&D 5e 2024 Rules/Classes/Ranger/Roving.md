# Roving

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p121-ranger.png|320]]

Your [[Speed]] increases by 10 feet while you aren’t wearing Heavy armor. You also have a [[Climbing|Climb]] Speed and a [[Swimming|Swim]] Speed equal to your Speed.
