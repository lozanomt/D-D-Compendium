# Mind Blank

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Mind Blank
> *Level 8 Abjuration (Bard, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 24 hours  
>
> Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has Immunity to Psychic damage and the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition. The target is also unaffected by anything that would sense its emotions or alignment, read its thoughts, or magically detect its location, and no spell - not even *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Wish|Wish]]* - can gather information about the target, observe it remotely, or control its mind.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
