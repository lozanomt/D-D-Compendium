## What it covers

- Reading stat blocks and running monsters
- Alphabetical monster entries
- Animal statistics
- Monster conversion guidance
- Lists organized by habitat, creature type, group, and challenge rating

## Using this in the vault

Use this when selecting creatures for encounters. Keep encounter plans and world-specific creature lore in the relevant campaign or world notes. When playing in another DM’s campaign, use only creature information your table allows you to consult.

## Edition note

This book’s credits identify its first printing as February 2025. It belongs with the revised rules collection even though the Player’s Handbook and Dungeon Master’s Guide were printed in 2024.
