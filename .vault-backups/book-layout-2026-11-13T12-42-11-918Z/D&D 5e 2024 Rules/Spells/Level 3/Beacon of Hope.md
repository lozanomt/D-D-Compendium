#### Beacon of Hope
*Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 minute

Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has [[Advantage]] on Wisdom saving throws and[[Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]] and regains the maximum number of Hit Points possible from any healing.
