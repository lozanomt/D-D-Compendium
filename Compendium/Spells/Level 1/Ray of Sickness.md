# Ray of Sickness

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Ray of Sickness
> *Level 1 Necromancy (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You shoot a greenish ray at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Poison damage and has the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
