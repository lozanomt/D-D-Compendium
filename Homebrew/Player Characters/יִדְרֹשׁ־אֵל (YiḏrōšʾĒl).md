> > ### Character Traits
> > 
> > **Personality:** I speak with the quiet confidence of one raised to lead, though I rarely reveal my royal heritage. I listen more than I speak, observing others carefully before deciding whom to trust. I believe righteousness is proven through action, not proclamation, and I cannot willingly ignore the suffering of the innocent. I offer mercy freely to the repentant, but stand resolute against those who exploit the weak. Though exile has taught me humility and caution, I still carry a prince’s sense of duty. I seek wisdom before conflict, prefer stealth over needless bloodshed, and consider every life I can save a victory greater than any enemy I defeat.
> > 
> > **Ideals:** Righteousness (Ṣedeq). True righteousness is not measured by power, wealth, or birthright, but by justice, mercy, and faithfulness. I am called to defend the innocent, liberate the oppressed, heal the suffering, and confront evil. I believe authority is a sacred responsibility, never a license to rule through fear. (Good)
> > 
> > **Bonds:** I am Yidreš’el, son of Melekh-Raz, heir to the hidden kingdom of Beit HaSod. Though exile separates me from my homeland, I remain bound to my father, my people, and the sacred covenant of Ṣedeq. I carry the honor of my house and the responsibility to preserve its ancient wisdom. I will return when the time is right, but until then, every captive I free, every innocent I protect, and every life I restore brings me closer to becoming the righteous leader my people deserve.
> > 
> > **Flaws:** I carry the pride of a prince and the burden of believing I must answer every injustice I witness. I struggle to accept that some people cannot be saved, and I sometimes risk my life—and the lives of my companions—to protect a stranger. Though I preach mercy and humility, I find it difficult to forgive those who deliberately exploit the innocent. My greatest fear is that, in my determination to defeat evil, I may become as merciless as those I oppose.

> > ## Backstory & Notes
> > 
> > ### Backstory
> > 
> > יִדְרֹשׁ־אֵל מִבֵּית הַסּוֹד בֶּן־מֶלֶךְ־רָז
> > Yiḏrōš-ʾĒl mi-Bêṯ ha-Sôḏ ben-Meleḵ-Rāz
> > 
> > Yiḏrōš-ʾĒl was born among the בְּנֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם (Bənê ha-Šāmayim), the Children of the Heavens, an ancient winged celestial people dwelling in בֵּית הַסּוֹד (Bêṯ ha-Sôḏ), the House of the Secret. Son of מֶלֶךְ־רָז (Meleḵ-Rāz), he was raised under בְּרִית הַסּוֹד (Bərīṯ ha-Sôḏ), the Covenant of the Secret Council, in devotion to צֶדֶק (Ṣeḏeq), righteousness and justice.
> > 
> > Moved by mortal suffering, he willingly surrendered his wings to descend into the world below. The sacrifice left two scars upon his back and severed his passage home, for the hidden paths to his kingdom cannot be traversed without wings.
> > 
> > Now a wandering disciple of דֶּרֶךְ הָרַחֲמִים (Dereḵ hā-Raḥămīm), the Way of Mercy, he heals the wounded, frees captives, and defends the oppressed. He conceals his royal birth, striving to temper princely pride with humility and justice with compassion.
> > 
> > Known as the Seeker, he hopes through his deeds to earn the names Yôšīaʿ-ʾĒl (Liberator), Yip̄deh-ʾĒl (Redeemer), and Rəp̄āʾēl (Healer).
> > 
> > צֶדֶק צֶדֶק תִּרְדֹּף
> > Ṣeḏeq, Ṣeḏeq tirdōp̄.
> > “Justice, justice shall you pursue.”
> > — Deuteronomy 16:20
> > 
> > **Allies & Organizations:** My father, Melekh-Raz, and the people of Beit HaSod remain dear to me, though I must keep my distance to protect their secrecy. Beyond my homeland, I seek fellowship with those who defend the innocent, heal the suffering, and pursue righteousness.
> > 
> > **Enemies:** Tyrants, enslavers, corrupt rulers, and servants of darkness who oppose Ṣedeq. I seek to liberate the oppressed, expose deception, and confront evil with justice and mercy. I bear no enemies from Beit HaSod; my exile was voluntary.
