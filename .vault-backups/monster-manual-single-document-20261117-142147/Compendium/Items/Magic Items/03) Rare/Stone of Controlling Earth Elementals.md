# Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Stone of Controlling Earth Elementals
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p315-stone-of-controlling-earth-elementals.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> While touching this 5-pound stone to the ground, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to summon an [[Earth Elemental|Earth Elemental]] in an unoccupied space you choose within 30 feet. It obeys your commands and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[Initiative|Initiative]] count. It disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a Bonus Action. The stone can't summon another elemental until the next dawn.
>

>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=315|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
