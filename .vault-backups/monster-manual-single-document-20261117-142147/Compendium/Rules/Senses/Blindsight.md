# Blindsight

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Blindsight
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you have Blindsight, you can see within a specific range without relying on physical sight. Within that range, you can see anything that isn’t behind [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Total Cover|Total Cover]] even if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition or are in [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Darkness|Darkness]]. Moreover, in that range, you can see something that has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
