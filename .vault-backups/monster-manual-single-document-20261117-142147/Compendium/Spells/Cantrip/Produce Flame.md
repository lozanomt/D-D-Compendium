# Produce Flame

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Produce Flame
> *Conjuration Cantrip (Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** 10 minutes  
>
> A flickering flame appears in your hand and remains there for the duration. While there, the flame emits no heat and ignites nothing, and it sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. The spell ends if you cast it again.
>
> Until the spell ends, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to hurl fire at a creature or an object within 60 feet of you. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Fire damage.
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
