# Swarm of Ravens

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Swarm of Ravens
>
> *Medium Swarm of Tiny Beasts, Unaligned*
>
> ![[mm-p373-swarm-of-ravens.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 12  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 11 (2d8 + 2)  
> **Speed:** 10 ft., [[Flying|Fly]] 50 ft. MOD SAVE MOD SAVE MOD SAVE  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> | **Int** | 5 | -3 | -3 | **Wis** | 12 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** | 6 | -2 | -2 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Perception|Perception]] +5  
> **Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing  
> **Immunities:** [[Charmed|Charmed]], [[Frightened|Frightened]], [[Grappled|Grappled]], [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[Petrified|Petrified]], [[Prone|Prone]], [[Restrained|Restrained]], [[Stunned|Stunned]]  
> **Senses:** [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 15  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 1/4 (XP 50; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Beaks.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied.
>
> ***Cacophony (Recharge 6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. *Failure:* The target has the [[Deafened|Deafened]] condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Deafened, the target also has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
>
