# Dwarf

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dwarf
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p191-dwarf.png|320]]
>
> **Creature Type:** Humanoid  
> **Size:** Medium (about 4 - 5 feet tall)  
> **Speed:** 30 feet  
>
> As a Dwarf, you have these special traits.
>
> ***Darkvision.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] with a range of 120 feet.
>
> ***Dwarven Resilience.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Poison damage. You also have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition.
>
> ***Dwarven Toughness.*** Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.
>
> ***Stonecunning.*** As a Bonus Action, you gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Tremorsense|Tremorsense]] with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You must be on a stone surface or touching a stone surface to use this Tremorsense. The stone can be natural or worked. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
