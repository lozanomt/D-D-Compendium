# Indomitable

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Indomitable
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p093-fighter.png|320]]
>
> If you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it with a bonus equal to your Fighter level. You must use the new roll, and you can’t use this feature again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. You can use this feature twice before a Long Rest starting at level 13 and three times before a Long Rest starting at level 17.
>
