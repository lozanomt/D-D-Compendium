## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Gloves of Missile Snaring
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) If you're hit by an attack roll made with a Ranged or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Thrown|Thrown]] weapon while wearing these gloves, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] to reduce the damage by 1d10 plus your Dexterity modifier if you have a free hand.
>
> If you reduce the damage to 0, you can catch the ammunition or weapon if it is small enough for you to hold in that hand.
>
