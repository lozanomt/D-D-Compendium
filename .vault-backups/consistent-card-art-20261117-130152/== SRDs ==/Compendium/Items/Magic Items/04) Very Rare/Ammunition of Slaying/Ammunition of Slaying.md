# Ammunition of Slaying

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ammunition of Slaying [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/04) Very Rare/Ammunition of Slaying/Ammunition of Slaying|(Varies)]]
> *Armor (Any Ammunition), Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This magic ammunition is meant to slay creatures of a particular type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the table below. If a creature of that type takes damage from the ammunition, the creature makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking an extra 6d10 Force damage on a failed save or half as much extra damage on a successful one.
>
> After dealing its extra damage to a creature, the ammunition becomes nonmagical.
>
> |  1d100  | Creature Type |
> | :-----: | ------------- |
> | 01 - 10 | Aberrations   |
> | 11 - 15 | Beasts        |
> | 16 - 20 | Celestials    |
> | 21 - 25 | Constructs    |
> | 26 - 35 | Dragons       |
> | 36 - 45 | Elementals    |
> | 46 - 50 | Humanoids     |
> | 51 - 60 | Fey           |
> | 61 - 70 | Fiends        |
> | 71 - 75 | Giants        |
> | 76 - 80 | Monstrosities |
> | 81 - 85 | Oozes         |
> | 86 - 90 | Plants        |
> | 91 - 00 | Undead        |
>
> (You can create the individual ammunition as you need it, there are way too many variants for me to want to go through and make them all. If you use the default magic cost rules, the cost of these arrows would be 20,000 GP + the base cost of the item. Unlike other ammunition, this makes the most sense to be sold in instances of 1 since getting a full batch of 50 *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Ammunition/Needles|Needles]] of Slaying* seems a bit outrageous.)
>
