>
> **AC:** 12  
> **Initiative:** +4 (14)

> **Speed:** 10 ft.  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> **Immunities:** Poison, Psychic; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 6  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)  
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Smother.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the rug can give it the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) instead of dealing damage. Until the grapple ends, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] conditions, is suffocating, and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The rug can smother only one creature at a time.
>
> While grappling the target, the rug can't take this action, the rug halves the damage it takes (round down), and the target takes the same amount of damage.
>
