# Potion of Resistance

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Resistance [[Potion of Resistance|(200 GP)]]
> *Potion, Uncommon*
>
> ![[srd-p236-table-2.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> When you drink this potion, you have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to one type of damage for 1 hour. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.
>
> | 1d10 | Damage Type |
> | :--: | ----------- |
> |  1   | Acid        |
> |  2   | Cold        |
> |  3   | Fire        |
> |  4   | Force       |
> |  5   | Lightning   |
> |  6   | Necrotic    |
> |  7   | Poison      |
> |  8   | Psychic     |
> |  9   | Radiant     |
> |  10  | Thunder     |
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
