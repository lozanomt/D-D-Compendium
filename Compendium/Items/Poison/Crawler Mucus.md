# Crawler Mucus

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Crawler Mucus [[Crawler Mucus|(200 GP)]]
> *[[Contact|Contact]] Poison*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Crawler Mucus must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The creature also has the [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition while Poisoned in this way. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
