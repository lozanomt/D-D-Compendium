# Damage Types

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Damage Types
>
> Attacks and other harmful effects deal different types of damage. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Damage|Damage]] types have no rules of their own, but other rules, such as Resistance, rely on the types. The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Damage Types|Damage Types]] table offers examples to help a GM assign a type to a new effect.
