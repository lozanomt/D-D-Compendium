# Self-Restoration

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Self-Restoration
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p103-monk.png|320]]
>
> Through sheer force of will, you can remove one of the following conditions from yourself at the end of each of your turns: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]. In addition, forgoing food and drink doesn’t give you levels of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
>
