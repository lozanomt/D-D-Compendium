# Starry Wisp

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Starry Wisp
> *Evocation Cantrip (Bard, Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You launch a mote of light at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Radiant damage, and until the end of your next turn, it emits [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] in a 10-foot radius and can't benefit from the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
