# Sneak Attack

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sneak Attack
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]
>
> You know how to strike subtly and exploit a foe’s distraction. Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on the roll and the attack uses a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Finesse|Finesse]] or a Ranged weapon. The extra damage’s type is the same as the weapon’s type.
>
> You don’t need Advantage on the attack roll if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the ally doesn’t have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, and you don’t have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll.
>
> The extra damage increases as you gain Rogue levels, as shown in the Sneak Attack column of the Rogue Features table.
>
