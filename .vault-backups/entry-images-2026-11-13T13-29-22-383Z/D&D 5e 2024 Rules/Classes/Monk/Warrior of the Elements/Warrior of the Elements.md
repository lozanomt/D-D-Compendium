# Warrior of the Elements

**Class:** [[Classes/Monk/Monk|Monk]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Elemental Attunement

At your turn’s start, spend 1 Focus Point for ten minutes of elemental power, ending if Incapacitated. Unarmed Strikes gain 10 feet of reach; choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage and force a Strength save to pull or push a hit target up to 10 feet.

### Level 3: Manipulate Elements

Learn Elementalism, using Wisdom.

### Level 6: Elemental Burst

A Magic action and 2 Focus Points create a 20-foot-radius Sphere within 120 feet. Creatures make Dexterity saves against three Martial Arts dice of chosen elemental damage, taking half on success.

### Level 11: Stride of the Elements

While Elemental Attunement is active, gain Fly and Swim Speeds equal to your Speed.

### Level 17: Elemental Epitome

While attuned, resist one chosen elemental damage type, changeable each turn. Step of the Wind adds 20 feet of Speed and can damage nearby creatures during movement, each once per turn. Once on each of your turns, an Unarmed Strike can deal an extra Martial Arts die of its damage type.
