# Potion of Poison

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Poison
> *Potion, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> The potion looks, smells, and tastes like a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/00) Varies/Potions of Healing/Potion of Healing|Potion of Healing]] or another beneficial potion; illusion [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|magic]] masks its true [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Nature|nature]]. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Identify|Identify]] reveals the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Deception|deception]]. Drinking it deals 4d6 Poison damage and requires a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Poison
>
> Potion, Uncommon This concoction looks, smells, and tastes like a
>
> Potion of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Healing|Healing]] or another beneficial potion. However, it is actually poison masked by illusion magic.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Identify|Identify]] reveals its true nature.
>
> If you drink this potion, you take 4d6 Poison damage and must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=236|SRD 5.2.1, p. 236]]
