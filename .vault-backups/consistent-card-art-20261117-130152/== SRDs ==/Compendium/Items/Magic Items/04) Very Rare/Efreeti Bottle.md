## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Efreeti Bottle
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare When you take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to remove the stopper of this painted brass bottle, a cloud of thick smoke flows out of it. At the end of your turn, the smoke disappears with a flash of harmless fire, and an Efreeti appears in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you.
>
> The first time the bottle is opened, the GM rolls on the following table to determine what happens.
