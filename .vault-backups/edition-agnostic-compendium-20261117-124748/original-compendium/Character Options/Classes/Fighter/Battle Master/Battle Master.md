>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Combat Superiority
>
> Learn three maneuvers, usable one per attack, with four d8 Superiority Dice. Recover dice on Short or Long Rests. Maneuver DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + your choice of Strength or Dexterity modifier. Gain two maneuvers at levels 7, 10, and 15; gain extra dice at 7 and 15. See the linked source for the full maneuver menu.
>
> #### Level 3: Student of War
>
> Gain one Artisan’s Tools proficiency and one additional skill from the Fighter’s level-1 skill list.
>
> #### Level 7: Know Your Enemy
>
> A Bonus Action reveals Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities of a visible creature within 30 feet. Use once per Long Rest; spend a Superiority Die to restore it.
>
> #### Level 10: Improved Combat Superiority
>
> Superiority Dice become d10s.
>
> #### Level 15: Relentless
>
> Once per turn when using a maneuver, roll a d8 instead of spending a Superiority Die.
>
> #### Level 18: Ultimate Combat Superiority
>
> Superiority Dice become d12s.
