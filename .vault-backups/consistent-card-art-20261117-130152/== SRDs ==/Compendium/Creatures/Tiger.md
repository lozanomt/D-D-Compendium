>
> ***Prowl.*** The tiger moves up to half its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] without provoking [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]]. At the end of this movement, the tiger can take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]] action.
