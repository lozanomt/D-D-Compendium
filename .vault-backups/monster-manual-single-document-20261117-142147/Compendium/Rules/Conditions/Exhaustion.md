# Exhaustion

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Exhaustion
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Exhaustion condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Exhaustion Levels.*** This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Exhaustion level. You die if your Exhaustion level is 6.
>
> ***D20 Tests Affected.*** When you make a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], the roll is reduced by 2 times your Exhaustion level.
>
> **Speed:** Reduced.*** Your  
> **Speed:** is reduced by a number of feet equal to 5 times your Exhaustion level.  
>
> ***Removing Exhaustion Levels.*** Finishing a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]] removes 1 of your Exhaustion levels. When your Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
