> [[Weapon|Weapon]] (Any Simple or Martial), Legendary (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]] by a Paladin) You gain a +3 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with it, that creature takes an extra 2d10 Radiant damage.
>
> While you hold the drawn weapon, it creates a 10-foot [[Emanation|Emanation]] originating from you. You and all creatures [[Friendly|Friendly]] to you in the [[Emanation|Emanation]] have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. If you have 17 or more levels in the Paladin class, the size of the [[Emanation|Emanation]] increases to 30 feet.
>
