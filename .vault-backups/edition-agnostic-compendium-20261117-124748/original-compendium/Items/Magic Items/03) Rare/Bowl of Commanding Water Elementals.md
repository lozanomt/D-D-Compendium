# Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p243-boots-of-elvenkind.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> While the bowl is filled with water and you are within 5 feet of it, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to summon a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Water Elemental|Water Elemental]] in an unoccupied space as close to the bowl as possible. It understands your languages, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count. It disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a Bonus Action. The bowl can't summon another elemental until the next dawn. The bowl is about 1 foot in diameter and half as deep, and holds about 3 gallons.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Bowl of Commanding Water Elementals
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare While this bowl is filled with water and you are within 5 feet of it, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to summon a Water Elemental . The elemental appears in an unoccupied space as close to the bowl as possible, understands your languages, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count. The elemental disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]]. The bowl can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
>
> The bowl is about 1 foot in diameter and half as deep. It holds about 3 gallons.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=214|SRD 5.2.1, p. 214]]
