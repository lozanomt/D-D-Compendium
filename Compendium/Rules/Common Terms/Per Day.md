# Per Day

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Per Day
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If a rule says you can use something a certain number of times per day, that means you must finish a [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]] to use it again after you run out of uses.
>
