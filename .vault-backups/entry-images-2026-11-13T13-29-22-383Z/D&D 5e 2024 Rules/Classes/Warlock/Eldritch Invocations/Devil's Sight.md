# Devil's Sight

#### Devil’s Sight
*Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock*

You can see normally in [[Dim Light]] and [[Rules/Common Terms/Darkness|Darkness]] - both magical and nonmagical - within 120 feet of yourself.
