# Giant Insect

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Giant Insect
> *Level 4 Conjuration (Druid)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> You summon a giant centipede, spider, or wasp (chosen when you cast the spell). It manifests in an unoccupied space you can see within range and uses the **Giant Insect** stat block. The form you choose determines certain details in its stat block. The creature disappears when it drops to 0 [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or when the spell ends.
>
> The creature is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, the creature shares your [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count, but it takes its turn immediately after yours. It obeys your verbal commands (no action required by you). If you don't issue any, it takes the [[Compendium/Rules/Actions/Dodge|Dodge]] action and uses its movement to avoid danger.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
>
