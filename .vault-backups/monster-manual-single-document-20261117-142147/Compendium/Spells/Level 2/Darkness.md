# Darkness

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Darkness
> *Level 2 Evocation (Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, M (bat fur and a piece of coal)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> For the duration, magical [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Darkness|Darkness]] spreads from a point within range and fills a 15-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]]. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't illuminate it.
>
> Alternatively, you cast the spell on an object that isn't being worn or carried, causing the Darkness to fill a 15-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from that object. Covering that object with something opaque, such as a bowl or helm, blocks the Darkness.
>
> If any of this spell's area overlaps with an area of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] created by a spell of level 2 or lower, that other spell is dispelled.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
