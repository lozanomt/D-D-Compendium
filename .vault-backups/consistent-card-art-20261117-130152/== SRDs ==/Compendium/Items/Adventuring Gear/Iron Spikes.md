# Iron Spikes

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Iron Spikes [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Iron Spikes|(1 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Iron Spikes come in bundles of ten. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can use a blunt object, such as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Weapons/Simple Melee/Light Hammer|Light Hammer]], to hammer a spike into wood, earth, or a similar material. You can do so to jam a door shut or to then tie a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Rope|Rope]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Chain|Chain]] to the Spike.
>
> **Weight:** 5 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
