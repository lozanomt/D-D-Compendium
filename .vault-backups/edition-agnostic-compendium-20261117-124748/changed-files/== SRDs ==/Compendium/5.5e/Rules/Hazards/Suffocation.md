# Suffocation

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Suffocation
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature can hold its breath for a number of minutes equal to 1 plus its Constitution modifier (minimum of 30 seconds) before suffocation begins. When a creature runs out of breath or is choking, it gains 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level at the end of each of its turns. When a creature can breathe again, it removes all levels of Exhaustion it gained from suffocating.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
