# Remove Curse

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Remove Curse
> *Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> At your touch, all [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Curses|curses]] affecting one creature or object end. If the object is a cursed magic item, its curse remains, but the spell breaks its owner's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]] to the object so it can be removed or discarded.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
