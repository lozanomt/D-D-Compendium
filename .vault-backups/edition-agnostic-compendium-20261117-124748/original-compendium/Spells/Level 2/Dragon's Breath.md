# Dragon's Breath

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dragon's Breath
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a hot pepper)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You touch one willing creature, and choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until the spell ends, the target can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to exhale a 15-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cone|Cone]]. Each creature in that area makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 damage of the chosen type on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
