# Freedom of Movement

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Freedom of Movement
> *Level 4 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a leather strap)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target's movement is unaffected by [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]], and spells and other magical effects can neither reduce the target's Speed nor cause the target to have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] conditions. The target also has a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed equal to its Speed.
>
> In addition, the target can spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape from nonmagical restraints, such as manacles or a creature imposing the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition on it.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
