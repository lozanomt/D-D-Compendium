# Sun Blade

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Sun Blade
> *Weapon (Longsword), Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p316-sun-blade.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> **Blade of Radiance.** While grasping the hilt, take a Bonus Action to create or dismiss its radiant blade. With the blade present, it functions as a [[Magic|magic]] [[Longsword|Longsword]] with the [[Finesse|Finesse]] property. Proficiency with Longswords or Shortswords grants proficiency with the Sun Blade. You gain a +2 bonus to [[Attack|attack]] and damage rolls with it; it deals Radiant damage instead of Slashing. A hit against an Undead deals an extra 1d8 Radiant damage.
>
> **Sunlight.** The blade emits sunlight as [[Bright Light|Bright Light]] within 15 feet and [[Dim Light|Dim Light]] for another 15 feet. While it exists, take a Magic action to increase or decrease both radii by 5 feet each, to a minimum of 10 feet each or a maximum of 30 feet each.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Sun Blade
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p316-sun-blade.png|320]]
>
> [[Weapon|Weapon]] (Longsword), Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) This item appears to be a sword hilt.
>
> Blade of Radiance. While grasping the hilt, you can take a [[Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to cause a blade of pure radiance to spring into existence or make the blade disappear. While the blade exists, this magic weapon functions as a Longsword with the [[Finesse|Finesse]] property. If you are proficient with Longswords or Shortswords, you are proficient with the Sun Blade .
>
> You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this weapon, which deals Radiant damage instead of Slashing damage. When you hit an Undead with it, that target takes an extra 1d8 Radiant damage.
>
> Sunlight. The sword's luminous blade emits [[Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 15-foot radius and [[Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 15 feet. The light is sunlight. While the blade persists, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to expand or reduce its radius of [[Bright Light|Bright Light]] and [[Dim Light|Dim Light]] by 5 feet each, to a maximum of 30 feet each or a minimum of 10 feet each.
>
