# Sovereign Glue

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Sovereign Glue
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p308-spell-scroll.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This adhesive permanently bonds two objects. Store it in a jar or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Flask|flask]] coated inside with [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/02) Uncommon/Oil of Slipperiness|Oil of Slipperiness]]. A found container holds 1d6 + 1 ounces. One ounce covers a 1-foot-square surface. Applying an ounce takes a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, and the glue takes 1 minute to set. Once set, its bond can be broken only by [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/05) Legendary/Universal Solvent|Universal Solvent]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/03) Rare/Oil of Etherealness|Oil of Etherealness]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Wish|Wish]].
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Sovereign Glue
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary This viscous, milky-white substance can form a permanent adhesive bond between any two objects. It must be stored in a jar or flask that has been coated inside with Oil of Slipperiness . When found, a container contains 1d6 + 1 ounces.
>
> One ounce of the glue can cover a 1-foot square surface. Applying an ounce of Sovereign Glue  takes a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, and the applied glue takes 1 minute to set. Once it has done so, the bond it creates can be broken only by the application of Universal Solvent or Oil of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 7/Etherealness|Etherealness]], or with a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 9/Wish|Wish]] spell.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=244|SRD 5.2.1, p. 244]]
