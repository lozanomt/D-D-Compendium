# Shape-Shifting

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Shape-Shifting
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If an effect, such as [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] or the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 4/Polymorph|Polymorph]]* spell, lets you shape-shift, its description specifies what happens to you. Unless that description says otherwise, any ongoing effects on you - conditions, spells, curses, and the like - carry over from one form to the other. You revert to your true form if you die.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
