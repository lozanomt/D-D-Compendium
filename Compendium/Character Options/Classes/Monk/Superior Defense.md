# Superior Defense

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Superior Defense
>
> ![[phb-p103-monk.png|320]]
>
> At the start of your turn, you can expend 3 Focus Points to bolster yourself against harm for 1 minute or until you have the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. During that time, you have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to all damage except Force damage.
>
> #### Handbook source
>
> [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=104|Player’s Handbook (2024), PDF page 104]]
>
