# Vicious Mockery

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Vicious Mockery
> *Enchantment Cantrip (Bard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p340-vicious-mockery.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You unleash a string of insults laced with subtle enchantments at one creature you can see or hear within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d6 Psychic damage and have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
