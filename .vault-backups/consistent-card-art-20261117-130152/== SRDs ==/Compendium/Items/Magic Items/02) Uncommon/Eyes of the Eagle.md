# Eyes of the Eagle

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Eyes of the Eagle [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Magic Items/02) Uncommon/Eyes of the Eagle|(400 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p264-eyes-of-charming.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]]) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
