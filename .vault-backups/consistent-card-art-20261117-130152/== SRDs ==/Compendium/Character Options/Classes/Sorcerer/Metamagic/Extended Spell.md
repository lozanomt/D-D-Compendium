# Extended Spell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Extended Spell
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p141-sorcerer.png|320]]
>
> **Cost:** 1 Sorcery Points  
>
> When you cast a spell that has a duration of 1 minute or longer, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to double its duration to a maximum duration of 24 hours. If the affected spell requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on any saving throw you make to maintain that Concentration.
>
