# Spellcasting Focus

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Spellcasting Focus
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Spellcasting Focus|Spellcasting Focus]] is an object that certain creatures can use in place of a spell's Material components if those materials aren't consumed by the spell and don't have a cost specified. Some classes allow its members to use certain types of Spellcasting Focuses. See also  "Spells" ("Casting Spells").
>
