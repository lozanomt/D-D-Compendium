# Silence

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Silence
> *Level 2 Illusion (Bard, Cleric, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the Sphere has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to Thunder damage, and creatures have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]] condition while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a Verbal component is impossible there.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
