# Pipes of the Sewers

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Pipes of the Sewers
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[dmg-p288-pipes-of-the-sewers.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While the pipes are on your person, ordinary and giant rats are Indifferent toward you and won't [[Attack|attack]] unless you threaten or [[Harm|harm]] them. The pipes have 3 charges and regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.
>
> If you play the pipes as a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can take a Bonus Action to expend 1-3 charges, calling one [[Swarm of Rats|Swarm of Rats]] per charge if the DM determines there are enough rats within half a mile. A charge is wasted if there are too few rats. Called swarms move toward the music by the shortest available route but are not otherwise under your control.
>
> Whenever a Swarm of Rats not controlled by another creature comes within 30 feet while you play, it makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a success, it behaves normally and can't be swayed by the music for 24 hours.
>
> On a failed save, the swarm becomes Friendly to you and your allies while you continue playing each round as a Magic action. It obeys your commands; without commands it defends itself but otherwise takes no actions. If it starts its turn more than 30 feet from you, your control ends; it behaves normally and can't be swayed by the music for 24 hours.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Pipes of the Sewers
>
> ![[dmg-p288-pipes-of-the-sewers.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While these pipes are on your person, ordinary rats and giant rats are [[Indifferent|Indifferent]] toward you and won't attack you unless you threaten or harm them.
>
> The pipes have 3 charges and regain 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you play the pipes as a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can take a [[Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to expend 1 to 3 charges, calling forth one Swarm of Rats with each expended charge if enough rats are within half a mile of you to be called in this fashion (as determined by the GM). If there aren't enough rats to form a swarm, the charge is wasted.
>
> Called swarms move toward the music by the shortest available route but aren't under your control otherwise.
>
> Whenever a Swarm of Rats  that isn't under another creature's control comes within 30 feet of you while you are playing the pipes, the swarm makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the swarm behaves as it normally would and can't be swayed by the pipes' music for the next 24 hours. On a failed save, the swarm is swayed by the pipes' music and becomes [[Friendly|Friendly]] to you and your allies for as long as you continue to play the pipes each round as a [[Magic|Magic]] action. A [[Friendly|Friendly]] swarm obeys your commands. If you issue no commands to a [[Friendly|Friendly]] swarm, it defends itself but otherwise takes no actions. If a [[Friendly|Friendly]] swarm starts its turn more than 30 feet away from you, your control over that swarm ends, and the swarm behaves as it normally would and can't be swayed by the pipes' music for the next 24 hours.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=234|SRD 5.2.1, p. 234]]
