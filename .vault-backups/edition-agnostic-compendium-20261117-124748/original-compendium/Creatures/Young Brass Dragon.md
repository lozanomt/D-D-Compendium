> **Speed:** 40 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Burrowing|Burrow]] 20 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Fly]] 80 ft.  
>

> **Skills:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] +6, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]] +5, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +3  
> **Immunities:** Fire  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] 30 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 120 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 16  
> **Languages:** Common, Draconic  
> **CR:** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)  
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The dragon makes three Rend attacks. It can replace two attacks with a use of Sleep Breath.
>
> ***Rend.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) Slashing damage.
>
> ***Fire Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 40-foot-long, 5-footwide Line. *Failure:* 38 (11d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>
> ***Sleep Breath.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot Cone. *Failure:* The target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition until the end of its next turn, at which point it repeats the save. *Second Failure:* The target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition for 1 minute. This effect ends for the target if it takes damage or a creature within 5 feet of it takes an action to wake it.
>
