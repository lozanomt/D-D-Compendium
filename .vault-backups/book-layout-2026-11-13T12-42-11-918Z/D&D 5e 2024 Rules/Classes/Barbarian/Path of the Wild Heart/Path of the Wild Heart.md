# Path of the Wild Heart

**Class:** [[Classes/Barbarian/Barbarian|Barbarian]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Animal Speaker

Cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals as Rituals, using Wisdom.

### Level 3: Rage of the Wilds

Choose when entering Rage: Bear grants damage Resistance except Force, Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant; Eagle combines Dash and Disengage with the Rage Bonus Action and later Bonus Actions; Wolf gives allies Advantage against enemies within 5 feet of you.

### Level 6: Aspect of the Wilds

Choose Owl (60-foot Darkvision, or +60 feet to existing Darkvision), Panther (Climb Speed), or Salmon (Swim Speed). Change the choice after a Long Rest.

### Level 10: Nature Speaker

Cast Commune with Nature as a Ritual, using Wisdom.

### Level 14: Power of the Wilds

Choose with each Rage: Falcon grants flight while unarmored; Lion impedes nearby enemies attacking other targets; Ram can knock Large or smaller targets Prone on melee hits.
