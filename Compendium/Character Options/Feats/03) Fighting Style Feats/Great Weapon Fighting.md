# Great Weapon Fighting

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Great Weapon Fighting
> *Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you roll damage for an attack you make with a Melee weapon that you are holding with two hands, you can treat any 1 or 2 on a damage die as a 3. The weapon must have the [[Two-Handed|Two-Handed]] or [[Versatile|Versatile]] property to gain this benefit.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
