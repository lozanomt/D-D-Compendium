# Hover

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hover
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Some creatures can hover, as noted in their stat blocks, and some spells and other effects grant the ability to hover. Hovering while [[Flying|Flying]] prevents you from falling in certain circumstances.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
