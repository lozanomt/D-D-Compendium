## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Longevity
>
> Potion, Very Rare When you drink this potion, your physical age is reduced by 1d6 + 6 years, to a minimum of 13 years.
>
> Each time you subsequently drink a Potion of Longevity, there is 10 percent cumulative chance that you instead age by 1d6 + 6 years.
>
> Suspended in this amber liquid is a tiny heart that, against all reason, is still beating. These ingredients vanish when the potion is opened.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=236|SRD 5.2.1, p. 236]]
