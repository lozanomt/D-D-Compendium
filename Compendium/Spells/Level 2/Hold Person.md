# Hold Person

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hold Person
> *Level 2 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Choose a Humanoid that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional Humanoid for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
