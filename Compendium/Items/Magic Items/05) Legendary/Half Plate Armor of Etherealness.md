# Half Plate Armor of Etherealness

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Half Plate Armor of Etherealness
> *Armor (Half Plate Armor), Legendary*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p289-half-plate-armor-of-etherealness.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the armor, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action and use a [[Command|command]] word to gain the effect of [[Etherealness|Etherealness]]. It ends immediately if you remove the armor or take a Magic action to repeat the command word. This property recharges at the next dawn.
>
> The [[Book|book]] lists this under **[[Plate Armor of Etherealness|Plate Armor of Etherealness]]** and permits either [[Half Plate Armor|Half Plate Armor]] or [[Plate Armor|Plate Armor]].
