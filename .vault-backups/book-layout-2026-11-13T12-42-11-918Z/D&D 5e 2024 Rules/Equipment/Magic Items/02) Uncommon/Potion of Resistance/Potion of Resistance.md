#### Potion of Resistance [[Potion of Resistance|(200 GP)]]
*Potion, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** No

When you drink this potion, you have [[Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to one type of damage for 1 hour. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.

|  7   | Poison      |
|  8   | Psychic     |
|  9   | Radiant     |
|  10  | Thunder     |
