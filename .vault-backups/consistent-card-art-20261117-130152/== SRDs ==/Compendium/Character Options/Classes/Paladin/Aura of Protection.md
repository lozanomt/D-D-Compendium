# Aura of Protection

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Aura of Protection
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p111-paladin.png|320]]
>
> You radiate a protective, unseeable aura in a 10-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] that originates from you. The aura is inactive while you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. You and your allies in the aura gain a bonus to saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum bonus of +1). If another Paladin is present, a creature can benefit from only one Aura of Protection at a time; the creature chooses which aura while in them.
>
> At Paladin level 7, your subclass may enhance your aura in one of the following ways:
> - **Oath of Devotion:** You and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition while in your Aura of Protection If a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] ally enters the aura, that condition has no effect on that ally while there.
>
> ***Aura of Courage.*** At Paladin level 10, you and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition while in your Aura of Protection If a Frightened ally enters the aura, that condition has no effect on that ally while there.
>
> ***Smite of Protection.*** At Paladin level 15, if your subclass is the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Oath of Devotion/Oath of Devotion|Oath of Devotion]], whenever you cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Divine Smite|Divine Smite]]*, you and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Half Cover|Half Cover]] while in your Aura of Protection. The aura has this benefit until the start of your next turn.
>
> ***Aura Expansion.*** At Paladin level 18, your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Aura of Protection|Aura of Protection]] is now a 30-foot Emanation.
>
