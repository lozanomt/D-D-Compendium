# Boots of Speed

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Boots of Speed
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p243-boots-of-elvenkind.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the boots, take a Bonus Action to click the heels together. Your [[Speed|Speed]] doubles, and creatures making [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] against you have [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on those [[Attack|attack]] rolls. Clicking your heels together again ends the effect. After a total of 10 minutes of use, the boots stop functioning until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> *Rules summary checked against the supplied book page image; see the source for the original wording and illustration.*
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=243|Dungeon Master's Guide]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Boots of Speed
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p243-boots-of-elvenkind.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While you wear these boots, you can take a Bonus
>
> [[Action|Action]] to click the boots' heels together. If you do, the boots double your [[Speed|Speed]], and any creature that makes an Opportunity [[Attack|Attack]] against you has [[Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll. If you click your heels together again, you end the effect.
>
> When you've used the boots' property for a total of 10 minutes, the magic ceases to function for you until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=214|SRD 5.2.1, p. 214]]
