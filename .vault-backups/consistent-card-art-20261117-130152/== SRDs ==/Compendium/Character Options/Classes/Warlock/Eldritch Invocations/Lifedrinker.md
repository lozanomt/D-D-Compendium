# Lifedrinker

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Lifedrinker
> **Prerequisite:** Level 9+ Warlock, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Warlock/Eldritch Invocations/Pact of the Blade|Pact of the Blade]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can deal an extra 1d6 Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage (your choice) to the creature, and you can expend one of your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Point Dice|Hit Point Dice]] to roll it and regain a number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to the roll plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 Hit Point).
>
