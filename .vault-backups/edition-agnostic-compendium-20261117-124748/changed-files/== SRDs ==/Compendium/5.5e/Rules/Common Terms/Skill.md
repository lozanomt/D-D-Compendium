# Skill

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Skill
>
> A skill is an area of specialization associated with an ability check. If you have proficiency in a skill, you can add your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus when you make an ability check associated with that skill. See also "Playing the Game" ("[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]]").
>
