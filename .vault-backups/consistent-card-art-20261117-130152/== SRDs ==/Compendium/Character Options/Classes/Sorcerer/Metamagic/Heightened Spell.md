# Heightened Spell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Heightened Spell
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p141-sorcerer.png|320]]
>
> **Cost:** 2 Sorcery Points  
>
