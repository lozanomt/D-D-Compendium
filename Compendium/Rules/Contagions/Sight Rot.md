# Sight Rot

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Sight Rot
> *Magical Contagion*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Any Beast or Humanoid that drinks water tainted by Sight Rot must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the contagion ends.
>
> ***Fighting the Contagion.*** Magic such as a *[[Compendium/Spells/Level 6/Heal|Heal]]* or *[[Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Lesser Restoration|Lesser Restoration]]* spell ends the contagion immediately. A character who is proficient with an [[Compendium/Items/Tools/Herbalism Kit|Herbalism Kit]] can use it to create one dose of nonmagical ointment, which takes 1 hour. When applied to the eyes of a creature suffering from Sight Rot, the ointment suppresses the contagion on that creature for 24 hours. If the contagion is suppressed in this way for a total of 72 hours (requiring three doses and applications of the ointment), the contagion ends on the creature.
>
> ***Spreading the Contagion.*** Any Humanoid that makes skin contact with a creature infected with Sight Rot must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or also become infected with the contagion. On a successful save, the Humanoid can’t catch the contagion from that particular infected creature for the next 24 hours.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
