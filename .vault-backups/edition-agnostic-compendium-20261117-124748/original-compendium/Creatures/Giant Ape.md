# Giant Ape

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Giant Ape
>
> *Huge Beast, Unaligned*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p356-giant-ape.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 12  
> **Initiative:** +5 (15)

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> | **Int** | 5 | -3 | -3 | **Wis** | 12 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** | 7 | -2 | -2 |
>
> **Skills:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]] +9, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] +4, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Survival|Survival]] +4  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 14  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 7 (XP 2,900; PB +3)  
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The ape makes two Fist attacks.
>
> ***Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.
>
> ***Boulder Toss (Recharge 6).*** The ape hurls a boulder at a point it can see within 90 feet. *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point. *Failure:* 24 (7d6) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
> #### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Leap.*** The ape jumps up to 30 feet by spending 10 feet of movement.
>
