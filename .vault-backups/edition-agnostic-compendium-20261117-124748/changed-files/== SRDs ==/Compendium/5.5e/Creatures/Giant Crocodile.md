> **CR:** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Hold Breath.*** The crocodile can hold its breath for 1 hour.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The crocodile makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 15). While Grappled, the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition and can't be targeted by the crocodile's Tail.
>
> ***Tail.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
