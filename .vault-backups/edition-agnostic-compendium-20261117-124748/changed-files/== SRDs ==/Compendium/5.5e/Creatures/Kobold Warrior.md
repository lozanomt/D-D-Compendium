# Kobold Warrior

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Kobold Warrior
>
> *Small Dragon, Neutral*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p187-kobold-warrior.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 14  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 7 (3d6 - 3)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

>
> **Gear:** Daggers (3)  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 8  
> **Languages:** Common, Draconic  
> **CR:** 1/8 (XP 25; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The kobold has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the kobold has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Dagger.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.
>
