# Superior Hunter's Defense

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Superior Hunter's Defense
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to give yourself [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to that damage and any other damage of the same type until the end of the current turn.
>
