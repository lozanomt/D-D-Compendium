# Caltrops

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Caltrops [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Caltrops|(1 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can spread Caltrops from their bag to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 1 Piercing damage and have its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Caltrops.
>
> **Weight:** 2 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
