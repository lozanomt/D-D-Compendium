# Survivor

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Survivor
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p099-champion.png|320]]
>
> You attain the pinnacle of resilience in battle, giving you these benefits.
>
> ***Defy Death.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]]. Moreover, when you roll 18-20 on a Death Saving Throw, you gain the benefit of rolling a 20 on it.
>
> ***Heroic Rally.*** At the start of each of your turns, you regain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to 5 plus your Constitution modifier if you are [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bloodied|Bloodied]] and have at least 1 Hit Point.
>
