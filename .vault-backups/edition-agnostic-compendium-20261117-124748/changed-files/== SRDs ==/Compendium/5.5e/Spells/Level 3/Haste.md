# Haste

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Haste
> *Level 3 Transmutation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a shaving of licorice root)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Choose a willing creature that you can see within range. Until the spell ends, the target's Speed is doubled, it gains a +2 bonus to Armor Class, it has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity saving throws, and it gains an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used to take only the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] (one attack only), [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Dash|Dash]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Disengage|Disengage]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action.
>
> When the spell ends, the target is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] and has a Speed of 0 until the end of its next turn, as a wave of lethargy washes over it.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
