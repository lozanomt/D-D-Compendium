# Warrior of Mercy

**Class:** [[Classes/Monk/Monk|Monk]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Hand of Harm

Once per turn after damaging a creature with an Unarmed Strike, spend 1 Focus Point for extra Necrotic damage equal to a Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier.

### Level 3: Hand of Healing

A Magic action and 1 Focus Point heal a touched creature by a Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier. During Flurry of Blows, replace one strike with this healing without its additional Focus cost.

### Level 3: Implements of Mercy

Gain Insight, Medicine, and Herbalism Kit proficiency.

### Level 6: Physician’s Touch

Hand of Harm can also Poison until your next turn ends. Hand of Healing can end one condition: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or Stunned.

### Level 11: Flurry of Healing and Harm

During Flurry of Blows, replace each strike with Hand of Healing, and apply Hand of Harm to a damaging Flurry strike, without their additional Focus costs. Hand of Harm remains once per turn. This enhanced benefit has Wisdom-modifier uses (minimum one) per Long Rest.

### Level 17: Hand of Ultimate Mercy

A Magic action and 5 Focus Points revive a creature dead no longer than 24 hours with 4d10 + Wisdom modifier Hit Points, removing the listed conditions. Once per Long Rest.
