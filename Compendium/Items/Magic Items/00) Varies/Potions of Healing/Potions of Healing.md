# Potions of Healing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Potions of Healing [[Potions of Healing|(Varies)]]
> *Potion, Rarity Varies*
>
> ![[dmg-p292-potion-of-healing.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
> **Weight:** 0.5 lb  
>
> As a Bonus Action, you can drink it or administer it to another creature within 5 feet of yourself. The creature that drinks the magical red fluid in this vial regains [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. The number of Hit Points depends on the potion's rarity, as shown in the table below.
>
> Whatever the potency, the potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated.
