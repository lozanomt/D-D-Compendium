# Land's Aid

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Land's Aid
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p088-circle-of-the-land.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a use of your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] and choose a point within 60 feet of yourself. Vitality-giving flowers and life-draining thorns appear for a moment in a 10-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on that point. Each creature of your choice in the Sphere must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 2d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. One creature of your choice in that area regains 2d6 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. The damage and healing increase by 1d6 when you reach Druid levels 10 (3d6) and 14 (4d6).
>
