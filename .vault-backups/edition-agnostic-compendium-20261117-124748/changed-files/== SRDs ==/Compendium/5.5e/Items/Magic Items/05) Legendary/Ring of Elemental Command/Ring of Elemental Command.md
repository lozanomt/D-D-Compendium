## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Elemental Command
>
> Ring, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) Each Ring of Elemental [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Command|Command]] is linked to one of the four Elemental Planes. The GM chooses or randomly determines the linked plane. For example, a Ring of Elemental [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Command|Command]] (air) is linked to the Elemental Plane of Air.
>
> Every Ring of Elemental [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Command|Command]] has the following two properties:
>
> Elemental [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Bane|Bane]].  While wearing the ring, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls against Elementals and they have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you.
>
> Elemental [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 4/Compulsion|Compulsion]].  While wearing the ring, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to try to compel an
>
> Elemental you see within 60 feet of yourself. The Elemental makes a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw.
>
> On a failed save, the Elemental has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition until the start your next turn, and you determine what it does with its move and action on its next turn.
>
> Elemental Focus. While wearing the ring, you benefit from additional properties corresponding to the ring's linked Elemental Plane:
>
> Air. You know Auran, you have Resistance to Lightning damage, and you have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] equal to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] and can hover.
>
> Earth.  You know Terran, and you have Resistance to Acid damage. Terrain composed of rubble, rocks, or dirt isn't [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]] for you. In addition, you can move through solid earth or rock as if those areas were [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]] without disturbing the matter through which you pass. If you end your turn in solid earth or rock, you are shunted out to the nearest unoccupied space you last occupied.
>
> Fire. You know Ignan, and you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to Fire damage.
>
> Water. You know Aquan, you gain a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Swim Speed|Swim Speed]] of 60 feet, and you can breathe underwater.
>
> Spellcasting. The ring has 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.
>
> While wearing the ring, you can cast a spell from it. Choose the spell from the list of available spells based on the Elemental Plane the ring is linked to, as shown in the following table. The table indicates how many charges you must expend to cast the spell, which has a save DC of 18.
