# Ring of the Ram

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of the Ram
> *Ring, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p299-ring-of-the-ram.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing it, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and expend 1-3 charges to make a ranged spell [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|attack]] against a creature you can see within 60 feet. The spectral ram attacks with a +7 bonus. On a hit, each expended charge deals 2d10 Force damage and pushes the target 5 feet away.
>
> Alternatively, expend 1-3 charges as a Magic action to try to break a nonmagical object you can see within 60 feet that isn't worn or carried. The ring makes a Strength check with a +5 bonus for each expended charge.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of the Ram
>
> Ring, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to expend 1 to 3 charges to make a ranged spell attack against one creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself. The ring produces a spectral ram's head and makes its attack roll with a +7 bonus. On a hit, for each charge you spend, the target takes 2d10 Force damage and is pushed 5 feet away from you.
>
> Alternatively, you can expend 1 to 3 of the ring's charges as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to try to break a nonmagical object you can see within 60 feet of yourself that isn't being worn or carried. The ring makes a Strength check with a +5 bonus for each charge you spend.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=239|SRD 5.2.1, p. 239]]
