# Winged Boots

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Winged Boots [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/02) Uncommon/Winged Boots|(400 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p328-weapon-of-warning.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> These boots have 4 charges and regain 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to expend 1 charge, gaining a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Fly]] Speed of 30 feet for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
