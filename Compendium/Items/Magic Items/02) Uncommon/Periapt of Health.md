# Periapt of Health

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Periapt of Health
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[dmg-p288-periapt-of-health.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the pendant, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to regain 2d4 + 2 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. This property recharges at the next dawn. In addition, while wearing it you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Periapt of Health
>
> ![[dmg-p288-periapt-of-health.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this pendant, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to regain 2d4 + 2 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> In addition, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition while you wear this pendant.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=234|SRD 5.2.1, p. 234]]
