# Ability Check

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ability Check
>
> An ability check is a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]] that represents using one of the six abilities—or a specific skill associated with an ability—to overcome a challenge. See also "Playing the Game" ("D20 Tests" and "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]]").
