# Investment of the Chain Master

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Investment of the Chain Master
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Warlock/Eldritch Invocations/Pact of the Chain|Pact of the Chain]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> When you cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]*, you infuse the summoned familiar with a measure of your eldritch power, granting the creature the following benefits.
>
> ***Aerial or Aquatic.*** The familiar gains either a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Fly]] Speed or a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed (your choice) of 40 feet.
>
> ***Quick Attack.*** As a Bonus Action, you can command the familiar to take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action.
>
> ***Necrotic or Radiant Damage.*** Whenever the familiar deals Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can make it deal Necrotic or Radiant damage instead.
>
> ***Your Save DC.*** If the familiar forces a creature to make a saving throw, it uses your spell save DC.
>
> ***Resistance.*** When the familiar takes damage, you can take a Reaction to grant it [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] against that damage.
>
