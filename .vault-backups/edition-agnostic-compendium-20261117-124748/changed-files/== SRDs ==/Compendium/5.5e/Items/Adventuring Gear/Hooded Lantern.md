# Hooded Lantern

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hooded Lantern [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Hooded Lantern|(5 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p230-hooded-lantern.png|320]]
>
> A Hooded Lantern burns [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Oil|Oil]] as fuel to cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. As a Bonus Action, you can lower the hood, reducing the light to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.
>
> **Weight:** 2 lb.  
>
