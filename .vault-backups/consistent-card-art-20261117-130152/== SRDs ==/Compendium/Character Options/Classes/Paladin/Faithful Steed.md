# Faithful Steed

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Faithful Steed
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p111-paladin.png|320]]
>
> You can call on the aid of an otherworldly steed. You always have the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Find Steed|Find Steed]]* spell prepared. You can also cast the spell once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
