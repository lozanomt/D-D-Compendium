# Helm of Telepathy

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Helm of Telepathy
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the helm, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Telepathy|Telepathy]] to a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Range|range]] of 30 feet and can cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Detect Thoughts|Detect Thoughts]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Suggestion|Suggestion]] (save DC 13) from it. Once either spell is cast from the helm, the helm can't cast that spell again until the next dawn.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Helm of Telepathy
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this helm, you have telepathy with a range of 30 feet, and you can cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Detect Thoughts|Detect Thoughts]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Suggestion|Suggestion]] (save DC 13) from the helm. Once either spell is cast from the helm, that spell can't be cast from it again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=226|SRD 5.2.1, p. 226]]
