## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Oathbow
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] (Longbow or Shortbow), Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) When you nock an arrow on this bow, it whispers in Elvish, "Swift defeat to my enemies." When you use this weapon to make a ranged attack, you can utter or sign the following command words: "Swift death to you who have wronged me." The target of your attack becomes your sworn enemy until it dies or until dawn 7 days later. You can have only one such sworn enemy at a time. When your sworn enemy dies, you can choose a new one after the next dawn.
>
> When you make a ranged attack roll with this weapon against your sworn enemy, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on the roll. In addition, your target gains no benefit from Half [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] or Three-Quarters [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]], and you suffer no [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] due to long range.
>
> If the attack hits, your sworn enemy takes an extra 3d6 Piercing damage.
>
> While your sworn enemy lives, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls with all other weapons.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=233|SRD 5.2.1, p. 233]]
