# Bestow Curse

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Bestow Curse
> *Level 3 Necromancy (Bard, Cleric, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You touch a creature, which must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become cursed for the duration. Until the curse ends, the target suffers one of the following effects of your choice:
>
> - Choose one ability. The target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on ability checks and saving throws made with that ability.
> - The target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against you.
> - In combat, the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw at the start of each of its turns or be forced to take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Dodge|Dodge]] action on that turn.
> - If you deal damage to the target with an attack roll or a spell, the target takes an extra 1d8 Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell using a level 4 spell slot, you can maintain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] on it for up to 10 minutes. If you use a level 5+ spell slot, the spell doesn't require Concentration, and the duration becomes 8 hours (level 5-6 slot) or 24 hours (level 7-8 slot). If you use a level 9 spell slot, the spell lasts until dispelled.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
