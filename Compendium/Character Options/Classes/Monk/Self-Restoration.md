# Self-Restoration

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Self-Restoration
>
> ![[phb-p103-monk.png|320]]
>
> Through sheer force of will, you can remove one of the following conditions from yourself at the end of each of your turns: [[Charmed|Charmed]], [[Frightened|Frightened]], or [[Poisoned|Poisoned]]. In addition, forgoing food and drink doesn’t give you levels of [[Exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
>

>
