# Animated Rug of Smothering

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Animated Rug of Smothering
>
> *Large Construct, Unaligned*
>
> ![[mm-p019-animated-armor.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 12  
> **Initiative:** +4 (14)  
> **HP:** 27 (5d10)  
> **Speed:** 10 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 17 | +3 | +3 | **Dex** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 10 | +0 | +0 |
> | **Int** | 1 | -5 | -5 | **Wis** | 3 | -4 | -4 | **Cha** | 1 | -5 | -5 |
>
> **Immunities:** Poison, Psychic; [[Charmed|Charmed]], [[Deafened|Deafened]], [[Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[Frightened|Frightened]], [[Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[Petrified|Petrified]], [[Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[Blindsight|Blindsight]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 6  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)  
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Smother.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, the rug can give it the [[Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13) instead of dealing damage. Until the grapple ends, the target has the [[Blinded|Blinded]] and [[Restrained|Restrained]] conditions, is suffocating, and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The rug can smother only one creature at a time.
>
> While grappling the target, the rug can't take this action, the rug halves the damage it takes (round down), and the target takes the same amount of damage.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
