#### Unarmored Defense
***Barbarian.*** While you aren’t wearing any armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers. You can use a [[Equipment/Armor/Shield|Shield]] and still gain this benefit.

***Monk.*** While you aren’t wearing armor or wielding a Shield, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Wisdom modifiers.
