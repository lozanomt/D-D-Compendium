# Opportunity Attacks

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Opportunity Attacks
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You can make an Opportunity Attack when a creature that you can see leaves your reach using its action, its Bonus Action, its Reaction, or one of its speeds. To make the Opportunity Attack, take a Reaction to make one melee attack with a weapon or an [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
