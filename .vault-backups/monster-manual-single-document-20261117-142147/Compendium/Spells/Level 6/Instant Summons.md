# Instant Summons

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Instant Summons
> *Level 6 Conjuration (Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 1 minute or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a sapphire worth 1,000+ GP)  
> **Duration:** Until dispelled  
>
> You touch the sapphire used in the casting and an object weighing 10 pounds or less whose longest dimension is 6 feet or less. The spell leaves an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] mark on that object and invisibly inscribes the object's name on the sapphire. Each time you cast this spell, you must use a different sapphire.
>
> Thereafter, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to speak the object's name and crush the sapphire. The object instantly appears in your hand regardless of physical or planar distances, and the spell ends.
>
> If another creature is holding or carrying the object, crushing the sapphire doesn't transport it, but instead you learn who that creature is and where that creature is currently located.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
