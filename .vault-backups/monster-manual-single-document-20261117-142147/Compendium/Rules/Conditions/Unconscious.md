# Unconscious

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Unconscious
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Unconscious condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Inert.*** You have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] conditions, and you drop whatever you're holding. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.
>
> **Speed:** 0.*** Your  
> **Speed:** is 0 and can't increase.  
>
> ***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]].
>
> ***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
>
> ***Automatic Critical Hits.*** Any attack roll that hits you is a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Critical Hit|Critical Hit]] if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.
>
> ***Unaware.*** You're unaware of your surroundings.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
