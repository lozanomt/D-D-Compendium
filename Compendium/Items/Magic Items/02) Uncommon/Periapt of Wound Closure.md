# Periapt of Wound Closure

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Periapt of Wound Closure
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[dmg-p288-periapt-of-wound-closure.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the pendant, you gain two benefits.
>
> **Life Preservation.** Whenever you make a [[Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throw]], you can change a roll of 9 or lower to a 10, turning a failed save into a successful one.
>
> **Natural Healing Boost.** Whenever you roll a Hit Point Die to regain [[Hit Points|Hit Points]], double the number of Hit Points it restores.
>

>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=287|Dungeon Master's Guide]], [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=288|p. 285 (PDF page 288)]]
