## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Horn of Blasting
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p274-horn-of-blasting.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare You can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to blow the horn, which emits a thunderous blast in a 30-foot [[Cone|Cone]] that is audible out to 600 feet. Each creature in the [[Cone|Cone]] makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 5d8 Thunder damage and has the [[Deafened|Deafened]] condition for 1 minute. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only. Glass or crystal objects in the [[Cone|Cone]] that aren't being worn or carried take 10d8 Thunder damage.
>
> Each use of the horn's magic has a 20 percent chance of causing the horn to explode. The explosion deals 10d6 Force damage to the user and destroys the horn.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=226|SRD 5.2.1, p. 226]]
