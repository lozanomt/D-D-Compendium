# Falling

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Falling
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature that falls takes 1d6 Bludgeoning damage at the end of the fall for every 10 feet it fell, to a maximum of 20d6. When the creature lands, it has the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition unless it avoids taking any damage from the fall.
>
> A creature that falls into water or another liquid can use its Reaction to make a DC 15 Strength ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) or Dexterity ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check to hit the surface head or feet first. On a successful check, any damage resulting from the fall is halved.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
