# Reach

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Reach
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Reach weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it, as well as when determining your reach for [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] with it.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
