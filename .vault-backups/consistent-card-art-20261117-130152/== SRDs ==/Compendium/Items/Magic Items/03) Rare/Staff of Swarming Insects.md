## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Staff of Swarming Insects
>
> Staff, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]] by a Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard) This staff has 10 charges.
>
> Insect Cloud. While holding the staff, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action and expend 1 charge to cause a swarm of harmless flying insects to fill a 30- foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from you. The insects remain for 10 minutes, making the area Heavily
>
> Obscured for creatures other than you. A strong wind (like that created by [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]] ) disperses the swarm and ends the effect.
>
> Spells. While holding the staff, you can cast one of the spells on the following table from it, using your spell save DC and spell attack modifier. The table indicates how many charges you must expend to cast the spell.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Spell|Spell]] Charge Cost [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 4/Giant Insect|Giant Insect]] 4 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 5/Insect Plague|Insect Plague]] 5
>
> Regaining Charges. The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, a swarm of insects consumes and destroys the staff, then disperses.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=246|SRD 5.2.1, p. 246]]
