# Circle of the Land

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Circle of the Land
>
> *Celebrate Connection to the Natural World*
>
> ![[phb-p088-circle-of-the-land.png|320]]
>
> The Circle of the Land comprises mystics and sages who safeguard ancient knowledge and rites. These Druids meet within sacred circles of trees or standing stones to whisper primal secrets in Druidic. The circle’s wisest members preside as the chief priests of their communities.
> #### Level 3: Circle of the Land Spells
> Whenever you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]], choose one type of land: arid, polar, temperate, or tropical. Consult the table below that corresponds to the chosen type; you have the spells listed for your Druid level and lower prepared.
> #### Circle of the Land Spells
> | Druid Level | Arid Land                                        | Polar Land                                             | Temperate Land                                      | Tropical Land                                       |
> | :---------: | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
> |      3      | *[[Blur\|Blur]]*, *[[Burning Hands\|Burning Hands]]*, *[[Fire Bolt\|Fire Bolt]]* | *[[Fog Cloud\|Fog Cloud]]*, *[[Hold Person\|Hold Person]]*, *[[Ray of Frost\|Ray of Frost]]* | *[[Misty Step\|Misty Step]]*, *[[Shocking Grasp\|Shocking Grasp]]*, *[[Sleep\|Sleep]]* | *[[Acid Splash\|Acid Splash]]*, *[[Ray of Sickness\|Ray of Sickness]]*, *[[Web\|Web]]* |
> |      5      | *[[Fireball\|Fireball]]*                                   | *[[Sleet Storm\|Sleet Storm]]*                                      | *[[Lightning Bolt\|Lightning Bolt]]*                                | *[[Stinking Cloud\|Stinking Cloud]]*                                |
> |      7      | *[[Blight\|Blight]]*                                     | *[[Ice Storm\|Ice Storm]]*                                        | *[[Freedom of Movement\|Freedom of Movement]]*                           | *[[Polymorph\|Polymorph]]*                                     |
> |      9      | *[[Wall of Stone\|Wall of Stone]]*                              | *[[Cone of Cold\|Cone of Cold]]*                                     | *[[Tree Stride\|Tree Stride]]*                                   | *[[Insect Plague\|Insect Plague]]*                                 |
> #### Level 3: [[Land's Aid|Land's Aid]]
> As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a use of your [[Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] and choose a point within 60 feet of yourself. Vitality-giving flowers and life-draining thorns appear for a moment in a 10-foot-radius [[Sphere|Sphere]] centered on that point. Each creature of your choice in the Sphere must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 2d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. One creature of your choice in that area regains 2d6 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]]. The damage and healing increase by 1d6 when you reach Druid levels 10 (3d6) and 14 (4d6).
> #### Level 6: [[Natural Recovery|Natural Recovery]]
> You can cast one of the level 1+ spells that you have prepared from your Circle Spells feature without expending a spell slot, and you must finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]] before you do so again.
>
> In addition, when you finish a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]], you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level that is equal to or less than half your Druid level (round up), and none of them can be level 6+. Once you recover spell slots with this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
> #### Level 10: [[Nature's Ward|Nature's Ward]]
> You are immune to the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition, and you have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to a damage type associated with your current land choice in the Circle Spells feature, as shown in the Nature’s Ward table.
> #### Nature's Ward
> | Land Type | Resistance |
> | --------- | ---------- |
> | Arid      | Fire       |
> | Polar     | Cold       |
> | Temperate | Lightning  |
> | Tropical  | Poison     |
> #### Level 14: [[Nature's Sanctuary|Nature's Sanctuary]]
> As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a use of your [[Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] and cause spectral trees and vines to appear in a 15-foot [[Cube|Cube]] on the ground within 120 feet of yourself. They last there for 1 minute or until you have the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition or die. You and your allies have [[Half Cover|Half Cover]] while in that area, and your allies gain the current [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] of your [[Nature's Ward|Nature's Ward]] while there. As a Bonus Action, you can move the Cube up to 60 feet to ground within 120 feet of yourself.
>
