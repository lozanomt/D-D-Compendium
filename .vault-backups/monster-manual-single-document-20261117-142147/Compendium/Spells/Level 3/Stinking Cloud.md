# Stinking Cloud

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Stinking Cloud
> *Level 3 Conjuration (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 90 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a rotten egg)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You create a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] of yellow, nauseating gas centered on a point within range. The cloud is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscured]]. The cloud lingers in the air for the duration or until a strong wind (such as the one created by *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]]*) disperses it.
>
> Each creature that starts its turn in the Sphere must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the end of the current turn. While Poisoned in this way, the creature can't take an action or a Bonus Action.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
