# Antilife Shell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Antilife Shell
> *Level 5 Abjuration (Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> An aura extends from you in a 10-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] for the duration. The aura prevents creatures other than Constructs and Undead from passing or reaching through it. An affected creature can cast spells or make attacks with Ranged or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Reach|Reach]] weapons through the barrier.
>
> If you move so that an affected creature is forced to pass through the barrier, the spell ends.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Antilife Shell
>
> *Level 5 Abjuration (Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Action|Action]]  
>
> **Range:** Self  
>
> **Components:** V, S  
>
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour An aura extends from you in a 10-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] for the duration. The aura prevents creatures other than Constructs and Undead from passing or reaching through it. An affected creature can cast spells Wisdom saving throw to end the effect, as described below.  
>
> Ending the Effect. If the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] creature ends its turn more than 120 feet away from the target, the creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the creature is no longer affected by the target. A creature that successfully saves against this effect is immune to it for 1 minute, after which it can be affected again.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=109|SRD 5.2.1, p. 109–110]]
