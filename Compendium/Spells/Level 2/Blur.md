# Blur

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Blur
> *Level 2 Illusion (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Your body becomes blurred. For the duration, any creature has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against you. An attacker is immune to this effect if it perceives you with [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] or [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]].
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
