# Slippers of Spider Climbing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Slippers of Spider Climbing
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p308-spell-scroll.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing these [[Compendium/Rules/Properties/Light|light]] shoes, you have a Climb [[Speed|Speed]] equal to your Speed and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings while keeping your hands free. The slippers do not allow this movement on slippery surfaces, such as surfaces covered by ice or oil.
>
