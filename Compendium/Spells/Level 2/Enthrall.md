# Enthrall

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Enthrall
> *Level 2 Enchantment (Bard, Warlock)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a Wisdom saving throw. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a -10 penalty to Wisdom ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]]) checks and [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] until the spell ends.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
