# Beacon of Hope

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Beacon of Hope
> *Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Choose any number of creatures within range. For the duration, each target has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom saving throws and[[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Death Saving Throw|Death Saving Throws]] and regains the maximum number of Hit Points possible from any healing.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
