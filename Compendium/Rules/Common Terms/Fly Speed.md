# Fly Speed

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Fly Speed
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A [[Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] can be used to travel through the air.
>
> While you have a [[Fly Speed|Fly Speed]], you can stay aloft until you land, fall, or die. See also  "[[Flying|Flying]]" and "[[Speed|Speed]]."
>
