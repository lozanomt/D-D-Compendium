# Grappling Hook

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Grappling Hook [[Grappling Hook|(2 GP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can throw the Grappling Hook at a railing, a ledge, or another catch within 50 feet of yourself, and the hook catches on if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity ([[Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check. If you tied a [[Rope|Rope]] to the hook, you can then climb it.
>
> **Weight:** 4 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
