# Stone Golem

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Stone Golem
>
> *Large Construct, Unaligned*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p303-stone-golem.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 18  
> **Initiative:** +3 (13)  
> **HP:** 220 (21d10 + 105)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 22 | +6 | +6 | **Dex** | 9 | -1 | -1 | **Con** | 20 | +5 | +5 |
> | **Int** | 3 | -4 | -4 | **Wis** | 11 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 1 | -5 | -5 |
>
> **Immunities:** Poison, Psychic; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 120 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** Understands Common plus two other languages but can't speak  
> **CR:** 10 (XP 5,900; PB +4)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Immutable Form.*** The golem can't shape-shift.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The golem has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The golem makes two attacks, using Slam or Force Bolt in any combination.
>
> ***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Force damage.
>
> ***Force Bolt.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +9, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) Force damage.
>
> #### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Slow (Recharge 5-6).*** The golem casts the *Slow* spell, requiring no spell components and using Constitution as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17).
>
