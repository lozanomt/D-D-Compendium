# Heavy

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Heavy
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it’s a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn’t at least 13 or if it’s a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn’t at least 13.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
