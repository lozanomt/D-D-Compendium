# Pole

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Pole [[Pole|(5 CP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Pole is 10 feet long. You can use it to touch something up to 10 feet away. If you must make a Strength ([[Athletics|Athletics]]) check as part of a High or Long [[Jumping|Jump]], you can use the Pole to vault, giving yourself [[Advantage|Advantage]] on the check.
>
> **Weight:** 7 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
