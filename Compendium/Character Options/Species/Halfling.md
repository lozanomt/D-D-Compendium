# Halfling

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Halfling
>
> ![[phb-p196-halfling.png|320]]
>
> **Creature Type:** Humanoid  
> **Size:** Small (about 2 - 3 feet tall)  
> **Speed:** 30 feet  
>
> As a Halfling, you have these special traits.
>
> ***Brave.*** You have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[Frightened|Frightened]] condition.
>
> ***Halfling Nimbleness.*** You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than you, but you can’t stop in the same space.
>
> ***Luck.*** When you roll a 1 on the d20 of a [[D20 Test|D20 Test]], you can reroll the die, and you must use the new roll.
>
> ***Naturally Stealthy.*** You can take the [[Hide|Hide]] action even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
