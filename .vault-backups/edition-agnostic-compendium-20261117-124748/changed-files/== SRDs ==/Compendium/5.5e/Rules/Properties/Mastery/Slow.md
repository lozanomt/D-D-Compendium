# Slow

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Slow
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn’t exceed 10 feet.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
