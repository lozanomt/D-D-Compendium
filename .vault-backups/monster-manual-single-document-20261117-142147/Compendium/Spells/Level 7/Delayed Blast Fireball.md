# Delayed Blast Fireball

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Delayed Blast Fireball
> *Level 7 Evocation (Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a ball of bat guano and sulfur)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> A beam of yellow light flashes from you, then condenses at a chosen point within range as a glowing bead for the duration. When the spell ends, the bead explodes, and each creature in a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes Fire damage equal to the total accumulated damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> The spell's base damage is 12d6, and the damage increases by 1d6 whenever your turn ends and the spell hasn't ended.
>
> If a creature touches the glowing bead before the spell ends, that creature makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the spell ends, causing the bead to explode. On a successful save, the creature can throw the bead up to 40 feet. If the thrown bead enters a creature's space or collides with a solid object, the spell ends, and the bead explodes.
>
> When the bead explodes, flammable objects in the explosion that aren't being worn or carried start [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Hazards/Burning|burning]].
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The base damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 7.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
