# Spare the Dying

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Spare the Dying
> *Necromancy Cantrip (Cleric, Druid)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 15 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> Choose a creature within range that has 0 Hit Points and isn't [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dead|dead]]. The creature becomes [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Stable|Stable]].
>
> ***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The range doubles when you reach levels 5 (30 feet), 11 (60 feet), and 17 (120 feet).
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
