> ***Light Hammer.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +4 (with Advantage if the target is [[Grappled|Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. or range 20/60 ft. *Hit:* 9 (3d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.
