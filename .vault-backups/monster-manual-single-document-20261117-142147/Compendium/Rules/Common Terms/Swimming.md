# Swimming

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Swimming
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Swim Speed can be used to swim without expending the extra movement normally associated with swimming.
>
> While you’re swimming, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]). You ignore this extra cost if you have a Swim Speed and use it to swim. At the DM’s option, moving any distance in rough water might require a successful DC 15 Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
