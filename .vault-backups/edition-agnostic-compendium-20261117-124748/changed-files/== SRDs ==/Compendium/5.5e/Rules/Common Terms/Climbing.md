# Climbing

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Climbing
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Climb Speed can be used in place of Speed to traverse a vertical surface without expending the extra movement normally associated with climbing.
>
> While you’re climbing, each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot (2 extra feet in [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]). You ignore this extra cost if you have a Climb Speed and use it to climb.
>
> At the DM’s option, climbing a slippery surface or one with few handholds might require a successful DC 15 Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
