# Hypnotic Pattern

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hypnotic Pattern
> *Level 3 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** S, M (a pinch of confetti)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You create a twisting pattern of colors in a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] within range. The pattern appears for a moment and vanishes. Each creature in the area who can see the pattern must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While Charmed, the creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition and a Speed of 0.
>
> The spell ends for an affected creature if it takes any damage or if someone else uses an action to shake the creature out of its stupor.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
