# Insect Plague

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Insect Plague
>
> *Level 5 Conjuration (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 300 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a locust)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> Swarming locusts fill a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on a point you choose within range. The Sphere remains for the duration, and its area is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Lightly Obscured|Lightly Obscured]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]].
>
> When the swarm appears, each creature in it makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 4d10 Piercing damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature also makes this save when it enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 5.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
