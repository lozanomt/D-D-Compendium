# Shield of the Cavalier

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Shield of the Cavalier
> *Armor (Shield), Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While holding the Shield, you gain a +2 bonus to Armor Class in addition to the Shield's normal bonus, and can use these properties.
>
> **Forceful Bash.** When you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, one attack can be a Shield attack against a target within 5 feet. Add your Proficiency Bonus and Strength modifier to the attack roll. A hit deals 2d6 + 2 plus your Strength modifier in Force damage. You can [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Mastery/Push|push]] a creature hit up to 10 feet directly away; if it is your size or smaller, you can also give it the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> **Protective Field.** As a Reaction when you or an ally you can see within 5 feet is targeted by an attack or makes a saving throw against an area of effect, create an immobile 5-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from you. Creatures and objects not fully contained within it are pushed to the nearest unoccupied spaces outside. The triggering attack or area of effect has no effect on creatures and objects inside. The Emanation lasts while you maintain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute. Nothing can pass into or out of it. Creatures and objects inside can't be damaged by attacks or effects originating outside, and creatures inside can't damage anything outside. This property recharges at the next dawn.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Shield of the Cavalier
>
> Armor ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]]), Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While holding this [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]], you have a +2 bonus to
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Armor Class|Armor Class]]. This bonus is in addition to the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]]'s normal bonus to AC.
>
> The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] has the following additional properties that you can use while holding it.
>
> Forceful Bash. When you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] against a target within 5 feet of yourself. Apply your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Proficiency|Proficiency]] Bonus and Strength modifier to the attack roll. On a hit, the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] deals Force damage to the target equal to 2d6 + 2 plus your Strength modifier, and if the target is a creature, you can push it up to 10 feet directly away from yourself. If the creature is your size or smaller, you can also knock it down, giving it the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition.
>
> Protective Field. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]], when you or an ally you can see within 5 feet of you is targeted by an attack or makes a saving throw against an area of effect, you can use the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] to create an immobile 5-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] originating from you. When the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] appears, any creatures or objects not fully contained within it are pushed into the nearest unoccupied spaces outside it. The attack or area of effect that triggered the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Reaction|Reaction]] has no effect on creatures and objects inside the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]], which lasts as long as you maintain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute. Nothing can pass into or out of the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]]. A creature or object inside the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] can't be damaged by attacks or effects originating from outside, nor can a creature inside the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] damage anything outside it. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=243|SRD 5.2.1, p. 243]]
