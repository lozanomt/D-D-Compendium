# Animal Friendship

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Animal Friendship
> *Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a morsel of food)  
> **Duration:** 24 hours  
>
> Target a Beast that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. If you or one of your allies deals damage to the target, the spells ends.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional Beast for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
