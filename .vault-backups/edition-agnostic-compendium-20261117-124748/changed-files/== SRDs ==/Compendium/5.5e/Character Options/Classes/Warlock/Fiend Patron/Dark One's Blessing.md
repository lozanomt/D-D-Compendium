# Dark One's Blessing

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Dark One's Blessing
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p165-fiend-patron.png|320]]
>
> When you reduce an enemy to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], you gain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Temporary Hit Points|Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Charisma modifier plus your Warlock level (minimum of 1 Temporary Hit Point). You also gain this benefit if someone else reduces an enemy within 10 feet of you to 0 Hit Points.
>
