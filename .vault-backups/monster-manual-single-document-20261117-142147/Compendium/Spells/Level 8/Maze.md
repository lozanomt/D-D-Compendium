# Maze

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Maze
> *Level 8 Conjuration (Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> You banish a creature that you can see within range into a labyrinthine demiplane. The target remains there for the duration or until it escapes the maze.
>
> The target can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Study|Study]] action to try to escape. When it does so, it makes a DC 20 Intelligence ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Investigation|Investigation]]) check. If it succeeds, it escapes, and the spell ends.
>
> When the spell ends, the target reappears in the space it left or, if that space is occupied, in the nearest unoccupied space.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
