#### Hunter's Lore
You can call on the forces of nature to reveal certain strengths and weaknesses of your prey. While a creature is marked by your *[[Hunter's Mark]]*, you know whether that creature has any [[Immunity|Immunities]], [[Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistances]], or [[Vulnerability|Vulnerabilities]], and if the creature has any, you know what they are.
