# Flame Strike

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Flame Strike
> *Level 5 Evocation (Cleric)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sulfur)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A vertical column of brilliant fire roars down from above. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foothigh [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cylinder|Cylinder]] centered on a point within range makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d6 Fire damage and 5d6 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Fire damage and the Radiant damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
