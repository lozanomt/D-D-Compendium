# Aura of Life

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Aura of Life
> *Level 4 Abjuration (Cleric, Paladin)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> An aura radiates from you in a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Necrotic damage, and your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximums can't be reduced. If an ally with 0 Hit Points starts its turn in the aura, that ally regains 1 Hit Point.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
