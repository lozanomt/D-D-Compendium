# Blessed Healer

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Blessed Healer
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p076-life-domain.png|320]]
>
> The healing spells you cast on others heal you as well. Immediately after you cast a spell with a spell slot that restores [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to one or more creatures other than yourself, you regain Hit Points equal to 2 plus the spell slot’s level.
>
