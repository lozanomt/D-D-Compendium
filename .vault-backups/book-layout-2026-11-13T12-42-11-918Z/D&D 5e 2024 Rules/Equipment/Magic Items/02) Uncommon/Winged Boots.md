#### Winged Boots [[Winged Boots|(400 GP)]]
*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

**Attunement:** Yes

These boots have 4 charges and regain 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take a [[Magic]] action to expend 1 charge, gaining a [[Flying|Fly]] Speed of 30 feet for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.
