## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Efficient Quiver
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon Each of the quiver's three compartments connects to an extradimensional space that allows the quiver to hold numerous items while never weighing more than 2 pounds. The shortest compartment can hold up to 60 Arrows, Bolts, or similar objects. The midsize compartment holds up to 18 Javelins or similar objects. The longest compartment holds up to 6 long objects, such as bows, Quarterstaffs, or Spears.
>
> You can draw any item the quiver contains as if doing so from a regular quiver or scabbard.
>
