# Hunter's Lore

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Hunter's Lore
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]
>
> You can call on the forces of nature to reveal certain strengths and weaknesses of your prey. While a creature is marked by your *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]]*, you know whether that creature has any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunities]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistances]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Vulnerability|Vulnerabilities]], and if the creature has any, you know what they are.
>
