# Archfey Patron

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Archfey Patron
>
> ![[phb-p163-archfey-patron.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Warlock|Warlock]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Archfey Spells
>
> Always prepare the source’s level-appropriate patron spells, including Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Sleep, Blink, Plant Growth, Dominate Beast, Greater Invisibility, Dominate Person, and Seeming.
>
> #### Level 3: Steps of the Fey
>
> Cast Misty Step without slots Charisma-modifier times per Long Rest, minimum one. Whenever casting it, choose Refreshing Step (1d10 Temporary Hit Points for you or a nearby creature) or Taunting Step (nearby foes save against Disadvantage on attacks against others).
>
> #### Level 6: Misty Escape
>
> Cast Misty Step as a Reaction to taking damage. Steps of the Fey gains Disappearing Step (brief Invisibility) and Dreadful Step (nearby foes save against 2d10 Psychic damage).
>
> #### Level 10: Beguiling Defenses
>
> Gain Immunity to Charmed. A Reaction after being hit by a visible attacker halves the damage and can inflict that much Psychic damage on it on a failed Wisdom save. Refresh on a Long Rest; a Pact Magic slot restores the use.
>
> #### Level 14: Bewitching Magic
>
> Immediately after casting an Enchantment or Illusion spell with an action and a spell slot, cast Misty Step as part of the same action without a slot.
