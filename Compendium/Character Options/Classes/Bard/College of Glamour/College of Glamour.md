# College of Glamour

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### College of Glamour
>
> ![[phb-p068-college-of-glamour.png|320]]
>
> **Class:** [[Bard|Bard]]
>
> A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.
>
> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Beguiling Magic
>
> Always prepare Charm Person and Mirror Image. After spending a spell slot on an Enchantment or Illusion spell, you may force a visible creature within 60 feet to make a Wisdom save or become Charmed or Frightened for up to a minute, repeating the save each turn. Use once per Long Rest; Bardic Inspiration can restore the use.
>
> #### Level 3: Mantle of Inspiration
>
> Spend Bardic Inspiration as a Bonus Action and roll the die. Up to your Charisma modifier in other creatures within 60 feet gain twice the roll in Temporary Hit Points and may use Reactions to move their Speed without Opportunity Attacks.
>
> #### Level 6: Mantle of Majesty
>
> Always prepare Command. Activate a one-minute Concentration mantle with a Bonus Action Command without a slot; while active, cast Command as a Bonus Action without a slot. Creatures Charmed by you automatically fail its save. Refresh on a Long Rest, or restore with a level 3+ slot.
>
> #### Level 14: Unbreakable Majesty
>
> As a Bonus Action, assume a majestic presence for one minute or until Incapacitated. The first time each turn an attacker hits you, it must pass a Charisma save or miss instead. Refresh on a Short or Long Rest.
>
