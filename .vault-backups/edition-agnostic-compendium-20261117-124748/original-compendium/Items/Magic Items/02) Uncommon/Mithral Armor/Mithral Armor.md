## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Mithral Armor
>
> Armor (Any Medium or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Heavy|Heavy]], Except [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Hide|Hide]] Armor), Uncommon
>
> Mithral is a light, flexible metal. Armor made of this substance can be worn under normal clothes. If the armor normally imposes [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.
>
