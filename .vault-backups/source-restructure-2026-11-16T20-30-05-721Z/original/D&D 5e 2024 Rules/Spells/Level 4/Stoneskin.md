# Stoneskin
*Level 4 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Placeholder.png|320]]


**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** [[Concentration]], up to 1 hour

Until the spell ends, one willing creature you touch has [[Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
