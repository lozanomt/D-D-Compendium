# Assassin

**Class:** [[Classes/Rogue/Rogue|Rogue]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Assassinate

Gain Advantage on Initiative. During combat’s first round, gain Advantage against creatures that have not acted; a Sneak Attack hit adds damage equal to Rogue level.

### Level 3: Assassin’s Tools

Gain a Disguise Kit and Poisoner’s Kit with proficiency in both.

### Level 9: Infiltration Expertise

After an hour’s study, mimic someone’s speech or handwriting unerringly. Steady Aim no longer sets your Speed to 0.

### Level 13: Envenom Weapons

Cunning Strike’s Poison also deals 2d6 Poison damage whenever the target fails its save; this damage ignores Poison Resistance.

### Level 17: Death Strike

A first-round Sneak Attack hit forces a Constitution save or doubles the attack’s damage. DC = 8 + Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus.
