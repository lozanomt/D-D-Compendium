# Chain

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Chain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Chain|(5 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Strength ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Athletics|Athletics]]) check. If the creature’s legs are bound, the creature has the Restrained condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 Dexterity ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]]) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a successful DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check as an action.
>
> **Weight:** 10 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
