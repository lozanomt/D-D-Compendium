# Action Surge

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p093-fighter.png|320]]

You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action, except the [[Magic]] action. Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]]. Starting at level 17, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.
