# Stunned

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Stunned
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Stunned condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Incapacitated.*** You have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
>
> ***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
>
> ***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
