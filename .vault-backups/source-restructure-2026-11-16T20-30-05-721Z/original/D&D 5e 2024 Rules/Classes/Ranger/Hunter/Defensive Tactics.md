# Defensive Tactics

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p130-hunter.png|320]]


You gain one of the following feature options of your choice. Whenever you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest]], you can replace the chosen option with the other one.

***Escape the Horde.*** [[Opportunity Attacks]] have [[Disadvantage]] against you.

***Multiattack Defense.*** When a creature hits you with an attack roll, that creature has [[Disadvantage]] on all other attack rolls against you this turn.
