# Second Wind

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Second Wind
>
> ![[phb-p093-fighter.png|320]]
>
> You have a limited well of physical and mental stamina that you can draw on. As a Bonus Action, you can use it to regain [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to 1d10 plus your Fighter level. You can use this feature twice. You regain one expended use when you finish a [[Short Rest|Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]]. When you reach certain Fighter levels, you gain more uses of this feature, as shown in the Second Wind column of the Fighter Features table.
>
> ***Tactical Mind.*** At Fighter level 2, you have a mind for tactics on and off the battlefield. When you fail an ability check, you can expend a use of your Second Wind to push yourself toward success. Rather than regaining [[Hit Points|Hit Points]], you roll 1d10 and add the number rolled to the ability check, potentially turning it into a success. If the check still fails, this use of Second Wind isn’t expended.
>
> ***Tactical Shift.*** At Fighter level 5, whenever you activate your Second Wind with a Bonus Action, you can move up to half your [[Speed|Speed]] without provoking [[Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].
>
