# Light Domain

**Class:** [[Classes/Cleric/Cleric|Cleric]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Light Domain Spells

Always prepare the domain spells as you reach their Cleric levels. The source table includes Burning Hands, Faerie Fire, Scorching Ray, See Invisibility, Daylight, Fireball, Arcane Eye, Wall of Fire, Flame Strike, and Scrying.

### Level 3: Radiance of the Dawn

Spend Channel Divinity as a Magic action: dispel magical Darkness in a 30-foot Emanation. Chosen creatures there make Constitution saves against 2d10 + Cleric level Radiant damage, taking half on success.

### Level 3: Warding Flare

React to a visible creature’s attack within 30 feet, before its hit or miss, to impose Disadvantage. Uses equal Wisdom modifier, minimum one; recover on a Long Rest.

### Level 6: Improved Warding Flare

Recover Warding Flare on Short or Long Rests. Using it can also grant the attack’s target 2d6 + Wisdom modifier Temporary Hit Points.

### Level 17: Corona of Light

A Magic action creates a one-minute sunlight aura: 60 feet of Bright Light and 30 more feet of Dim Light. Enemies in its Bright Light have Disadvantage against Radiance of the Dawn and spells dealing Fire or Radiant damage. Uses equal Wisdom modifier, minimum one, per Long Rest.
