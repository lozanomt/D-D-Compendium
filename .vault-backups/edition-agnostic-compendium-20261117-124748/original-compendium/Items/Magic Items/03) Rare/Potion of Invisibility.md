## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Invisibility
>
> Potion, Rare This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid. When you drink the potion, you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition for 1 hour. The effect ends early if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.
>
