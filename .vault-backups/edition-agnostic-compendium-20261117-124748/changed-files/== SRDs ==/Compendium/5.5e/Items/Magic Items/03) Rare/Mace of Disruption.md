## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Mace of Disruption
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Weapon|Weapon]] (Mace), Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) When you hit a Fiend or an Undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra 2d6 Radiant damage. If the target has 25 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition until the end of your next turn.
>
> Light. While you hold this weapon, it sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet.
