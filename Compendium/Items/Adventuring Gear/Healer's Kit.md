# Healer's Kit

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Healer's Kit [[Healer's Kit|(5 GP)]]
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Healer’s Kit has ten uses. As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can expend one of its uses to [[Stable|stabilize]] an [[Unconscious|Unconscious]] creature that has 0 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] without needing to make a Wisdom ([[Medicine|Medicine]]) check.
>
> **Weight:** 3 lb.  
>
