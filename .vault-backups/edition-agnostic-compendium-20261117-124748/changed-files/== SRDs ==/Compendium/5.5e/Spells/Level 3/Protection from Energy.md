# Protection from Energy

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Protection from Energy
> *Level 3 Abjuration (Cleric, Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to one damage type of your choice: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder.
>
