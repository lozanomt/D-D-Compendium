# Fog Cloud

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fog Cloud
> *Level 1 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> You create a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] of fog centered on a point within range. The Sphere is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]]*) disperses it.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The fog's radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
