# Fear

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fear
> *Level 3 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a white feather)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Each creature in a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cone|Cone]] must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or drop whatever it is holding and have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition for the duration.
>
> A Frightened creature takes the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Dash|Dash]] action and moves away from you by the safest route on each of its turns unless there is nowhere to move. If the creature ends its turn in a space where it doesn't have line of sight to you, the creature makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the spell ends on that creature.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
