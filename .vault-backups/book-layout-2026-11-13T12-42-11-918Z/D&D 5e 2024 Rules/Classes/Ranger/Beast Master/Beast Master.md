# Beast Master

**Class:** [[Classes/Ranger/Ranger|Ranger]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Primal Companion

Summon a Beast of the Land, Sea, or Sky using the source stat blocks. It acts on your turn, normally Dodging unless commanded with your Bonus Action; replacing one Attack-action attack also commands Beast’s Strike. It acts freely if you are Incapacitated. A slot can revive a recently dead beast, and a Long Rest can replace it.

### Level 7: Exceptional Training

When you command its action with a Bonus Action, also command Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help using its Bonus Action. Its attacks can deal Force instead of normal damage.

### Level 11: Bestial Fury

When commanded to use Beast’s Strike, the beast attacks twice. Its first hit each turn against your Hunter’s Mark target adds that spell’s bonus damage as Force.

### Level 15: Share Spells

A spell targeting yourself can also affect your companion if it is within 30 feet.
