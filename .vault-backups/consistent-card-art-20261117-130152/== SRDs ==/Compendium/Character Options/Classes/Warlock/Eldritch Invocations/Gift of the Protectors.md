# Gift of the Protectors

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Gift of the Protectors
> **Prerequisite:** Level 9+ Warlock, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Character Options/Classes/Warlock/Eldritch Invocations/Pact of the Tome|Pact of the Tome]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> A new page appears in your *Book of Shadows* when you conjure it. With your permission, a creature can take an action to write its name on that page, which can contain a number of names equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one name).
>
> When any creature whose name is on the page is reduced to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] but not killed outright, the creature magically drops to 1 Hit Point instead. Once this magic is triggered, no creature can benefit from it until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can erase a name on the page by touching it.
>
