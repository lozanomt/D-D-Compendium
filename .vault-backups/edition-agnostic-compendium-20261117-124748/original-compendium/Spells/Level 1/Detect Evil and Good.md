# Detect Evil and Good

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Detect Evil and Good
> *Level 1 Divination (Cleric, Paladin)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> For the duration, you sense the location of any Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, or Undead within 30 feet of yourself. You also sense whether the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Hallow|Hallow]]* spell is active there and, if so, where.
>
> The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
