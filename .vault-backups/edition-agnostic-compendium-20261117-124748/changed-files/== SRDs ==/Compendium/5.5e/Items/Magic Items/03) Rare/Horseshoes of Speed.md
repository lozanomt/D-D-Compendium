# Horseshoes of Speed

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Horseshoes of Speed [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/03) Rare/Horseshoes of Speed|(4,000 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> These horseshoes come in a set of four. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can touch one of the horseshoes to the hoof of a horse or similar creature, whereupon the horseshoe affixes itself to the hoof. Removing a horseshoe also takes a Magic action.
>
> While all four horseshoes are attached to the same creature, its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is increased by 30 feet.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
