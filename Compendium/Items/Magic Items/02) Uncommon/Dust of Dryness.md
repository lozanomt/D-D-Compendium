## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Dust of Dryness
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon This small packet contains 1d6 + 4 pinches of dust.
>
> As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can sprinkle a pinch of the dust over water, turning up to a 15-foot [[Cube|Cube]] of water into one marble-sized pellet, which floats or rests near where the dust was sprinkled. The pellet's weight is negligible. A creature can take a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action to smash the pellet against a hard surface, causing the pellet to shatter and release the water the dust absorbed. Doing so destroys the pellet and ends its magic.
>
> As a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can sprinkle a pinch of the dust on an Elemental within 5 feet of yourself that is composed mostly of water (such as a Water Elemental). Such a creature exposed to a pinch of the dust makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 10d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=219|SRD 5.2.1, p. 219]]
