## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Eversmoking Bottle
>
> ![[dmg-p262-eversmoking-bottle.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can open or close this bottle.
>
> Opening the bottle causes thick smoke to billow out, forming a cloud that fills a 60-foot [[Emanation|Emanation]] originating from the bottle. The area within the smoke is [[Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscured]].
>
> Each minute the bottle remains open, the size of the [[Emanation|Emanation]] increases by 10 feet until it reaches its maximum size of 120 feet.
>
> Closing the bottle causes the cloud to become fixed in place until it disperses after 10 minutes.
>
> A strong wind (such as that created by the [[Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]] spell) disperses the cloud after 1 minute.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=220|SRD 5.2.1, p. 220–221]]
