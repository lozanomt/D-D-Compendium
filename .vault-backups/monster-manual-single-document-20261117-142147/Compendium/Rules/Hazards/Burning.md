# Burning

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Burning
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A burning creature or object takes 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. As an action, you can extinguish fire on yourself by giving yourself the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
