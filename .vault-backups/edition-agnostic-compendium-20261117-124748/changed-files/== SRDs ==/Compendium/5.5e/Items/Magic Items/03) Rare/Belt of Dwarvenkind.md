# Belt of Dwarvenkind

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Belt of Dwarvenkind [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/03) Rare/Belt of Dwarvenkind|(4,000 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing this belt, you gain the following benefits:
>
> **Dwarvish.** You know Dwarvish.
> **Friend of Dwarvenkind.** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Charisma ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Persuasion|Persuasion]]) checks made to interact with dwarves and duergar.
> **Toughness.** Your Constitution score increases by 2, to a maximum of 20.
>
> In addition, while attuned to the belt, you have a 50 percent chance each day at dawn of growing a full beard if you can grow one, or a thicker beard if you already have one.
>
> If you aren't a dwarf or duergar, you gain the following additional benefits while wearing the belt:
>
> **Darkvision.** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] with a range of 60 feet.
> **Resilience.** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
