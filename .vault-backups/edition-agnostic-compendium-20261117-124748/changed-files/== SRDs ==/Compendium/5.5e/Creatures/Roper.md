> ### Roper

>

>
> **AC:** 20  
> **Initiative:** +5 (15)  
> **HP:** 93 (11d10 + 33)

>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> | **Int** | 7 | -2 | -2 | **Wis** | 16 | +3 | +3 | **Cha** | 6 | -2 | -2 |

> **Skills:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] +6, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +5  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 16  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Spider Climb.*** The roper can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The roper makes two Tentacle attacks, uses Reel, and makes two Bite attacks.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 17 (3d8 + 4) Piercing damage.
>
> ***Tentacle.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 60 ft. *Hit:* The target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 14) from one of six tentacles, and the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition until the grapple ends.
>
> The tentacle can be damaged, freeing a creature it has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] when destroyed (AC 20, HP 10, Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage). Damaging the tentacle deals no damage to the roper, and a destroyed tentacle regrows at the start of the roper's next turn.
>
> ***Reel.*** The roper pulls each creature [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] by it up to 30 feet straight toward it.
>
