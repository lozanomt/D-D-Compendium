# Greater Restoration

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Greater Restoration
> *Level 5 Abjuration (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You touch a creature and magically remove one of the following effects from it:
>
> - 1 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level
> - The [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]] condition
> - A curse, including the target's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]] to a cursed magic item
> - Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores
> - Any reduction to the target's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Point]] maximum
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
