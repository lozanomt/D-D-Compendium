# Remorhaz

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Remorhaz
> *Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>
> ![[mm-p260-remorhaz.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 17  
> **Initiative:** +5 (15)  
> **HP:** 195 (17d12 + 85)  
> **Speed:** 40 ft., [[Burrowing|Burrow]] 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 24 | +7 | +7 | **Dex** | 13 | +1 | +1 | **Con** | 21 | +5 | +5 |
> | **Int** | 4 | -3 | -3 | **Wis** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Cha** | 5 | -3 | -3 |
>
> **Immunities:** Cold, Fire  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft., [[Tremorsense|Tremorsense]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 10  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 11 (XP 7,200; PB +4)  
>
> **Habitat**Arctic
> **Treasure** None
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Heat Aura.*** At the end of each of the remorhaz's turns, each creature in a 5-foot [[Emanation|Emanation]] originating from the remorhaz takes 16 (3d10) Fire damage.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (2d10 + 7) Piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) Fire damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 17), and it has the [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition until the grapple ends.
>
> #### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Swallow.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 19, one Large or smaller creature [[Grappled|Grappled]] by the remorhaz (it can have up to two creatures swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The target is swallowed by the remorhaz, and the Grappled condition ends. A swallowed creature has the [[Blinded|Blinded]] and [[Restrained|Restrained]] conditions, it has [[Total Cover|Total Cover]] against attacks and other effects outside the remorhaz, and it takes 10 (3d6) Acid damage plus 10 (3d6) Fire damage at the start of each of the remorhaz's turns.
>
> If the remorhaz takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the remorhaz must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, each of which falls in a space within 5 feet of the remorhaz and has the [[Prone|Prone]] condition. If the remorhaz dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the [[Restrained|Restrained]] condition and can escape from the corpse by using 15 feet of movement, exiting Prone.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
