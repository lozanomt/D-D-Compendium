# Relentless Rage

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Relentless Rage
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p053-barbarian.png|320]]
>
> Your Rage can keep you fighting despite grievous wounds. If you drop to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] while your Rage is active and don’t die outright, you can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. If you succeed, your Hit Points instead change to a number equal to twice your Barbarian level.
>
> Each time you use this feature after the first, the DC increases by 5. When you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], the DC resets to 10.
>
