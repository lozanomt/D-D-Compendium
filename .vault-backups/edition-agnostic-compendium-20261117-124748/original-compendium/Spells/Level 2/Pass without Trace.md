# Pass without Trace

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Pass without Trace
> *Level 2 Abjuration (Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (ashes from burned mistletoe)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 hour  
>
> You radiate a concealing aura in a 30-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Dexterity ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Stealth|Stealth]]) checks and leave no tracks.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
