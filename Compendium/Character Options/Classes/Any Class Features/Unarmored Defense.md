# Unarmored Defense

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Unarmored Defense
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> ***Barbarian.*** While you aren’t wearing any armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers. You can use a [[Compendium/Items/Armor/Shield|Shield]] and still gain this benefit.
>
> ***Monk.*** While you aren’t wearing armor or wielding a Shield, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Wisdom modifiers.
