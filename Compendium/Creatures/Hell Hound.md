# Hell Hound

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Hell Hound
> *Medium Fiend, Lawful Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p167-hell-hound.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 15  
> **Initiative:** +1 (11)  
> **HP:** 58 (9d8 + 18)  
> **Speed:** 50 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 17 | +3 | +3 | **Dex** | 12 | +1 | +1 | **Con** | 14 | +2 | +2 |
> | **Int** | 6 | -2 | -2 | **Wis** | 13 | +1 | +1 | **Cha** | 6 | -2 | -2 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Perception|Perception]] +5  
> **Immunities:** Fire  
> **Senses:** [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 15  
> **Languages:** Understands Infernal but can't speak  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> **Habitat** Mountain, Planar (Hell), Underdark
> **Treasure** None
>
> #### Traits
> ***Pack Tactics.*** The hound has [[Advantage|Advantage]] on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hound's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally doesn't have the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
> #### Actions
> ***Multiattack.*** The hound makes two Bite attacks.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) Fire damage.
>
> ***Fire Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 15-foot [[Cone|Cone]]. *Failure:* 17 (5d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>
