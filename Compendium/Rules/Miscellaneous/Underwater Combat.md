# Underwater Combat

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Underwater Combat
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A fight underwater follows these rules.
>
> #### Impeded Weapons
> When making a melee attack roll with a weapon underwater, a creature that lacks a [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the attack roll unless the weapon deals Piercing damage.
>
> A ranged attack roll with a weapon underwater automatically misses a target beyond the weapon’s normal range, and the attack roll has Disadvantage against a target within normal range.
> #### Fire Resistance
> Anything underwater has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Fire damage.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
