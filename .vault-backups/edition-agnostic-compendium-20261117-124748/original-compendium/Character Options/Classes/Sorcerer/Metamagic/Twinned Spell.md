# Twinned Spell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Twinned Spell
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p141-sorcerer.png|320]]
>
> **Cost:** 1 Sorcery Points  
>
> When you cast a spell, such as *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Charm Person|Charm Person]]*, that can be cast with a higher-level spell slot to target an additional creature, you can spend 1 Sorcery Point to increase the spell’s effective level by 1.
>
