# Divination

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Divination
> *Level 4 Divination (Cleric, Druid, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action or [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Ritual|Ritual]]  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (incense worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> This spell puts you in contact with a god or a god's servants. You ask one question about a specific goal, event, or activity to occur within 7 days. The GM offers a truthful reply, which might be a short phrase or cryptic rhyme. The spell doesn't account for circumstances that might change the answer, such as the casting of other spells.
>
> If you cast the spell more than once before finishing a [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
