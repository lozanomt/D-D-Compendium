# Crawler Mucus

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Crawler Mucus [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Poison/Crawler Mucus|(200 GP)]]
> *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Poison/Contact|Contact]] Poison*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Crawler Mucus must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 minute. The creature also has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] condition while Poisoned in this way. The creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
>
