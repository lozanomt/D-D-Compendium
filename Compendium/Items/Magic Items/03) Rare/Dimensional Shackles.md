## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Dimensional Shackles
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p257-dragon-scale-mail.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare You can take a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action to place these shackles on a creature that has the [[Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
>
> The shackles adjust to fit a creature of Small to
>
> Large size. The shackles prevent a creature bound by them from using any method of extradimensional movement, including teleportation or travel to a different plane of existence. They don't prevent the creature from passing through an interdimensional portal.
>
> You and any creature you designate when you use the shackles can take a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action to remove them. Once every 30 days, the bound creature can make a DC 30 Strength (Athletics) check. On a successful check, the creature breaks free and destroys the shackles.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=218|SRD 5.2.1, p. 218]]
