## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Wings of Flying
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this cloak, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to turn the cloak into a pair of wings on your back. The wings lasts for 1 hour or until you end the effect early as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action. The wings give you a
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Fly Speed|Fly Speed]] of 60 feet. If you are aloft when the wings disappear, you fall. When the wings disappear, you can't use them again for 1d12 hours.
>
