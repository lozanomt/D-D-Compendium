# Shield of Missile Attraction

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Shield of Missile Attraction
> *Armor (Shield), Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p307-shield-of-missile-attraction.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While holding the Shield, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to damage from attacks made with Ranged weapons.
>
> **Curse.** Attuning to the Shield [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Curses|curses]] you until you are targeted by [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 3/Remove Curse|Remove Curse]] or similar [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|magic]]. Removing the Shield does not end the curse. Whenever an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|attack]] with a Ranged weapon targets a creature within 10 feet of you, you become the target instead.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Shield of Missile Attraction
>
> Armor ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]]), Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While holding this [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]], you have Resistance to damage from attacks made with Ranged weapons.
>
> Curse. This [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you are targeted by a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 3/Remove Curse|Remove Curse]] spell or similar magic. Removing the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] fails to end the curse on you. Whenever an attack with a Ranged weapon targets a creature within 10 feet of you, the curse causes you to become the target instead.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=243|SRD 5.2.1, p. 243]]
