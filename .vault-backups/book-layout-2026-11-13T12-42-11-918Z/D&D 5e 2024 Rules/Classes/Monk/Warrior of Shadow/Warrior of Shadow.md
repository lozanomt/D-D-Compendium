# Warrior of Shadow

**Class:** [[Classes/Monk/Monk|Monk]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Shadow Arts

Spend 1 Focus Point to cast Darkness without components; see through your own casting and reposition its area at each turn’s start. Gain 60-foot Darkvision, or extend existing Darkvision by 60 feet, and learn Minor Illusion using Wisdom.

### Level 6: Shadow Step

From Dim Light or Darkness, teleport as a Bonus Action up to 60 feet to a visible unoccupied space also in Dim Light or Darkness. Gain Advantage on your next melee attack before the turn ends.

### Level 11: Improved Shadow Step

Spend 1 Focus Point with Shadow Step to ignore its lighting requirements and make an Unarmed Strike immediately after teleporting as part of that Bonus Action.

### Level 17: Cloak of Shadows

A Magic action and 3 Focus Points while in Dim Light or Darkness grant a one-minute shadow form. Gain Invisible, pass through occupied spaces as Difficult Terrain, and use Flurry of Blows without Focus cost. Ends on Incapacitation or ending a turn in Bright Light; consult source for shunting if ending in an occupied space.
