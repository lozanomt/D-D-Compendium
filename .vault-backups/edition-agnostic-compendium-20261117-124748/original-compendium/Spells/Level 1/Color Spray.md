# Color Spray

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Color Spray
> *Level 1 Illusion (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colorful sand)  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colorful light. Each creature in a 15-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cone|Cone]] originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of your next turn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
