# Wild Resurgence

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wild Resurgence
>
> ![[phb-p081-druid.png|320]]
>
> Once on each of your turns, if you have no uses of [[Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] left, you can give yourself one use by expending a spell slot (no action required). In addition, you can expend one use of Wild Shape (no action required) to give yourself a level 1 spell slot, but you can’t do so again until you finish a [[Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>

>
