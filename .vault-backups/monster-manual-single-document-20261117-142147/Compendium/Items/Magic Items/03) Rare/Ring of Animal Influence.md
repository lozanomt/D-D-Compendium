> Ring, Rare This ring has 3 charges, and it regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save DC 13) from it:
>
> - [[Animal Friendship|Animal Friendship]]
>
> - [[Fear|Fear]] (affects Beasts only)
>
> - [[Speak with Animals|Speak with Animals]]
