# Lightly Obscured

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Lightly Obscured
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You have Disadvantage on Wisdom ([[Perception|Perception]]) checks to see something in a Lightly Obscured space.
