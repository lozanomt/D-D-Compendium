# Holy Aura

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Holy Aura
> *Level 8 Abjuration (Cleric)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a reliquary worth 1,000+ GP)  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> For the duration, you emit an aura in a 30-foot [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]]. While in the aura, creatures of your choice have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on all saving throws, and other creatures have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against them. In addition, when a Fiend or an Undead hits an affected creature with a melee attack roll, the attacker must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the end of its next turn.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
