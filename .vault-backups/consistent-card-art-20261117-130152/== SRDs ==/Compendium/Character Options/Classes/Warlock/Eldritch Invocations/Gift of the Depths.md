# Gift of the Depths

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Gift of the Depths
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You can breathe underwater, and you gain a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Swimming|Swim]] Speed equal to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]].
>
> You can also cast *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 3/Water Breathing|Water Breathing]]* once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
