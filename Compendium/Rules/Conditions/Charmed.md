# Charmed

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Charmed
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Charmed condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Can't Harm the Charmer.*** You can't [[Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|attack]] the charmer or target the charmer with damaging abilities or magical effects.
>
> ***Social Advantage.*** The charmer has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on any ability check to interact with you socially.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
