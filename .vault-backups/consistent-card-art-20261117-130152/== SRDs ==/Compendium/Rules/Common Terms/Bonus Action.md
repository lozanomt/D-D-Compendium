# Bonus Action

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Bonus Action
>
> A [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] is a special action that you can take on the same turn that you take an action. You can't take more than one [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] on a turn, and you have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]] to take only if a rule explicitly says so. See also "Playing the Game" ("Actions").
>
