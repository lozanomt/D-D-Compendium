## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Crystal Ball of Mind Reading
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While touching this crystal orb, you can cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Scrying|Scrying]] (save DC 17) with it. In addition, you can cast [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 2/Detect Thoughts|Detect Thoughts]] (save DC 17) targeting creatures you can see within 30 feet of the spell's sensor. You don't need to concentrate on this [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 2/Detect Thoughts|Detect Thoughts]]  spell to maintain it during its duration, but it ends if the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Scrying|Scrying]] spell ends.
>
