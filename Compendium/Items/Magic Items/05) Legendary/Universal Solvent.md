# Universal Solvent

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Universal Solvent
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p321-universal-solvent.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> A found tube contains 1d6 + 1 ounces. Take a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action to pour one or more ounces onto a surface within [[Reach|reach]]. Each ounce instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including [[Sovereign Glue|Sovereign Glue]].
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Universal Solvent
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p321-universal-solvent.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. When found, a tube contains 1d6 + 1 ounces.
>
> You can take a [[Utilize|Utilize]] action to pour 1 or more ounces of solvent from the tube onto a surface within reach. Each ounce instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including Sovereign Glue.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=250|SRD 5.2.1, p. 250]]
