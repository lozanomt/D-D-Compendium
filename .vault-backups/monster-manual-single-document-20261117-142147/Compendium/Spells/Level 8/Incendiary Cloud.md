# Incendiary Cloud

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Incendiary Cloud
> *Level 8 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> A swirling cloud of embers and smoke fills a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on a point within range. The cloud's area is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscured]]. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (like that created by *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]]*) disperses it.
>
> When the cloud appears, each creature in it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d8 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature must also make this save when the Sphere moves into its space and when it enters the Sphere or ends its turn there. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
>
> The cloud moves 10 feet away from you in a direction you choose at the start of each of your turns.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
