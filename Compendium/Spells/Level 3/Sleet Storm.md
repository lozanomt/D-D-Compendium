# Sleet Storm

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sleet Storm
> *Level 3 Conjuration (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a miniature umbrella)  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> Until the spell ends, sleet falls in a 40-foot-tall, 20-foot-radius [[Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cylinder|Cylinder]] centered on a point you choose within range. The area is [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Heavily Obscured|Heavily Obscured]], and exposed flames in the area are doused.
>
> Ground in the Cylinder is [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]]. When a creature enters the Cylinder for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or have the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition and lose [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]].
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
