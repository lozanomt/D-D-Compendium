> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Combat Inspiration
>
> A recipient of your Bardic Inspiration can add its roll to the damage of an attack after hitting, or use a Reaction when hit to add its roll to AC against that attack.
>
> #### Level 3: Martial Training
>
> Gain Martial weapon proficiency and Medium armor and Shield training. Simple or Martial weapons may serve as a focus for spells from your Bard list.
>
> #### Level 6: Extra Attack
>
> Attack twice with the Attack action. One attack may instead be replaced by a cantrip with an action casting time.
>
> #### Level 14: Battle Magic
>
> After casting a spell with an action casting time, make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
