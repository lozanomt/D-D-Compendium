# Chime of Opening

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Chime of Opening
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p247-circlet-of-blasting.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> This hollow metal tube is about 1 foot long and weighs 1 pound. As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, strike it to cast [[Knock|Knock]]. Instead of the spell's normal knocking sound, the chime rings clearly and is audible up to 300 feet. It can be used 10 times; after the tenth use it cracks and becomes useless.
>

>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf#page=247|Dungeon Master's Guide]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Chime of Opening
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p247-circlet-of-blasting.png|320]]
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare This hollow metal tube measures about 1 foot long and weighs 1 pound. As a [[Magic|Magic]] action, you can strike the chime to cast [[Knock|Knock]]. The spell's customary knocking sound is replaced by the clear, ringing tone of the chime, which is audible out to 300 feet.
>
> The chime can be used 10 times. After the tenth time, it cracks and becomes useless.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=215|SRD 5.2.1, p. 215]]
