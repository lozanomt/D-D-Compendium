# Ritual Adept

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Ritual Adept
>
> ![[phb-p167-wizard.png|320]]
>
> You can cast any spell as a [[Ritual|Ritual]] if that spell has the Ritual tag and the spell is in your spellbook. You needn’t have the spell prepared, but you must read from the book to cast a spell in this way.
>

>
