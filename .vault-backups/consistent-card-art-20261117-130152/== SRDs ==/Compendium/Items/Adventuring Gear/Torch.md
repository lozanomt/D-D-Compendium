# Torch

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Torch [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Items/Adventuring Gear/Torch|(1 CP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Torch burns for 1 hour, casting [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 20 feet. When you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can attack with the Torch, using it as a Simple Melee weapon. On a hit, the target takes 1 Fire damage.
>
> **Weight:** 1 lb.  
>
