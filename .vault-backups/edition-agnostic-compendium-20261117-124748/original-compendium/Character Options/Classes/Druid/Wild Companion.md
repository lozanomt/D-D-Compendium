# Wild Companion

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wild Companion
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p081-druid.png|320]]
>
> You can summon a nature spirit that assumes an animal form to aid you. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a spell slot or a use of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] to cast the *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]* spell without Material components. When you cast the spell in this way, the familiar is Fey and disappears when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Wild Companion
>
> You can summon a nature spirit that assumes an animal form to aid you. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a spell slot or a use of Wild Shape to cast the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Find Familiar|Find Familiar]] spell without Material components.
>
> When you cast the spell in this way, the familiar is Fey and disappears when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=43|SRD 5.2.1, p. 43]]
