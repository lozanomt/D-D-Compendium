# Blinded

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Blinded
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> While you have the Blinded condition, you experience the following effects.
>
> ***Can't See.*** You can't see and automatically fail any ability check that requires sight.
>
> ***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]], and your attack rolls have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]].
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
