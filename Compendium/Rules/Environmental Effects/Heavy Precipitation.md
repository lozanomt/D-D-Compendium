# Heavy Precipitation

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Heavy Precipitation
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Everything within an area of heavy rain or heavy snowfall is [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Lightly Obscured|Lightly Obscured]], and creatures in the area have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on all Wisdom ([[Compendium/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]]) checks. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
