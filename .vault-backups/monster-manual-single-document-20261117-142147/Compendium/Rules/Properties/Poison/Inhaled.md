# Inhaled

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Inhaled
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Poisonous powders and gases take effect when inhaled. Blowing the powder or releasing the gas subjects creatures in a 5-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] to its effect. The resulting cloud dissipates immediately afterward. Holding one’s breath is ineffective against inhaled poisons, as they affect nasal membranes, tear ducts, and other parts of the body.
>
