# Countercharm

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Countercharm
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p061-bard.png|320]]
>
> You can use musical notes or words of power to disrupt mind-influencing effects. If you or a creature within 30 feet of you fails a saving throw against an effect that applies the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition, you can take a Reaction to cause the save to be rerolled, and the new roll has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]].
>
