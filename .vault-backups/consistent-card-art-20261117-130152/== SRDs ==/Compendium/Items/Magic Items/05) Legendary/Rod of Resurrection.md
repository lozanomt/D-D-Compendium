## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Rod of Resurrection
>
> Rod, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]])
>
> The rod has 5 charges. While you hold it, you can cast one of the following spells from it: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 6/Heal|Heal]] (expends 1 charge) or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 7/Resurrection|Resurrection]] (expends 5 charges).
>
> The rod regains 1 expended charge daily at dawn.
>
> If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the rod disappears in a harmless burst of radiance.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=242|SRD 5.2.1, p. 242]]
