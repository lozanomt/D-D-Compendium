# Bugbear Stalker

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Bugbear Stalker
>
> *Medium Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p064-bugbear-stalker.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 15  
> **Initiative:** +2 (12)  
> **HP:** 65 (10d8 + 20)  
> **Speed:** 30 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 17 | +3 | +3 | **Dex** | 14 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 14 | +2 | +4 |
> | **Int** | 11 | +0 | +0 | **Wis** | 12 | +1 | +3 | **Cha** | 11 | +0 | +0 |
>
> **Skills:**  
> **Stealth:** +6, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Survival|Survival]] +3  
> **Gear:** Chain Shirt, Javelins (6), Morningstar  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 11  
> **Languages:** Common, Goblin  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn't spend extra movement to move a creature it is grappling.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes two Javelin or Morningstar attacks.
>
> ***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 10 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 13 (3d6 + 3) Piercing damage.
>
> ***Morningstar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 (with Advantage if the target is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage.
>
> #### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, one Medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13).
>
