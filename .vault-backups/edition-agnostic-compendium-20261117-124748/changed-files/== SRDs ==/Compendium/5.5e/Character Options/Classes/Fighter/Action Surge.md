# Action Surge

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Action Surge
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p093-fighter.png|320]]
>
> You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action, except the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action. Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. Starting at level 17, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.
>
