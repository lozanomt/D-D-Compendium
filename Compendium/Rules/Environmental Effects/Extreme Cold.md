# Extreme Cold

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Extreme Cold
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When the temperature is 0 degrees Fahrenheit (-17 degrees Celsius) or lower, a creature exposed to the extreme cold must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw at the end of each hour or gain 1 [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]] level. Creatures that have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] or [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Immunity|Immunity]] to Cold damage automatically succeed on the save.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
