# Ball Bearings

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Ball Bearings [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Ball Bearings|(1 GP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action, you can spill Ball Bearings from their pouch. They spread to cover a level, 10-foot-square area within 10 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Ball Bearings.
>
> **Weight:** 2 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
