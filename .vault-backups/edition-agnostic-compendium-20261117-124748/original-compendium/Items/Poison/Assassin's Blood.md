# Assassin's Blood

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Assassin's Blood [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Poison/Assassin's Blood|(150 GP)]]
> *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Poison/Ingested|Ingested]] Poison*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Assassin’s Blood makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 6 (1d12) Poison damage and has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.
