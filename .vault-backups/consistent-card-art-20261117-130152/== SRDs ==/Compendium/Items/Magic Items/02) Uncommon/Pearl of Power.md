# Pearl of Power

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Pearl of Power
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes, by a spellcaster  
>
> While the pearl is on your person, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to regain one expended spell slot of level 3 or lower. Once used, the pearl can't be used again until the next dawn.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Pearl of Power
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]] by a Spellcaster) While this pearl is on your person, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to regain one expended spell slot of level 3 or lower. Once you use the pearl, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
