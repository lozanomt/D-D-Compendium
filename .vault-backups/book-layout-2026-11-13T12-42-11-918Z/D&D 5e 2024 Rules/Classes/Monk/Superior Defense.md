#### Superior Defense
At the start of your turn, you can expend 3 Focus Points to bolster yourself against harm for 1 minute or until you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition. During that time, you have [[Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to all damage except Force damage.
