# Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals
> *Wondrous Item, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p244-bracers-of-defense.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> While within 5 feet of the brazier, take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to summon a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Creatures/Fire Elemental|Fire Elemental]] in an unoccupied space as close to the brazier as possible. It understands your languages, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count. It disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a Bonus Action. The brazier can't summon another elemental until the next dawn.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare While you are within 5 feet of this brazier, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to summon a Fire Elemental .
>
> The elemental appears in an unoccupied space as close to the brazier as possible, understands your languages, obeys your commands, and takes its turn immediately after you on your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count. The elemental disappears after 1 hour, when it dies, or when you dismiss it as a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]]. The brazier can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=214|SRD 5.2.1, p. 214]]
