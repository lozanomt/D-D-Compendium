# Cloak of Arachnida

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Cloak of Arachnida
> *Wondrous Item, Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing this black silk cloak, you gain the following benefits.
>
> - **Poison [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]].** You have Resistance to Poison damage.
> - **[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Spider Climb|Spider Climb]].** You have a Climb [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] equal to your Speed and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings while leaving your hands free.
> - **[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Creatures/Spider#Monster Manual|Spider]] Walk.** You can't be caught in webs of any sort, and move through webs as though they were [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]].
> - **Web.** You can cast Web (save DC 13). Its web fills twice its normal area. This property recharges at the next dawn.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Cloak of Arachnida
>
> Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) This fine garment is made of black silk interwoven with faint, silvery threads. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits.
>
> Poison Resistance. You have Resistance to Poison damage.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 2/Spider Climb|Spider Climb]]. You have a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Climb Speed|Climb Speed]] equal to your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.
>
> Spider Walk. You can't be caught in webs of any sort and can move through webs as if they were [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]].
>
> Web. You can cast Web (save DC 13). The web created by the spell fills twice its normal area. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=215|SRD 5.2.1, p. 215]]
