# Magic

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Magic
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> When you take the Magic action, you cast a spell that has a casting time of an action or use a feature or magic item that requires a Magic action to be activated.
>
> If you cast a spell that has a casting time of 1 minute or longer, you must take the Magic action on each turn of that casting, and you must maintain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]] while you do so. If your Concentration is broken, the spell fails, but you don’t expend a spell slot.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
