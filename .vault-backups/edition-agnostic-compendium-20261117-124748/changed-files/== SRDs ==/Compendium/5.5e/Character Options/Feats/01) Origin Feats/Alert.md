# Alert

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Alert
> *Origin Feat*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You gain the following benefits.
>
> **Initiative:** Proficiency.*** When you roll [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], you can add your Proficiency Bonus to the roll.  
>
> **Initiative:** Swap.*** Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your  
> **Initiative:** with the  
> **Initiative:** of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
