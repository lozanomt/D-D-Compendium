# Wholeness of Body

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Wholeness of Body
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p110-warrior-of-the-open-hand.png|320]]
>
> You gain the ability to heal yourself. As a Bonus Action, you can roll your Martial Arts die. You regain a number of [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] equal to the number rolled plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1 Hit Point regained). You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
