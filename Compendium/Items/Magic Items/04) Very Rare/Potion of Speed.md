## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Speed
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Potion, Very Rare When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [[Haste|Haste]] spell for 1 minute (no [[Concentration|Concentration]] required) without suffering the wave of lethargy that typically occurs when the effect ends.
>
> This potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own.
>
