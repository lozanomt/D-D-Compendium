# Surprise

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Surprise
>
> If a creature is caught unawares by the start of combat, that creature is surprised, which causes it to have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] roll. See also "Playing the Game" ("Combat").
>
