# Wind Fan

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Wind Fan [[Wind Fan|(400 GP)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p328-weapon-of-warning.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> While holding this fan, you can cast *[[Gust of Wind|Gust of Wind]]* (save DC 13) from it. Each subsequent time the fan is used before the next dawn, it has a cumulative 20 percent chance of not working; if the fan fails to work, it tears into useless, nonmagical tatters.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
