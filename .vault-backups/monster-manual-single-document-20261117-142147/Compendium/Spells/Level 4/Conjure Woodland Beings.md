# Conjure Woodland Beings

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Conjure Woodland Beings
> *Level 4 Conjuration (Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> You conjure nature spirits that flit around you in a 10-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Areas of Effect/Emanation|Emanation]] for the duration. Whenever the Emanation enters the space of a creature you can see and whenever a creature you can see enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a Wisdom saving throw. The creature takes 5d8 Force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature makes this save only once per turn.
>
> In addition, you can take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Actions/Disengage|Disengage]] action as a Bonus Action for the spell's duration.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 4.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
