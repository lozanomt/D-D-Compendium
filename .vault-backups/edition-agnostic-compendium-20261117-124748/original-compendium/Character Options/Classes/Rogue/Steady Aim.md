# Steady Aim

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Steady Aim
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]
>
> As a Bonus Action, you give yourself [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on your next attack roll on the current turn. You can use this feature only if you haven’t moved during this turn, and after you use it, your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] is 0 until the end of the current turn.
>
