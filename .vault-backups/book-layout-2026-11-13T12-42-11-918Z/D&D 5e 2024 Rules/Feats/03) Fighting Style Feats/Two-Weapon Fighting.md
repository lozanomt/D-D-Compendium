#### Two-Weapon Fighting
*Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature)*

When you make an extra attack as a result of using a weapon that has the [[Rules/Properties/Light|Light]] property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren’t already adding it to the damage.
