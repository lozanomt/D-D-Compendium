# Unarmored Movement

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Unarmored Movement
>
> ![[phb-p103-monk.png|320]]
>
> Your speed increases by 10 feet while you aren’t wearing armor or wielding a [[Compendium/Items/Armor/Shield|Shield]]. This bonus increases when you reach certain Monk levels, as shown on the Monk Features table.
>
> ***Acrobatic Movement.*** At Monk level 9, while you aren’t wearing armor or wielding a Shield, you gain the ability to move along vertical surfaces and across liquids on your turn without falling during the movement.
>
