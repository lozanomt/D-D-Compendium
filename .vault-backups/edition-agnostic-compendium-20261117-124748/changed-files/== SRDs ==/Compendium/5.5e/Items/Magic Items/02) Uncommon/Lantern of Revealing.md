## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Lantern of Revealing
>
> Wondrous Item, Uncommon While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern's [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]]. You can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Utilize|Utilize]] action to lower the hood, reducing the lantern's light to [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] in a 5-foot radius.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=228|SRD 5.2.1, p. 228–229]]
