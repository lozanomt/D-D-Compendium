# Fire Shield

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fire Shield
> *Level 4 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of phosphorus or a firefly)  
> **Duration:** 10 minutes  
>
> Wispy flames wreathe your body for the duration, shedding [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet.
>
> The flames provide you with a warm shield or a chill shield, as you choose. The warm shield grants you [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Cold damage, and the chill shield grants you Resistance to Fire damage.
>
> In addition, whenever a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack roll, the shield erupts with flame. The attacker takes 2d8 Fire damage from a warm shield or 2d8 Cold damage from a chill shield.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf#page=277|Player's Handbook]]
