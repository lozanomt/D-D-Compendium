# Armor Training

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Armor Training
>
> Armor training allows you to use armor of a certain category without the following drawbacks. If you wear Light, Medium, or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Heavy|Heavy]] armor and lack training with it, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]] that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can't cast spells. If you use a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Shield|Shield]] and lack training with it, you don't gain its AC bonus. See also  "[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]" and "Equipment" ("Armor").
>
