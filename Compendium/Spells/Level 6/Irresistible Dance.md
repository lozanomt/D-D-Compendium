# Irresistible Dance

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Irresistible Dance
> *Level 6 Enchantment (Bard, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> One creature that you can see within range must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a successful save, the target dances comically until the end of its next turn, during which it must spend all its movement to dance in place.
>
> On a failed save, the target has the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]] condition for the duration. While Charmed, the target dances comically, must use all its movement to dance in place, and has [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Dexterity saving throws and attack rolls, and other creatures have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls against it. On each of its turns, the target can take an action to collect itself and repeat the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
