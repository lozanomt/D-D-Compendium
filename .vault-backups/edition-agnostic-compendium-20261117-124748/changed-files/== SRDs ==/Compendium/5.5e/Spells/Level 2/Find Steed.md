# Find Steed

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Find Steed
> *Level 2 Conjuration (Paladin)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 30 feet  
> **Components:** V, S  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> You summon an otherworldly being that appears as a loyal steed in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. This creature uses the **Otherworldly Steed** stat block. If you already have a steed from this spell, the steed is replaced by the new one.
>
> The steed resembles a Large, rideable animal of your choice, such as a horse, a camel, a dire wolf, or an elk. Whenever you cast the spell, choose the steed's creature type — Celestial, Fey, or Fiend which determines certain traits in the stat block.
>
> ***Combat.*** The steed is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, it shares your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count, and it functions as a controlled mount while you ride it (as defined in the rules on mounted combat). If you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition, the steed takes its turn immediately after yours and acts independently, focusing on protecting you.
>
> ***Disappearance of the Steed.*** The steed disappears if it drops to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] or if you die. When it disappears, it leaves behind anything it was wearing or carrying. If you cast this spell again, you decide whether you summon the steed that disappeared or a different one.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
>
> [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Creatures/Otherworldly Steed|no-title clean]]
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
