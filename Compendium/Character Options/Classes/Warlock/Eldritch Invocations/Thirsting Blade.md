# Thirsting Blade

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Thirsting Blade
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock, [[Pact of the Blade|Pact of the Blade]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You gain the [[Extra Attack|Extra Attack]] feature for your pact weapon only. With that feature, you can attack twice with the weapon instead of once when you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
>
