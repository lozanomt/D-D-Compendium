# Path of the World Tree

**Class:** [[Classes/Barbarian/Barbarian|Barbarian]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Vitality of the Tree

Entering Rage grants Temporary Hit Points equal to your Barbarian level. At each turn’s start during Rage, another creature within 10 feet can gain d6s of Temporary Hit Points equal to your Rage Damage bonus; these vanish when Rage ends.

### Level 6: Branches of the Tree

While raging, react when a visible creature starts its turn within 30 feet. A failed Strength save teleports it beside you; you may then set its Speed to 0 for that turn. DC = 8 + Strength modifier + Proficiency Bonus.

### Level 10: Battering Roots

On your turn, Heavy or Versatile Melee weapons gain 10 feet of reach and can apply Push or Topple in addition to a different mastery property.

### Level 14: Travel Along the Tree

Teleport up to 60 feet when entering Rage and with Bonus Actions during it. Once per Rage, extend one teleport to 150 feet and bring up to six willing creatures within 10 feet, placing them near your destination.
