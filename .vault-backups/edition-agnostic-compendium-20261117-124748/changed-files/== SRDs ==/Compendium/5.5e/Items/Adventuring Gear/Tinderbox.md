# Tinderbox

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Tinderbox [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Tinderbox|(5 SP)]]
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A Tinderbox is a small container holding flint, fire steel, and tinder (usually dry cloth soaked in light oil) used to kindle a fire. Using it to light a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Candle|Candle]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Lamp|Lamp]], Lantern, or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Adventuring Gear/Torch|Torch]] - or anything else with exposed fuel - takes a Bonus Action. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.
>
> **Weight:** 1 lb.  
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Tinderbox
>
> A Tinderbox is a small container holding flint, fire steel, and tinder (usually dry cloth soaked in light oil) used to kindle a fire. Using it to light a Candle, Lamp, Lantern, or Torch—or anything else with exposed fuel—takes a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]]. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=100|SRD 5.2.1, p. 100]]
