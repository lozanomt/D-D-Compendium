# Wand of Enemy Detection

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Wand of Enemy Detection
> *Wand, Rare*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p322-wand-of-enemy-detection.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The [[Wand|wand]] has 7 charges. While holding it, take a [[Magic|Magic]] action and expend 1 charge. For 1 minute, you know the direction, but not the distance, of the nearest creature Hostile to you within 60 feet. It detects Hostile creatures that are [[Invisible|Invisible]], ethereal, disguised, hidden, or in plain sight. The effect ends if you stop holding the wand.
>
> It regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend its last charge, roll 1d20; on a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Wand of Enemy Detection
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p322-wand-of-enemy-detection.png|320]]
>
> Wand, Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]])
>
> This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can take a [[Magic|Magic]] action to expend 1 charge. For 1 minute, you know the direction of the nearest creature [[Hostile|Hostile]] to you within 60 feet, but not its distance from you. The wand can sense the presence of [[Hostile|Hostile]] creatures that are [[Invisible|Invisible]], ethereal, disguised, or hidden, as well as those in plain sight. The effect ends if you stop holding the wand.
>
> Regaining Charges. The wand regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=250|SRD 5.2.1, p. 250]]
