# Extra Attack

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Extra Attack
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action on your turn, when a class or subclass grants this feature.
>
> - **Fighter:** three attacks at Fighter level 11, and four at Fighter level 20.
> - **Warlock:** [[Thirsting Blade|Thirsting Blade]] grants Extra Attack for the pact weapon only. [[Devouring Blade|Devouring Blade]] requires Warlock level 12 and Thirsting Blade, and increases this to three pact-weapon attacks.
> - **Multiclassing:** Extra Attack features from different classes do not add together. Thirsting Blade does not grant additional attacks on top of another source of Extra Attack. See [[Multiclassing|Multiclassing]].
>
> Some subclasses permit replacing an attack with a cantrip. Follow the relevant subclass’s rule; this is not a general benefit of Extra Attack.
