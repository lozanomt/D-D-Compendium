# Regenerate

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Regenerate
> *Level 7 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Druid)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 1 minute  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a prayer wheel)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> A creature you touch regains 4d8 + 15 [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. For the duration, the target regains 1 Hit Point at the start of each of its turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
