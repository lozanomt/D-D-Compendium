# Midnight Tears

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Midnight Tears [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Poison/Midnight Tears|(1,500 GP)]]
> *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Poison/Ingested|Ingested]] Poison*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature that ingests Midnight Tears suffers no effect until the stroke of midnight. Any effect that ends the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition neutralizes this poison. If the poison hasn’t been neutralized before midnight, the creature makes a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 31 (9d6) Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
>
