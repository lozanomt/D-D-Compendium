# Foresight

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Foresight
> *Level 9 Divination (Bard, Druid, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** 1 minute  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a hummingbird feather)  
> **Duration:** 8 hours  
>
> You touch a willing creature and bestow a limited ability to see into the immediate future. For the duration, the target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Tests]], and other creatures have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls against it. The spell ends early if you cast it again.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
