# Ring of Evasion

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Evasion
> *Ring, Rare*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p296-ring-of-evasion.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you fail a Dexterity saving throw while wearing it, take a Reaction and expend 1 charge to succeed on that save instead.
>
