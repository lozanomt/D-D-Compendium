## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Staff of the Python
>
> Staff, Uncommon (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can throw this staff so that it lands in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of you, causing the staff to become a Giant Constrictor
>
> Snake in that space. The snake is under your control and shares your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] count, taking its turn immediately after yours.
>
> On your turn, you can mentally command the snake (no action required) if it is within 60 feet of you and you don't have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
>
> You decide what action the snake takes and where it moves during its turn, or you can issue it a general command, such as to attack your enemies or guard a location. Absent commands from you, the snake defends itself.
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]], you can command the snake to revert to staff form in its current space, and you can't use the staff's property again for 1 hour. If the snake is reduced to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], it dies and reverts to its staff form; the staff then shatters and is destroyed. If the snake reverts to staff form before losing all its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], it regains all of them.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=247|SRD 5.2.1, p. 247]]
