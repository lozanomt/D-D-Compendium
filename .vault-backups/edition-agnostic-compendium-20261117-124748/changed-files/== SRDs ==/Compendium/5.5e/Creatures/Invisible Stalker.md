# Invisible Stalker

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Invisible Stalker
>
> *Large Elemental, Neutral*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p182-invisible-stalker.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 14  
> **Initiative:** +7 (22)  
> **HP:** 97 (13d10 + 26)  
> **Speed:** 50 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Flying|Fly]] 50 ft. (hover)  
>

> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|

> | **Int** | 10 | +0 | +0 | **Wis** | 15 | +2 | +2 | **Cha** | 11 | +0 | +0 |
>
> **Skills:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] +8, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Stealth|Stealth]] +10  
> **Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 18  
> **Languages:** Common, Primordial (Auran)  
> **CR:** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Air Form.*** The stalker can enter an enemy's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
>
> ***Invisibility.*** The stalker has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Invisible|Invisible]] condition.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The stalker makes three Wind Swipe attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Vortex.
>
> ***Wind Swipe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Force damage.
>
> ***Vortex.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 14, one Large or smaller creature in the stalker's space. *Failure:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Thunder damage, and the target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 13). Until the grapple ends, the target can't cast spells with a Verbal component and takes 7 (2d6) Thunder damage at the start of each of the stalker's turns.
>
