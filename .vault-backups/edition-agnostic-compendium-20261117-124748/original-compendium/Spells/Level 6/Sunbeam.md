# Sunbeam

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sunbeam
> *Level 6 Evocation (Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a magnifying glass)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 1 minute  
>
> You launch a sunbeam in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Line|Line]]. Each creature in the Line makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d8 Radiant damage and has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition until the start of your next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.
>
> Until the spell ends, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to create a new Line of radiance.
>
> For the duration, a mote of brilliant radiance shines above you. It sheds [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 30 feet. This light is sunlight.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
