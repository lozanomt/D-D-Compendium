> #### Features
>
> #### Level 3: Dazzling Footwork
>
> While unarmored and without a Shield: Advantage on dancing Performance checks; AC = 10 + Dexterity + Charisma; spending Bardic Inspiration as part of an action, Bonus Action, or Reaction permits an Unarmed Strike as part of it. Unarmed attacks can use Dexterity and deal a Bardic Inspiration die + Dexterity in Bludgeoning damage without expending that die.
>
> #### Level 6: Inspiring Movement
>
> When a visible enemy ends its turn within 5 feet, spend a Reaction and Bardic Inspiration to move half your Speed. An eligible ally may also move half its Speed using its Reaction; neither movement provokes Opportunity Attacks.
>
> #### Level 6: Tandem Footwork
>
> When rolling Initiative while not Incapacitated, spend Bardic Inspiration and add its roll to your Initiative and that of allies within 30 feet who can see or hear you.
>
> #### Level 14: Leading Evasion
>
> For Dexterity saves that normally halve damage, take none on success and half on failure. Share this with creatures within 5 feet making the same save. Unavailable while Incapacitated.
