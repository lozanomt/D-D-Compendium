# Glabrezu

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Glabrezu
>
> *Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil*
>
> ![[mm-p140-glabrezu.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 17  
> **Initiative:** +6 (16)  
> **HP:** 189 (18d10 + 90)  
> **Speed:** 40 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 20 | +5 | +9 | **Dex** | 15 | +2 | +2 | **Con** | 21 | +5 | +9 |
> | **Int** | 19 | +4 | +4 | **Wis** | 17 | +3 | +7 | **Cha** | 16 | +3 | +7 |
>
> **Skills:** [[Deception|Deception]] +7, [[Perception|Perception]] +7  
> **Resistances:** Cold, Fire, Lightning  
> **Immunities:** Poison; [[Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[Truesight|Truesight]] 120 ft.; [[Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 17  
> **Languages:** Abyssal; [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Telepathy|Telepathy]] 120 ft.  
> **CR:** 9 (XP 5,000; PB +4)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Demonic Restoration.*** If the glabrezu dies outside the Abyss, its body dissolves into ichor, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Abyss.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The glabrezu has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The glabrezu makes two Pincer attacks and uses Pummel or Spellcasting.
>
> ***Pincer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [[Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 15) from one of two pincers.
>
> ***Pummel.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature [[Grappled|Grappled]] by the glabrezu. *Failure:* 15 (3d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. *Success:* Half damage.
>
> ***Spellcasting.*** The glabrezu casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):
>
> - **At Will:** *Darkness*, *Detect Magic*, *Dispel Magic*
> - **1/Day Each:** *Confusion*, *Fly*, *Power Word Stun*
>
