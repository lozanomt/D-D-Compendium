# Fiendish Resilience

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Fiendish Resilience
>
> ![[phb-p165-fiend-patron.png|320]]
>
> Choose one damage type, other than Force, whenever you finish a [[Short Rest|Short]] or [[Long Rest|Long Rest]]. You have [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to that damage type until you choose a different one with this feature.
