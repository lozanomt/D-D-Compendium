# Tactical Master

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Tactical Master
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p093-fighter.png|320]]
>
> When you attack with a weapon whose mastery property you can use, you can replace that property with the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Mastery/Push|Push]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Mastery/Sap|Sap]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Mastery/Slow|Slow]] property for that attack.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Tactical Master
>
> When you attack with a weapon whose mastery property you can use, you can replace that property with the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Properties/Mastery/Push|Push]], Sap, or Slow property for that attack.
>
