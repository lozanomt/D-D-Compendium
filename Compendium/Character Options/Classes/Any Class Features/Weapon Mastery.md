# Weapon Mastery

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Weapon Mastery
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> Weapon Mastery depends on the class that grants it. Use the class’s own progression table for the number of mastered weapon kinds.
>
> | Class | Initial choices | Eligible weapons | Replacement after a Long Rest |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [[Barbarian\|Barbarian]] | 2 | Simple or Martial Melee weapons | Change one choice |
> | [[Fighter\|Fighter]] | 3 | Simple or Martial weapons | Change one choice |
> | [[Paladin\|Paladin]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
> | [[Ranger\|Ranger]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
> | [[Rogue\|Rogue]] | 2 | Weapons with which you have proficiency | Change weapon choices |
>
> Barbarian and Fighter gain more choices at the levels shown in their class tables. Ranged weapons are eligible where the class’s restrictions allow them.
