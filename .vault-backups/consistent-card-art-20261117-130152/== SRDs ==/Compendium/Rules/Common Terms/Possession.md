# Possession

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Possession
>
> Some effects cause a creature to be possessed by another creature or entity. A possessing effect defines how the possession operates. [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Possession|Possession]] can be prevented by the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 1/Protection from Evil and Good|Protection from Evil and Good]] spell and ended by the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Spells/Level 5/Dispel Evil and Good|Dispel Evil and Good]] spell.
>
