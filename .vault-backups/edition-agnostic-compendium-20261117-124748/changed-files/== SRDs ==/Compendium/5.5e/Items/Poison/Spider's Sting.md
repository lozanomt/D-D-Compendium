# Spider's Sting

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Spider's Sting
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> #### Spider’s Sting [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Poison/Spider's Sting|(200 GP)]]
> *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Properties/Poison/Injury|Injury]] Poison*
>
> A creature subjected to Spider’s Sting must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour. If the creature fails the save by 5 or more, the creature also has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition while Poisoned in this way. The creature wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
>
