# Spike Growth

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Spike Growth
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Druid, Ranger)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 150 feet  
> **Components:** V, S, M (seven thorns)  
> **Duration:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> The ground in a 20-foot-radius [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] centered on a point within range sprouts hard spikes and thorns. The area becomes [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]] for the duration. When a creature moves into or within the area, it takes 2d4 Piercing damage for every 5 feet it travels.
>
> The transformation of the ground is camouflaged to look natural. Any creature that can't see the area when the spell is cast must take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Search|Search]] action and succeed on a Wisdom ([[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Survival|Survival]]) check against your spell save DC to recognize the terrain as hazardous before entering it.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
