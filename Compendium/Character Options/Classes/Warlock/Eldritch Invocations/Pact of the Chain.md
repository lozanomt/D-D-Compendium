# Pact of the Chain

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Pact of the Chain
>
> ![[phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You learn the *[[Find Familiar|Find Familiar]]* spell and can cast it as a [[Magic|Magic]] action without expending a spell slot.
>
> When you cast the spell, you choose one of the normal forms for your familiar or one of the following special forms: [[Imp#Monster Manual|Imp]], [[Pseudodragon#Monster Manual|Pseudodragon]], [[Quasit#Monster Manual|Quasit]], [[Skeleton#Monster Manual|Skeleton]], [[Slaad Tadpole|Slaad Tadpole]], [[Sphinx of Wonder#Monster Manual|Sphinx of Wonder]], [[Sprite#Monster Manual|Sprite]], or [[Venomous Snake#Monster Manual|Venomous Snake]].
>
> Additionally, when you take the [[Attack|Attack]] action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to allow your familiar to make one attack of its own with its Reaction.
>

>

## SRD 5.2.1
