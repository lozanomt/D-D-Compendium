# Trickery Domain

![[D&D 5e 2024 Rules/z. Load Bearing Folders/Attachments/Artwork/Players Handbook/phb-p078-trickery-domain.png|320]]


**Class:** [[Classes/Cleric/Cleric|Cleric]]

A handbook reference summary. Use the linked PDF for complete wording, spell/resource tables, targeting restrictions, and exceptions.

## Features

### Level 3: Blessing of the Trickster

A Magic action grants you or a willing creature within 30 feet Advantage on Stealth checks until your next Long Rest or reuse.

### Level 3: Invoke Duplicity

Use Channel Divinity as a Bonus Action to create a one-minute intangible duplicate within 30 feet. Cast spells from its space using your own senses, move it with Bonus Actions, and gain Advantage against a creature that can see the illusion when both you and the illusion are within 5 feet of it. Ends if dismissed or you are Incapacitated.

### Level 3: Trickery Domain Spells

Always prepare the spells in the domain table as you reach its levels, including Charm Person, Disguise Self, Invisibility, Pass without Trace, Hypnotic Pattern, Nondetection, Confusion, Dimension Door, Dominate Person, and Modify Memory.

### Level 6: Trickster’s Transposition

When using the Bonus Action to create or move your duplicate, you may teleport by exchanging places with it.

### Level 17: Improved Duplicity

Your allies also gain Advantage against creatures within 5 feet of the duplicate. When the illusion ends, you or a creature near it regains Hit Points equal to your Cleric level.
