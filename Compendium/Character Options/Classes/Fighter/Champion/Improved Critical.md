# Improved Critical

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Improved Critical
>
> ![[phb-p099-champion.png|320]]
>
> Your attack rolls with weapons and [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strikes]] can score a [[Critical Hit|Critical Hit]] on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.
>
> ***Superior Critical.*** At Fighter level 15, your attack rolls with weapons and Unarmed Strikes can now score a Critical Hit on a roll of 18-20 on the d20.
>
