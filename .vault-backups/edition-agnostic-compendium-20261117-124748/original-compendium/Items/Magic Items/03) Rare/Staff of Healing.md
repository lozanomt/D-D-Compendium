# Staff of Healing

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Staff of Healing [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Items/Magic Items/03) Rare/Staff of Healing|(4,000 GP)]]
> *Staff, Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p312-staff-of-frost.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** By a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Bard/Bard|Bard]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Cleric/Cleric|Cleric]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Druid|Druid]]  
>
> This staff has 10 charges. While holding the staff, you can cast one of the spells on the following table from it, using your spellcasting ability modifier. The table indicates how many charges you must expend to cast the spell.
>
> | Spell                    |                       Charge Cost                        |
> | ------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------: |
> | *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 1/Cure Wounds\|Cure Wounds]]*        | 1 charge per spell level (maximum 4 for a level 4 spell) |
> | *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 2/Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]]* |                            2                             |
> | *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 5/Mass Cure Wounds\|Mass Cure Wounds]]*   |                            5                             |
> ***Regaining Charges.*** The staff regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]]. If you expend the last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the staff vanishes in a flash of light, lost forever.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
