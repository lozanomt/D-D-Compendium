## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Robe of Eyes
>
> Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]])
>
> This robe is adorned with eyelike patterns. While you wear the robe, you gain the following benefits:
>
> All-Around Vision.  The robe gives you [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
>
> Special Senses.  You have Darkvision and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]], both with a range of 120 feet.
>
> Drawbacks. A Light spell cast on the robe or a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 3/Daylight|Daylight]] spell cast within 5 feet of the robe gives you the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of your turns, you make a Constitution saving throw (DC 11 for Light or DC 15 for [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 3/Daylight|Daylight]]), ending the condition on yourself on a success.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=240|SRD 5.2.1, p. 240]]
