# Stroke of Luck

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Stroke of Luck
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p131-rogue.png|320]]
>
> You have a marvelous knack for succeeding when you need to. If you fail a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/D20 Test|D20 Test]], you can turn the roll into a 20.
>
> Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
