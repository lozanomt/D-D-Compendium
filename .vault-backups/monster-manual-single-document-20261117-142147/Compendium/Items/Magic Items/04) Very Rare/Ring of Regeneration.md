# Ring of Regeneration

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Regeneration
> *Ring, Very Rare*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p297-ring-of-regeneration.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing the ring, you regain 1d6 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] every 10 minutes if you have at least 1 Hit Point. A lost body part regrows to full functionality after 1d6 + 1 days if you have at least 1 Hit Point the entire time.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Ring of Regeneration
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Dungeon Master's Guide/Artwork/dmg-p297-ring-of-regeneration.png|320]]
>
> Ring, Very Rare (Requires [[Attunement|Attunement]]) While wearing this ring, you regain 1d6 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] every 10 minutes if you have at least 1 Hit Point. If you lose a body part, the ring causes the missing part to regrow and return to full functionality after 1d6 + 1 days if you have at least 1 Hit Point the whole time.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=238|SRD 5.2.1, p. 238]]
