# True Seeing

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### True Seeing
> *Level 6 Divination (Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** Touch  
> **Components:** V, S, M (mushroom powder worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)  
> **Duration:** 1 hour  
>
> For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [[Compendium/Rules/Senses/Truesight|Truesight]] with a range of 120 feet.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
