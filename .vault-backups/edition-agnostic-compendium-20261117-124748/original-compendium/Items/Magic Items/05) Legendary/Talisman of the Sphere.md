## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Talisman of the Sphere
>
> Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Attunement|Attunement]]) While holding or wearing this talisman, you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on any Intelligence (Arcana) check you make to control a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] of Annihilation. In addition, when you start your turn in control of a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Sphere|Sphere]] of Annihilation, you can take a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action to move it 10 feet plus a number of additional feet equal to 10 times your Intelligence modifier. This movement doesn't have to be in a straight line.
>
