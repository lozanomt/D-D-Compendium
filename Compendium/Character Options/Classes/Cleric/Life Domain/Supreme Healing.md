# Supreme Healing

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Supreme Healing
>
> ![[phb-p076-life-domain.png|320]]
>
> When you would normally roll one or more dice to restore [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature with a spell or [[Channel Divinity|Channel Divinity]], don’t roll those dice for the healing; instead use the highest number possible for each die.

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Supreme Healing
>
> ![[phb-p076-life-domain.png|320]]
>
> When you would normally roll one or more dice to restore [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature with a spell or Channel Divinity, don't roll those dice for the healing; instead use the highest number possible for each die.
>
> For example, instead of restoring 2d6 [[Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature with a spell, you restore 12.
>
> **Source:** [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=40|SRD 5.2.1, p. 40]]
