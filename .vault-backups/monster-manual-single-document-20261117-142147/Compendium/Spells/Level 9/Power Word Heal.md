# Power Word Heal

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Power Word Heal
> *Level 9 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric)*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 60 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** Instantaneous  
>
> A wave of healing energy washes over one creature you can see within range. The target regains all its [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]]. If the creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Stunned|Stunned]] condition, the condition ends. If the creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition, it can use its Reaction to stand up.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
