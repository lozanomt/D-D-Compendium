# Blindness + Deafness

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Blindness + Deafness
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> #### Blindness / Deafness
> *Level 2 Transmutation (Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard)*
>
> **Casting Time:** Action  
> **Range:** 120 feet  
> **Components:** V  
> **Duration:** 1 minute  
>
> One creature that you can see within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or it has the [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] or [[Compendium/Rules/Conditions/Deafened|Deafened]] condition (your choice) for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
