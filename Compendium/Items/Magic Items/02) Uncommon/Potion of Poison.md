# Potion of Poison

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Potion of Poison
> *Potion, Uncommon*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** No  
>
> The potion looks, smells, and tastes like a [[Potion of Healing|Potion of Healing]] or another beneficial potion; illusion [[Magic|magic]] masks its true [[Nature|nature]]. [[Identify|Identify]] reveals the [[Deception|deception]]. Drinking it deals 4d6 Poison damage and requires a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, you have the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 1 hour.
>
