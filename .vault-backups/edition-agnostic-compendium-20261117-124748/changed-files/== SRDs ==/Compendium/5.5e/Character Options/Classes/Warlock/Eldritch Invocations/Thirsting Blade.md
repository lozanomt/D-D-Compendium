# Thirsting Blade

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Thirsting Blade
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock, [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Warlock/Eldritch Invocations/Pact of the Blade|Pact of the Blade]] Invocation*  
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p155-warlock.png|320]]
>
> You gain the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Extra Attack|Extra Attack]] feature for your pact weapon only. With that feature, you can attack twice with the weapon instead of once when you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
>

## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Thirsting Blade
>
> **Prerequisite:** Level 5+ Warlock, Pact of the Blade Invocation You gain the Extra [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] feature for your pact weapon only.
