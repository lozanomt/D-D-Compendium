# Wand of Polymorph

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Wand of Polymorph
> *Wand, Very Rare*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Attunement:** Yes, by a spellcaster  
>
> The [[Wand|wand]] has 7 charges. While holding it, expend 1 charge to cast [[Polymorph|Polymorph]] (save DC 15). It regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend its last charge, roll 1d20; on a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
>
