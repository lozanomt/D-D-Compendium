# Belt of Giant Strength

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Belt of Giant Strength
>
> ![[dmg-p239-belt-of-giant-strength.png|320]]
>
> #### Belt of Gaint Strength [[Belt of Giant Strength|(Varies)]]
> *Wondrous Item, Rarity Varies*
>
> **Attunement:** Yes  
>
> While wearing this belt, your Strength changes to a score granted by the belt. The type of giant determines the score (see the table below). The item has no effect on you if your Strength without the belt is equal to or greater than the belt’s score.
>
> | Belt                                                                     | Str, | Rarity    |
> | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----: | --------- |
> | *[[Belt of Hill Giant Strength\|Belt of Hill Giant Strength]]*                                        | 21   | Rare      |
> | *[[Belt of Frost Giant Strength\|Belt of Frost Giant Strength]]* or *[[Belt of Stone Giant Strength\|Belt of Stone Giant Strength]]* | 23   | Very Rare |
> | *[[Belt of Fire Giant Strength\|Belt of Fire Giant Strength]]*                                        | 25   | Very Rare |
> | *[[Belt of Cloud Giant Strength\|Belt of Cloud Giant Strength]]*                                       | 27   | Legendary |
> | *[[Belt of Storm Giant Strength\|Belt of Storm Giant Strength]]*                                       | 29   | Legendary |
>
