# Sacred Weapon

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Sacred Weapon
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p116-oath-of-devotion.png|320]]
>
> When you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action, you can expend one use of your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Paladin/Channel Oath|Channel Divinity]] to imbue one Melee weapon that you are holding with positive energy. For 10 minutes or until you use this feature again, you add your Charisma modifier to attack rolls you make with that weapon (minimum bonus of +1), and each time you hit with it, you cause it to deal its normal damage type or Radiant damage. The weapon also emits [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] 20 feet beyond that. You can end this effect early (no action required). This effect also ends if you aren’t carrying the weapon.
>
