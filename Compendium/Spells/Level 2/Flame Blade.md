# Flame Blade

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Flame Blade
> *Level 2 Evocation (Druid, Sorcerer)*
>
> ![[Sources/5.5e/Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> **Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
> **Range:** Self  
> **Components:** V, S, M (a sumac leaf)  
> **Duration:** [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], up to 10 minutes  
>
> You evoke a fiery blade in your free hand. The blade is similar in size and shape to a scimitar, and it lasts for the duration. If you let go of the blade, it disappears, but you can evoke it again as a Bonus Action.
>
> As a [[Compendium/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can make a melee spell attack with the fiery blade. On a hit, the target takes Fire damage equal to 3d6 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.
>
> The flaming blade sheds [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Bright Light|Bright Light]] in a 10-foot radius and [[Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Dim Light|Dim Light]] for an additional 10 feet.
>
> ***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 2.
>
> **Source:** [[Sources/5.5e/Player's Handbook/D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
