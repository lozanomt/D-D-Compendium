> [!statblock]
> ### Mummy
> *Medium or Small Undead, Lawful Evil*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p221-mummy.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 11  
> **Initiative:** -1 (9)  
> **HP:** 58 (9d8 + 18)  
> **Speed:** 20 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 16 | +3 | +3 | **Dex** | 8 | -1 | -1 | **Con** | 15 | +2 | +2 |
> | **Int** | 6 | -2 | -2 | **Wis** | 12 | +1 | +3 | **Cha** | 12 | +1 | +1 |
>
> **Vulnerabilities:** Fire  
> **Immunities:** Necrotic, Poison; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Charmed|Charmed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Exhaustion|Exhaustion]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Poisoned|Poisoned]]  
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Darkvision|Darkvision]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 11  
> **Languages:** Common plus two other languages  
> **CR:** 3 (XP 700; PB +2)  
>
> **Habitat** Desert, Swamp
> **Treasure** Relics
>
> #### Actions
> ***Multiattack.*** The mummy makes two Rotting Fist attacks and uses Dreadful Glare.
>
> ***Rotting Fist.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it is [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Curses|cursed]]. While cursed, the target can't regain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]], its Hit Point maximum doesn't return to normal when finishing a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], and its Hit Point maximum decreases by 10 (3d6) every 24 hours that elapse. A creature dies and turns to dust if reduced to 0 Hit Points by this attack.
>
> ***Dreadful Glare.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 11, one creature the mummy can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened]] condition until the end of the mummy's next turn. *Success:* The target is immune to this mummy's Dreadful Glare for 24 hours.
>
