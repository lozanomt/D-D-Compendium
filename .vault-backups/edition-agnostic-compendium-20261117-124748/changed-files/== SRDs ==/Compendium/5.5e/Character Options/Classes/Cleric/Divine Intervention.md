# Divine Intervention

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Divine Intervention
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p071-cleric.png|320]]
>
> You can call on your deity or pantheon to intervene on your behalf. As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, choose any Cleric spell of level 5 or lower that doesn’t require a Reaction to cast. As part of the same action, you cast that spell without expending a spell slot or needing Material [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Components|components]]. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> At Cleric level 20, you can call on even more powerful divine intervention. When you use your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Cleric/Divine Intervention|Divine Intervention]] feature, you can choose *[[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Spells/Level 9/Wish|Wish]]* when you select a spell. If you do so, you can't use Divine Intervention again until you finish 2d4 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rests]].
>
