## SRD 5.2.1

> [!info]
> ### Supreme Healing
>
> When you would normally roll one or more dice to restore [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature with a spell or Channel Divinity, don't roll those dice for the healing; instead use the highest number possible for each die.
>
> For example, instead of restoring 2d6 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] to a creature with a spell, you restore 12.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/System Reference Document/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=40|SRD 5.2.1, p. 40]]
