# Assassin's Blood

## Dungeon Master's Guide

> [!info]
> ### Assassin's Blood [[Assassin's Blood|(150 GP)]]
> *[[Ingested|Ingested]] Poison*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> A creature subjected to Assassin’s Blood makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 6 (1d12) Poison damage and has the [[Poisoned|Poisoned]] condition for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Dungeon Master's Guide.pdf|Dungeon Master's Guide]]
