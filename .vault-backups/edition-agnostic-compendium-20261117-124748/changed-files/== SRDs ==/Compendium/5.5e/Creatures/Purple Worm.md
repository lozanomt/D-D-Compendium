# Purple Worm

## Monster Manual

> [!statblock]
> ### Purple Worm
>
> *Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned*
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/mm-p252-purple-worm.png|320]]
>
> **AC:** 18  
> **Initiative:** +3 (13)  
> **HP:** 247 (15d20 + 90)  
> **Speed:** 50 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Burrowing|Burrow]] 50 ft.  
>
> | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE | | | MOD | SAVE |
> |:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--|--:|--:|--:|
> | **Str** | 28 | +9 | +9 | **Dex** | 7 | -2 | -2 | **Con** | 22 | +6 | +11 |
> | **Int** | 1 | -5 | -5 | **Wis** | 8 | -1 | +4 | **Cha** | 4 | -3 | -3 |
>
> **Senses:** [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Blindsight|Blindsight]] 30 ft., [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Senses/Tremorsense|Tremorsense]] 60 ft.; [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Passive Perception|Passive Perception]] 9  
> **Languages:** None  
> **CR:** 15 (XP 13,000; PB +5)  
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Tunneler.*** The worm can burrow through solid rock at half its Burrow Speed and leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The worm makes one Bite attack and one Tail Stinger attack.
>
> ***Bite.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 22 (3d8 + 9) Piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] condition (escape DC 19), and it has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition until the grapple ends.
>
> ***Tail Stinger.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 16 (2d6 + 9) Piercing damage plus 35 (10d6) Poison damage.
>
> #### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Swallow.*** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 19, one Large or smaller creature [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Grappled|Grappled]] by the worm (it can have up to three creatures swallowed at a time). *Failure:* The target is swallowed by the worm, and the Grappled condition ends. A swallowed creature has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] and [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] conditions, has Total Cover against attacks and other effects outside the worm, and takes 17 (5d6) Acid damage at the start of each of the worm's turns.
>
> If the worm takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the worm must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, each of which falls in a space within 5 feet of the worm and has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] condition. If the worm dies, any swallowed creature no longer has the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]] condition and can escape from the corpse using 20 feet of movement, exiting Prone.
>
> **Source:** [[== SRDs ==/Sources/5.5e/Monster Manual/D&D 5.5E - Monster Manual.pdf|Monster Manual]]
