# Nature's Sanctuary

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Nature's Sanctuary
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p088-circle-of-the-land.png|320]]
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Magic|Magic]] action, you can expend a use of your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Wild Shape|Wild Shape]] and cause spectral trees and vines to appear in a 15-foot [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] on the ground within 120 feet of yourself. They last there for 1 minute or until you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition or die. You and your allies have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Half Cover|Half Cover]] while in that area, and your allies gain the current [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] of your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Druid/Circle of the Land/Nature's Ward|Nature's Ward]] while there. As a Bonus Action, you can move the Cube up to 60 feet to ground within 120 feet of yourself.
>

They last there for 1 minute or until you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition or die. You and your allies have Half [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Cover|Cover]] while in that area, and your allies gain the current Resistance of your Nature's Ward while there.
>
> As a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Bonus Action|Bonus Action]], you can move the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Areas of Effect/Cube|Cube]] up to 60 feet to ground within 120 feet of yourself.
