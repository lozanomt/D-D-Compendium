> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Rage|Rage]]
> You can imbue yourself with a primal power called Rage, a force that grants you extraordinary might and resilience. You can enter it as a Bonus Action if you aren’t wearing Heavy armor.
>
> You can enter your Rage the number of times shown for your Barbarian level in the Rages column of the Barbarian Features table. You regain one expended use when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> While active, your Rage follows the rules below.
>
> **Damage:** Resistance.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Resistance|Resistance]] to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.  
>
> ***Rage Damage.*** When you make an attack using Strength - with either a weapon or an [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] - and deal damage to the target, you gain a bonus to the damage that increases as you gain levels as a Barbarian, as shown in the Rage Damage column of the Barbarian Features table.
>
> **Strength:** Advantage.*** You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.  
>
> ***No Concentration or Spells.*** You can’t maintain [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Concentration|Concentration]], and you can’t cast spells.
>
> ***Duration.*** The Rage lasts until the end of your next turn, and it ends early if you don Heavy armor or have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition. If your Rage is still active on your next turn, you can extend the Rage for another round by doing one of the following:
> - Make an attack roll against an enemy.
> - Force an enemy to make a saving throw.
> - Take a Bonus Action to extend your Rage.
>
> Each time the Rage is extended, it lasts until the end of your next turn. You can maintain a Rage for up to 10 minutes.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Unarmored Defense|Unarmored Defense]]
> While you aren’t wearing any armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.
> #### Level 1: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Weapon Mastery|Weapon Mastery]]
> Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of Simple or Martial Melee weapons of your choice, such as Greataxes and Handaxes. Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], you can practice weapon drills and change one of those weapon choices.
>
> When you reach certain Barbarian levels, you gain the ability to use the mastery properties of more kinds of weapons, as shown in the Weapon Mastery column of the Barbarian Features table.
> #### Level 2: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Danger Sense|Danger Sense]]
> You gain an uncanny sense of when things aren’t as they should be, giving you an edge when you dodge perils. You have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity saving throws unless you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition.
> #### Level 2: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Reckless Attack|Reckless Attack]]
> You can throw aside all concern for defense to attack with increased ferocity. When you make your first attack roll on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. Doing so gives you [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls using Strength until the start of your next turn, but attack rolls against you have Advantage during that time.
> #### Level 3: Barbarian Subclass
> You gain a Barbarian subclass of your choice. A subclass is a specialization that grants you features at certain Barbarian levels. For the rest of your career, you gain each of your subclass’s features that are of your Barbarian level or lower. The following subclasses are available for you to choose from:
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Path of the Berserker/Path of the Berserker|Path of the Berserker]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Path of the Wild Heart/Path of the Wild Heart|Path of the Wild Heart]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Path of the World Tree/Path of the World Tree|Path of the World Tree]]
> - [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Path of the Zealot/Path of the Zealot|Path of the Zealot]]
> #### Level 3: Primal Knowledge
> You gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the skill list available to Barbarians at level 1.
>
> In addition, while your Rage is active, you can channel primal power when you attempt certain tasks; whenever you make an ability check using one of the following skills, you can make it as a Strength check even if it normally uses a different ability: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Acrobatics]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Intimidation|Intimidation]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Perception|Perception]], [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Stealth|Stealth]], or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Skills/Survival|Survival]]. When you use this ability, your Strength represents primal power coursing through you, honing your agility, bearing, and senses.
> #### Level 4: Ability Score Improvement
> You gain the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Feats/02) General Feats/Ability Score Improvement|Ability Score Improvement]] feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Barbarian levels 8, 12, and 16.
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Any Class Features/Extra Attack|Extra Attack]]
> You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Attack|Attack]] action on your turn.
> #### Level 5: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Fast Movement|Fast Movement]]
> Your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] increases by 10 feet while you aren’t wearing Heavy armor.
> #### Level 7: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Feral Instinct|Feral Instinct]]
> Your instincts are so honed that you have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]] rolls.
> #### Level 7: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Instinctive Pounce|Instinctive Pounce]]
> As part of the Bonus Action you take to enter your Rage, you can move up to half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]].
> #### Level 9: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Brutal Strike|Brutal Strike]]
> If you use Reckless Attack, you can forgo any [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Advantage|Advantage]] on one Strength-based attack roll of your choice on your turn. The chosen attack roll mustn’t have [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]. If the chosen attack roll hits, the target takes an extra 1d10 damage of the same type dealt by the weapon or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]], and you can cause one Brutal Strike effect of your choice. You have the following effect options.
>
> ***Forceful Blow.*** The target is pushed 15 feet straight away from you. You can then move up to half your [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Speed|Speed]] straight toward the target without provoking [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]].
>
> ***Hamstring Blow.*** The target’s Speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of your next turn. A target can be affected by only one Hamstring Blow at a time - the most recent one.
> #### Level 11: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Relentless Rage|Relentless Rage]]
> Your Rage can keep you fighting despite grievous wounds. If you drop to 0 [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Hit Points|Hit Points]] while your Rage is active and don’t die outright, you can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. If you succeed, your Hit Points instead change to a number equal to twice your Barbarian level.
>
> Each time you use this feature after the first, the DC increases by 5. When you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short]] or [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]], the DC resets to 10.
> #### Level 13: Improved Brutal Strike
> You have honed new ways to attack furiously. The following effects are now among your Brutal Strike options.
>
> ***Staggering Blow.*** The target has [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the next saving throw it makes, and it can’t make [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Actions/Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] until the start of your next turn.
>
> ***Sundering Blow.*** Before the start of your next turn, the next attack roll made by another creature against the target gains a +5 bonus to the roll. An attack roll can gain only one Sundering Blow bonus.
> #### Level 15: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Persistent Rage|Persistent Rage]]
> When you roll [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Initiative|Initiative]], you can regain all expended uses of Rage. After you regain uses of Rage in this way, you can’t do so again until you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Long Rest|Long Rest]].
>
> In addition, your Rage is so fierce that it now lasts for 10 minutes without you needing to do anything to extend it from round to round. Your Rage ends early if you have the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|Unconscious]] condition (not just the [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Conditions/Incapacitated|Incapacitated]] condition) or don Heavy armor.
> #### Level 17: Improved Brutal Strike
> The extra damage of your Brutal Strike increases to 2d10. In addition, you can use two different Brutal Strike effects whenever you use your Brutal Strike feature.
> #### Level 18: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Indomitable Might|Indomitable Might]]
> If your total for a Strength check or Strength saving throw is less than your Strength score, you can use that score in place of the total.
> #### Level 19: Epic Boon
> You gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify.
> #### Level 20: [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Character Options/Classes/Barbarian/Primal Champion|Primal Champion]]
> You embody primal power. Your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 4, to a maximum of 25.
>
