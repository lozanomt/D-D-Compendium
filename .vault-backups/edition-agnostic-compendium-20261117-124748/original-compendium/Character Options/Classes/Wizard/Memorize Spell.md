# Memorize Spell

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Memorize Spell
>
> ![[== SRDs ==/Attachments/Artwork/phb-p167-wizard.png|320]]
>
> Whenever you finish a [[== SRDs ==/Compendium/5.5e/Rules/Common Terms/Short Rest|Short Rest]], you can study your spellbook and replace one of the level 1+ Wizard spells you have prepared for your Spellcasting feature with another level 1+ spell from the book.
>
