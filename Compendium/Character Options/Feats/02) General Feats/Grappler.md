# Grappler

## Player's Handbook

> [!info]
> ### Grappler
> *General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*
>
> ![[Placeholder.png|320]]
>
> You gain the following benefits.
>
> ***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
>
> ***Punch and Grab.*** When you hit a creature with an [[Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] as part of the [[Attack|Attack]] action on your turn, you can use both the Damage and the Grapple option. You can use this benefit only once per turn.
>
> ***Attack Advantage.*** You have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on attack rolls against a creature [[Grappled|Grappled]] by you.
>
> ***Fast Wrestler.*** You don't have to spend extra movement to move a creature Grappled by you if the creature is your size or smaller.
>
> **Source:** [[D&D 5.5E - Player's Handbook.pdf|Player's Handbook]]
